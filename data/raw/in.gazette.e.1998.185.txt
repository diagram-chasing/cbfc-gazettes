MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING 
PUBLIC NOTICE NO. 303/12/98-F (F) 

New Delhi, the 18th August, 1998 

No. 303/12/98-F(F).—-Reference is invited to this Ministry’s Public Notice No. 303/11/87-F (F) dated 31-7-87 notify¬ 
ing the Indian Panorma Regulations and subsequent amendments/modifications notified yjds Ministry’s Public Notices No. 
303/12/98-F (F) dated 15-7-88 and 24-2-89, No. 303/2l/89-F(F) dated 20-7-89, No. 303/25/90-F(F) dated 7-8-90, No. 303/9/ 
91-F(F) dated 20-6-91, No. 303/12/93-F(F) dated 17-8-93, No, 303/39/94-F(F) dated 3-7-95,8-8-95 and 19-2-96 and 303/4/97- 
F(F) dated 12-6-97. 

The aforesaid Regulations are further amended as follows :— 

1. For the existing regulations 7(1), (2) & (3), the following shall be substituted; namely:— 

“7(1) A film in any Indian language and certified by the Central Board of Film Certification between 1st September of the 
immediately preceding year and 31 st August of the year in which Indian Panorama films are being selected is eligible 
for entry. In case 31st August is a holiday, the next official working date shall be considered as the last date.”

“ 7 (2) A film in 16 mm, 35mm or in a wider gauge made in any Indian language and certified by the Central Board of 
Film Certification as a feature film/featurette irrespective of its length is eligible for feature film category. The 
question whether a film is a feature film or non-feature film shall be decided by the Feature film Jury and its 
decision shall be final. Therefore, doubtful cases shall be referred to the Feature Film Jury for a decision." 

“7(3) For the non-feature films section, only those films that are certified by the Central Board of Film Certification as 
Documentary/Newsreel/non-fiction/short fiction irrespective of their length are eligible." 

2. For the existing Regulations 8(1), (2), (4), 8(5) (a) and 8 (6) the following shall be substituted; namely :— 

“8(1) A producer and/or right holder desirous of entering his/her feature/non-feature film in the Indian Panorama shall 
apply to the Director, Directorate of Film Festivals, (IP Cell), Ministry of Information & Broadcasting, 4tb 
floor, Lok Nayak Bhavan, Khan Market, New Delhi-110003, Tele Nos. 4692849, 4620613, 4615953 Fax 
No. 0-11-4623430 on the prescribed entry form (IP-I) alongwith forms IP-II, LP-III (a), IP-IU (b), EP-III(c) and IP- 
m (d) and certificate issued by the Central Board of Film Certification.” 

“8(2) Entries for feature and non-feature films must be sent alongwith a non-refundable entry fee of Rs. 2000 for feature 
film s and Rs. 500 for non-feature category in the form of a Crossed Demand Draft in favour of the “Pay & 
Accounts Officer (Main Secretariat), Ministry of Information & Broadcasting” payable at the State Bank of India, 
New Delhi.” 

“8(4) The producer shall submit the print to the Incharge Print Unit, Directorate of Film Festivals (IP Cell), Ministry of 
Information & Broadcasting, Siri Fort Cultural Complex, Asian Games Village, August Kranti Marg, New Delhi- 
110049, Telefax : 0-11-6493089 so as to reach on or before the 15th September. In case 15th September is a 
holiday, the next working day will be treated as the last date for receipt of prints. The freight cost of the print and 
the material for sending it to the Directorate shall be borne by the producer.” 

“8(5)(a) Thirty copies Of the detailed synopsis in English comprising of maximum 3 to 4 pages of standard A-4 size on 
bond paper.” 

“8(6) In case of outstation entry, the print may be preferably airfreighted to the Incharge, Print Unit, Directorate of 
Fi lm Festivals (IP Cell), Ministry of Information & Broadcasting, Siri Fort Cultural Complex, Asian Games Vil¬ 
lage, August Kranti Marg, New Delhi-110 049, Telefax : 0-11-6493089 with a separate intimation regarding 
consignment number and other details.” 

3. In the Entry Form HU, for the existing SI. No. 24 and 26, the following shall be substituted; namely :— 

“24 “Print of the film to be returned to : 

(Please give Name, Telephone, Fax No. & Complete Address)” 

“26 D. D. No., Date and amount 

4. In the last page of Entry Form IP-I indicating the particulars of enclosures, under the heading ‘To be filled by the 

Applicant’; 

(a) for the existing S. Nos. 1 and 2, the following shall be substituted; namely 
“1 D. D. No., Date & Amount” 


"2 Thirty copies of the synopsis in English (one more copy to be sent with the print)". 

(b) after SI. No. 8 the following S. No. shall be inserted, namely:— 

“9. An affidavit on a stamped paper.” 

5. In Form I. P. -II, I. P.-III(a), I. P, 111(b), I. P.-III(c) and I. P.-IIl(d), the word “seal” shall be added below the signatures of 
Right Holder(s) and Producer(s).
