G.S.R. 173(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by section 8 of the Cinematograph Act, 1952 (37 of 
1952) read with sub-rule (9) of rule 22 and sub-rule (9) of rule 24 of the Cinematograph (Certification) Rules, 1983, 
the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Cinematograph (Certification) Rules, 
1983, namely;— 

1. Short title and commencement.—(1) These rules may be called the Cinematograph (Certification) (Amend¬ 
ment) Rules, 1997. 

(2) They shall come into force with effect from the 1st May 1997. 

2. In the Cinematograph (Certification) Rules, 1983, in the Second Schedule, for From VIII, the following shall
