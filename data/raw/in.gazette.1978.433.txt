CANTONMENT BOARD, NASIRABAD CANTONMENT 

Nnsirabad Cantonment, the 28th July 1978 

No. S.R.O. NCB/RB/1/4,— Whereas a draft of the Nasira- 
bad Cantonment (Control of Loud Speakers) Bye-Laws 1977 
was published with the Cantonment Board Public Notice No. 
639, dated the 26th July, 1977 ns required by Sub-Section (1) 
of Section 284 of the Cantonments Act, 1924 (2 of 1924) for 
inviting objections or suggestions from the public within a 
period of thirty days from the date of publication of the said 
public Notice; 

And whereas the said public Notice was put up on the 
Cantonment Notice Board on the 26th July, 1977; 

And whereas only one objection was received and considered 
by the Board; 

And whereas the Central Government have duly approved 
and confirmed the said draft of the bye-laws as required by 
sub-section (1) of section 284 of the said Act; 

Now r therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by 
clauses (28) and (38) of section 282, and section 283, of the 
said Act and in supersession of the Nasirabad Cantonment 
(Control of Loud Speakers) Bye-Laws, 1965, the Cantonment 
Board Nasirabad, hereby makes the following bye-laws; pub- 
lished with the notification of the Defence Department No, 
103, dated the 3rd April, 1965. 

1. Short title and commencement — 

(1) These bye-laws may bo called the Nasirabad Canton- 
ment (Control of Loud Speakers) Rye-Lnws, 1978. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date ofj their 
publication in the Official Gazette. 

2. Permission to use Loud Speaker on certain conditions — 

n?o person shall make use of any Loud Speaker within the 
limit of Nasirabad Cantonment except with the previous per- 
mission in writing of the Cantonment Executive Officer and 
on the following conditions, namely : — 

(1) that the application for grant of permission to the use 
of loud speaker for marriage or for any other occasion shall 
be made to the Cantonment Executive Officer on any working 
day during office hours on a Form specified in the schedule 
annexed to these bye-laws at least twenty four hours before 
the time the loud speaker is required to be played and the 
form shall be obtained from the Cantonment Executive Officer 
free of cost; 

(2) that the Cantonment Executive Officer may, having 
regard to public welfare and convenience, either refuse or 
grant permission for the use of the loud speaker for such 
period as may be specified by him in this behalf; 

(3) that an amplifier possessing more than 25 watts capacity 
shall ordinarily not be used; 

(4) that a loud speaker of 10 watts fitted in a wooden box 
oi t in a ground metal container shall be used; 

(5) that the volume of loud speaker shall not exceed the 
second stage of the regulator of the amplifier; 

(6) that the loud speaker shall not be fitted more than 
eight feet high from the ground level and in no case shall it 
be positioned on the top of a building; 

(7) that the loud speaker shall face towards the premises 
or place and the noise shall be restricted to the audience only; 

(8) that in the use of the loud speaker there shall be no 
hindrance to the traffic or annoyance to the public. 

Provided that no such permission shall be necessary for the 
use of loud speaker for the announcement of any notice or com- 
munique issued by the Central Government, the State Govern- 
ment, the Deputy Commissioner or the District Magistrate of 
the District, Superintendent of Police of the District or the 
Cantonment Executive Officer. 

Explanation In this bye-law, ‘Loud Speaker" includes 
any electrically or mechanically operated 
means of producing loud noise. 

3. Dealer to ascertain the permission first : 

No dealer of loud speaker shall supply loud speaker on hire 
without first ascertaining the written permission of the Canton- 
ment Executive Officer from the person hiring the loud speaker. 

4. Permission to be withdrawn in the interests of Public — 

Any permission given on conditions imposed under these 
bye-laws may be withdrawn or varied by the Cantonment 
Executive Officer, where such withdrawal or variation appears 
necessary in the interests of the public.

5. Appeal against orders of the Cantonment Executive Officer : 

Any persorn aggrieved by an order of the Cantonment Exe- 
cutive Officer made under these bye-laws may appeal to the 
Board within ten days from the date of communication of 
such order ; 

Provided that if the Board is satisfied that a copy of the 
order was received by the appellant after the expiry of the said 
period of ten days, it may entertain the appeal within a period 
of ten days from the date of receipt of the order, but not 
thereafter, 

6, Penalty for contravention of any bye-law : 

Contravention of any of these bye-laws shall be punishable/ 
with fine which may extend "to one hundred rupees and in the 
case of a continuing contravention, with an additional fine 
which may extend to five rupees for every day during such 
contravention continues after conviction for the first such 
contravention.
