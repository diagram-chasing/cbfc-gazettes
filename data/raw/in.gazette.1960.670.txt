THE INSTITUTE OF COST AND WORKS ACCOUNT- 
ANTS OF INDIA 

(Cost Accountants) 

NOTIFICATION 

Calcutta 16, the 2nd November 1960 

No. 39 C WA(3) / 60 — In exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 39 of the Cost and Works 
Accountants Act, 1959 (Act No. 23 of 1959), the Council 
of the Institute of Cost and Works Accountants of India 
has made the following amendments in the Cost and 
Works Accountants Regulations, 1959, the same having 
been previously published and approved by the Central 
Government as required by sub-section (3) of the said sec- 
tion. 

In the said Regulations: 

I. In Regulation 17, after the words “under clause (c) of 
sub-section (1) of section 20” and before the words “all 
arrears on account of the annual fee”, the following words 

shall be inserted, namely: 

“or under Article 20(c) of the Articles of Association 
of the dissolved company”. 

II. In sub-regulation (1) of Regulation 32, for the figures 
and word “30th June 1961”, the following figures and 
word shall be substituted, namely: 

“31st December 1961”. 

III. In sub-regulation (1) of Regulation 32, under the 
head “Group II” in sub-head “(b) Costing” for the 
brackets and words “(Four papers of three hours and 100 
marks each)” the following shall be substituted, namely: 

"(Four papers of two hours and 100 marks each)”. 

IV. In Regulation 32, in sub-regulations (3), (4) and (5) 
for the words “Economics of Industry and Commerce and 
Industrial Revolution in India”, wherever they occur, the 
following words shall be substituted, namely: 

“Economics of Industry and Commerce and Industrial 
Evolution in India”. 


V. For the existing sub-regulation (5) of Regulation 32, 
the following sub-regulation shall be substituted, namely : 

“(5) A candidate re-appearing under the second pro- 
viso to Regulation 29 for the Intermediate 
Examination prescribed in sub-regulation (3), or 
a candidate who has passed in or obtained exemp- 
tion from Group I or Group II of the Intermediate 
Examination prescribed in sub-regulation (1) shall 
be entitled to exemption — 

(a) if he had passed the previous examination as 
a whole, from all subjects of the examination 
except Office Methods and General Knowledge 
of Commerce and Finance, and Industrial and 
Commercial Law (excluding Company Law); 

(b) if he had passed in or obtained exemption 
from Group I of the previous examination, 
from the subjects Economics of Industry and 
Commerce and Industrial Evolution in India, 
Book-keeping and Accountancy and Practical 
Mathematics; and 

(c) if he had passed in or obtained exemption 
from Group II of the previous examination, 
from the subjects Elements of Engineering 
and Costing: 

Provided that no exemptions under this sub- 
regulation shall be admissible after Decem- 
ber 1962”. 

VI. In sub-regulation (1) of Regulation 35, under the 
head “Group II”, in sub-head “(b) Costing”, for the 
brackets and words “(Four papers of three hours and 100 
marks each)”, the following shall be substituted, namely: 

“(Four Papers of two hours and 100 marks each)”. 

VII. In Regulation 35, after sub-regulation (4), the fol- 
lowing sub-regulation (5) shall be added, namely: 

“(5) A candidate shall be entitled to exemption — 

(a) from Statistical Methods, if he has passed in 
or obtained exemption from Group I of the 
Final Examination prescribed in sub-regula- 
tion (1); or 

(b) from the papers on Costing, if he has passed 
in or obtained exemption from Group II of 
the Final Examination prescribed in sub- 
regulation (1). 

Provided that no exemptions under this sub- 
regulation shall be admissible after Decem- 
ber 1962.” 

VIII. For the existing sub-regulation (1) of Regulation 
46, the following sub-regulation (1) shall be substituted, 
namely : 

“(1) No candidate shall be admitted to the Manage- 
ment Accountancy Examination unless a minimum 
period of one year has elapsed between the date 
of his enrolment as a member of the Institute or 
of the dissolved company and the first day of the 
month of the examination”. 

IX. In Regulation 124, for the word “Secretary”, 
wherever it occurs, including in the heading thereof, the 
following words shall be substituted, namely: 

“Secretary of the Regional Council”.

No. l-CA(4)/60— In exercise of the powers conferred by 
sub-section (1) of Section 30 of the Chartered Account- 
ants Act, 1949 (XXXVIII of 1949), the Council of the Insti- 
tute of Chartered Accountants of India has made the follow- 
ing amendments in the Chartered Accountants Regula- 
tions, 1949, the same having been previously published and 
approved by the Central Government as required under 
sub-section (3) of the said Section. 

In the said Regulations: — 

I. In clause (g) of sub regulation (1) of Regulation 62-F, 
substitute the word “exempting any Chartered Account- 
ant in pratice or a firm of such Chartered Accountants 
from the operation of sub-section (1) of Section 27 of the 
Act for a period not exceeding six months, provided how- 
ever that all cases of infringement of the provisions of that 
sub-section, of a non-technical nature, shall be brought to 
the notice of the Council”, by the word “placing before 
the Council cases of infringement of a technical nature 
exceeding six months and all cases of infringement of non- 
technical nature, of the provisions of sub-section (1) of 
Section 27 of the Act”. 

II. In paragraph 2 of Regulation 87, after the words 
“owing to death” and before the words “and restoration 
to membership” add the words “or non-payment of any 
prescribed fees”. 

III. In Regulation 87, after the existing paragraph 4, 
add the following paragraph: — 

“4A. Exempting any chartered accountant in pratice 
or a firm of such chartered accountants from the 
operation of sub-section (1) of Section 27 of the 
Act for a period not exceeding six months, where 
the infringement is of a technical nature”. 

IV. In Regulation 87, after the existing paragraph 17, 
add the following:— 

17A. Condoning any break in the service of articled 
and audit clerks upto a maximum period of six 
months at his discretion”. 

V. In paragraph 18(b) of Regulation 87, delete the words 
“subject to the approval of the President".

REGIONAL COUNCILS

82-H(l)(i) Regional Councils may be formed for one or

tion 9. 

I. The State of Bombay. 

II. The State of Madras, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala and 
Mysore. 

III. The States of West Bengal. Assam. Orissa and the 

Union Territories of Manipur and Tripura. 

IV. The States of Uttar Pradesh, Bihar. Madhya Pra- 

desh and Rajasthan. 

V. The Union Territories of Delhi and Himachal Pra- 
desh and the State of Punjab. 

(ii) The Regional Councils shall at all times function 
subject to the control, supervision and direction of the 
Council of the Institute (hereinafter referred to as 
“Central Council") and/or any of its Committees. 

(2) The Regional Councils set up for the above Regional 
Constituencies shall be known by the names of Western 
India Regional Council. Southern India Regional Council. 
Eastern India Regional Council, Central India Regional 
Council and Northern India Regional Council respective-

(3) (i) The Western India Regional Council shall extend 
to the State of Bombay or the constituency as may from

of sub-section (2) of Section 9 of the Act. which includes 
the State of Madras. 

(iii) The Eastern India Regional Council shall extend 
to the States of West Bengal, Assam, Orissa and the Union 
Territories of Manipur and Tripura, or the constituency

and Rajasthan, or the constituency as may from time to 
time be specified by the Central Government clause (a) 
of sub-section (2) of Section 9 of the Act, which includes 
the State of Uttar Pradesh.

to the Union Territories of Delhi and Himachal Pradesh 
and the State of Punjab, or the constituency as may from

under clause (a) of sub-section (2) of Section 9 of the Act, 
which includes the Union Territory of Delhi. 

(vi) The areas covered by these constituencies shall for 
brevity be called Western Region, Southern Region, 
Eastern Region, Central and Northern Region respective- 
ly.

4. The duties and functions of the Regional Council 
shall be: — 

(i) To provide facilities for intercourse among members 
of Idle Institute in the respective region by regular meet- 
ings, arrangement of talks and lectures and for the 
acquisition and dissemination -of useful information in 
connection with the profession of Accountancy, including 
the award of prizes, etc. in suitable cases.

G — Office-Bearers and Committees 

(17) Each Regional Council shall, at its meeting held in 
the latter half of the month of September every year, as 
provided in sub-regulation (35) (i) below, shall eledt such 
of its members to b« respectively the Chairman, the 
Vice-Chairman, the Secretary and the Treasurer thereof 
and so often, as any of these offices becomes vacant, the 
Regional Council shall choose another person from among 
themselves to hold that office. If within half an hour from 
the time appointed for such a meeting, a quorum as 
mentioned in sub-regulation (38) of this Regulation is not 
present, the said meetmg shall, notwithstanding anything 
contained in sub-regulation (38), stand adjourned to the 
same day in the next week at the same time and place 
and at such adjourned meeting of the Regional Council, 
the members present, whatever their number, shall form 
the quorum and shall have power to transact all the 
business, which could properly have been transacted by 
the original meeting, had the necessary quorum been 
present. 

Provided further that on the dissolution of a Regional 
Council, the Chairman, Secretary and Treasurer for the 
time being, shall discharge the duties and functions of the 
Chairman, Secretary and Treasurer respectively as pro- 
vided in 'this Regulation until such time as the Chairman, 
Secretary and Treasurer of the next Regional Council are 
elected under the provisions of this Regulation a n d take 
over charge of their respective duties. 

(18) The Chairman of the Regional Council shall be the 
Chief Executive Authority of the Regional Council. 

(19) The Secretary shall be responsible for the per- 
formance of general duties of the office of the Regional 
Council under the guidance of the Chairman or in his 
absence the Vice-Chairman. 

(20) The Treasurer shall cause true accounts to be 
maintained of the assets and liabilities and also of the 
moneys received and expended and shall deal with 
matters in respect of which such receipts and expenditure 
take place. 

(21) The said office-bearers shall hold office until the 
meeting of the Regional Council in the next year to be 
held in the latter half of September, as provided in sub- 
regulation (35) (i) below. 

(22) The retiring o#lce-bearers will be eligible for re- 
election to any of the offices of the Regional Council if 
they continue to be members in the constituency con- 
cerned.' 

(23) The Regional Council at a meeting shall constitute 
such committees as the Central Council may direct and 
may also form such other committees as it deems neces- 
sary for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this 
Regulation. 

(24) Each of the committees shall consist of the Chair- 
man or the Vice-Chairman of the Regional Council ex- 
officio and such other members of the Regional Council 
as may be elected by that Council: 

Provided that there shall be at least three members 
including the Chairman or the Vice-Chairman in 
each of the Committees : 

Provided further that in the case of any committee 
constituted at the direction of the Central Council, 
out of the members to be elected, one shall be 
nominated by the Central Council from amongst 
the members of the Central Council from the 
Region concerned. 

(25) The Regional Council shall elect any member of 
the committee to be the Chairman of that Committee: 

Provided that in the case of any committee consti- 
tuted at the direction of the Central Council, 
unless otherwise nominated by the Central 
Council the Chairman or the Vice-Chairman of 
the Regional Council, as the case may be, shall 
be the Chairman of that Committee. 

(26) Every member of a committee shall hold office until 
the meeting of the Regional Council in the. next year to 
be held in the latter half of September, as provided in sub- 
regulation 35 (i), below. But subject to being a member of 
the Regional Council, be shall be eligible for re-election. 

(27) The rules regarding the meetings of the Regional 
Councils prescribed in sub-regulations (35) to (40) of this 
Regulation will, as far as possible, be applicable to the 
meetings of the committees of the Regional Councils. 


H — Finance and Accounts 

(28) Each Regional Council shall be self-supporting and 
shall not borrow or obtain credit without the prior sanc- 
tion of the Central Council. It may levy such further 
fees as it may consider necessary for members participat- 
ing in specific activities. It may also collect voluntary 
contributions for any specific activity with the prior 
sanction of the Central Council. 

(29) (i) The funds of the Regional Council shall be kept 
in one of the scheduled banks approved by the Central 
Council in this behalf. 

(ii) The funds of the Regional Council shall be em- 
ployed for such purposes as may from time to time be 
sanctioned by the Regional Council: 

Provided that no funds thereof shall be applied either 
directly or indirectly for payment to the members 
of the Regional Council except for reimbursing 
them for any expenses incurred by them in con- 
nection with the business of the Regional Council 
in the region concerned. 

(30i The accounts of the Regional Council, as main- 
tained by the Treasurer, shall be audited every year tw 
a Chartered Accountant in practice or a firm of such 
Chartered Accountants appointed by the Regional Consti- 
tuency at its Annual General Meeting and the accounts 
together with the audit report and the report of the 
Regional Council shall be sent to the members in the res- 
pective regions at least 14 days before the date of the 
Annual General Meeting of the Regional Constituency and 
shall be placed for adopting before the Annual General 
Meeting of the Constituency. The year will be from 1st 
April to 31st March of the next year. 

(31) A copy of the audited accounts and the report of 
the Regional Council, as adopted by the Annual General 
Meeting, shall be sent to the Central Council not later 
than one month after the date of the Annual General 
Meeting. The audited accounts of the various Regional 
Councils shall be annexed to the accounts of the Central 
Council for the relevant year, 

(32) The duty of carrying out the provisions of this 
Regulation is vested in the Regional Council and each of 
the Regional Councils is vested with necessary powers for 
carrying out the objects of this Regulation. 

(33) For this purpose the Regional Council may appoint 
such staff and servants for its office as may from time to 
time be decided, 

I — Property, Assets and Funds 

(34) All the property, assets and funds of the Regional 
Councils, shall at all times vest in the Central Council, 
but the Regional Councils shall administer them subject 
to the control, supervision and direction of the Central 
Council and/or any of its Committees. 

J — Meetings of the Regional Councils 

(35) (i) The Regional Council may meet as often as 
necessary for the conduct of its business. Provided that 
one meeting shall be held in the latter half of the month 
of September every year. 

(ii) Such business shall ordinarily be conducted at a 
meeting of that Council, provided that the Chairman, or 
in his absence the Vice-Chairman, may in any emergent 
case circulate papers among the members of the Regional 
Council for decision on any question but no decision on 
any question shall be taken by circulation of 1 papers, 
unless not less than two thirds of the members agree to 
decide such question by circulation of papers. In the event 
of such majority not agreeing, the Chairman or the Vice- 
Chairman, as 'the case may be, shall withdraw the papers 
from circulation and have the question decided at a meet- 
ing of the Regional Council. 

(iii) When the papers relating to any question are 
circulated for decision among the members, a period of 
not less than fifteen days, commencing from the date of 
such circulation shall elapse before any decision is taken 
on the question. 

(iv) Every decision taken by circulation of papers shall 
be noted by the Regional Council at its next meeting, 

(36) (i) The Secretary to the Regional Council shall 
issue notice at least 14 days before the date of the meet- 
ing in writing by post or otherwise to every member of 
the Regional Council. 

Provided that the said Secretary may issue notice at a 
shorter period if it is unanimously decided and agreed to 
by the members of the Regional Council in advance.

by the Chairman of the earlier meeting.

L — Duration of the Regional Council 

(53) (i) The duration of each Regional Council shall 
ordinarily be three years from the date of its constitution 
which shall be specified by the Central Council by a noti- 
fication in this behalf. 

Provided, however, that the Central Council may, if 
in its opinion circumstances so warrant, extend or shorten 
the life of any Regional Council by a notification in this 
behalf. 

(ii) On the expiry of the duration of a Regional Council, 
a new Regional Council to take office shall be constituted

M — Dissolution of the Regional Council 

(54) Notwithstanding anything contained in this Regu- 
lation, a Regional Council shall stand dissolved if: 

(i) a majority of three-fourths of the members on the 
Regional Register pass a resolution in a General 
Meeting of the members for the dissolution. 

(ii) after giving proper hearing to the Regional Coun- 
cil, the Central Council decides to dissolve the 
Regonal Council. 

N— Branches of Regional Councils 

(55) The Central Council may, by a notification in the 
Gazette of India, set up Branches of any or all Regional 
Councils in cities other than cities where Regional head- 
quarters are located and where not less than 50 members 
are residing within the limits of the city concerned or 
within a distance of 10 miles from the city limits, 

(56) The Branches shall at all times function subject to 
the control, supervision and direction of the Central 
Council through Regional Council concerned and shall be 
governed by such rules as may be specified from time to 
time by the Central Council by a notification in the 
Gazette of India. 

No. l-CA(15)/60 — In exercise of the powers conferred 
by clause (ii) of Part II of the Second Schedule to the 
Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, the Council of the 
Institute of Chartered Accountants of India is pleased to 
specify that a member of the Institute, whether in practice 
or not, who is employed by a Chartered Accountant in 
practice or by a firm of such Chartered Accountants, 
shall be deemed to be guilty of professional misconduct, 
if he is grossly negligent in the conduct of his duties.
