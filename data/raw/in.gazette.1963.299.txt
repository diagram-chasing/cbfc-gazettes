S.O, 1088. — The following draft of an Order which the Central Government 
proposes to make in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Essential 
Commodities Act, 1955 (10 of 1955) is hereby published for general information. 
Notice is hereby given that the said draft will be taken into consideration on or 
after 31st May 1963 and that any objection or suggestion with respect thereto 
received from any person before the said date will be considered by the Central 
Government. 


Draft Order 

1. Short title. — This Order may be called the Cold Storage Order, 1963. 

2. Definitions, — In this Order, unless th: context otherwise requires,— 

(a) “cold storage” means any refrigerated chamber or chambers which may 

be used for storing any food stuffs; 

(b) “Form” means a Form appended to this Order; 

(c) “licence” means a licence granted under clause 6; 

(d) “licensing Officer” means the Agricultural Marketing Adviser to the 

Government of India and includes any other officer empowered in 
this behalf by him with the approval of the Central Government; 

Ce) "Schedule” means the Schedule appended to this Order. 

3. Prohibition to store certain food stuffs In cold storage except under a 
licence. — No person shall carry on the business of storing any of the following 
foodstuffs in a cold storage except under and in accordance with the terms and 
conditions of a valid licence, namely: — 

(i) fruits, whether fresh, dry or dehydrated; 

(ii) vegetables and seed potatoes; 

(ill) fish, whether fresh, frozen or dried; 

(iv) meat, whether fresh, frozen or dried; 

(v) eggs, whether shelled or unshelled; 

(vi) milk and dairy products; and 
(vil) food products.

fin application in duplicate to' the licensing oificer in Form A together vith the 
Joes prescribed in clause 5 and a copy of the blue print of the cold storage bnildin* 

plan. 

5. Fees for licence. — -Th“ fees for a licence shall be—

(iii) Rupees two hundred and fifty— for storing foodstuffs in a cold storage 
with a capacity of 141-5 c.m. (5,000 c. ft.) and above. " 

6. Grant and refusal of licence. — (1) The licensing officer may, for reasons to be 
recorded, refuse to grant a licence to any applicant and shall furnish him with a 
copy of the order so passed. Where the application for a licence is refused the 
fees paid by the applicant along with the application shall be refunded to him. 

(2) Where an application for a licence is not refused under sub-clause (1) 
the licensing officer shall grant the applicant a licence in Form B. 

7. Period of validity of licence.— Every licence shall, unless previously 
cancelled or suspended, expire on the 31st day of December, next following. 

8. Renewal of licence. — (1) Every holder of a licence desiring to renew the 
licence shall before the date of expiry of the licence or within the grace period 
if any, prescribed in that regard by the licensing officer, make an application for 
renewal in duplicate to the licensing officer in Form C together with such fees 
as are payable under clause 5 for the original grant of the licence. 

(2) On receipt of such application together with such fees the licensing officer 
.may either renew the licence or refuse to renew the licence. Where the licens- 
ing officer refuses to renew ths licence he shall record the reasons for such refusal 
and shall furnish the applicant with a copy of the order so passed and shall refund 
the fees paid by the applicant for renewal of the licence. 

9. Condition of licence. — Every licence shall be subject to the conditions speci- 
fied in the Schedule. 

10. Power to call for Information, enter, etc. — (1) The licensing officer or any 
-other officer authorised by him in this behalf may with a view to s curing com- 
pliance with this Order, 

(a) require any holder of a licence to give any information in his posses- 

sion with respect to the foodstuffs stored by him In the cold storage; 

(b) enter upon and inspect any premises where such cold storage is instal- 

led or run, at all reasonable times, with a view to satisfying himself 
that the requirements of this Order are being complied with. 

(2) Every holder of a licence shall be bound to furnish the information so 
required to the licensing officer or such other officer and to afford all necessary 
facilities to the licensing officer or that other officer for the purpose of exercising 
hi? powers under paragraph (b) of sub-clause (1). 

11. Maintenance of records and submission of returns etc. — The licensing offi- 
cer may, by order in writing, direct any holder of a licence — 

(a) to maintain such books, accounts and records relating to his business of 

storing foodstuffs in a cold storage in such form as may be specified 
in the Order; 

(b) to submit to him such returns or statements in such form and contain- 

ing such information relating to that business and within such time 
as may be specified in the order. 

12. Power to cancel or suspend a licence. — The licensing officer may after giving 
the holder of a licence an opportunity of being heard, cancel or suspend the licence 
on any of the following grounds, namely: — 

(a) that the licence had been obtained by misrepresentation as to a material 
particular; or 

(h) that the holder of a licence has, without reasonable cause, failed to 
comply with the conditions subject to which the licence has been 
granted. 

13. Appeal,— Any person aggrieved by an order, — 

(a) refusing to grant or renew a licence, or 

(b) cancelling or suspending a licence,

Part 1 


Sanitary requirements — 

1. The premises where the cold storage is situated shall be clean and ade- 
quately lighted. It shall be cleaned, if so required by the Licensing Officer or 
any officer authorised by him, by lime washing or washing or painting or dis- 
infecting or de-adourising. 

2. The holder of the licence shall not store any product (s) which will be 
repugnant or alien to the smell of the foodstuff (s) stored in the cold storage. 
The namc(s) of the foodstuff (s) stored in a particular cold storage shall be 
exhibited prominently on a board fixed at the entrance of the cold storage. 

3. Adequate exhaust arrangement shall be provided to expel the stale air 
from the cold storage. 

4. All yards, out houses, stores, and all approaches of the premises where 
the cold storage is installed shall be kept clean and sanitary, 

5. No person suffering from any infectious or contagious disease shall be 
allowed to work in the cold storage. Arrangements shall be made for exami- 
nation of the employees working in the Cold Storage once a year to check that 
they arc free from any infectious, contagious, or other diseases and they shall 
be inoculated and vaccinated against the enteric group of diseases once a year 
and against small-pox once in two years and a certificate, thereof shall be kept 
for inspection. In case of any epidemic, all such employees shall be inoculated, 

6. The employees working in the cold storage shall be provided with proper 
clothes and boots to protect them from cold.

and suitable border gasket*. The design of the door(s) shall be such that a
