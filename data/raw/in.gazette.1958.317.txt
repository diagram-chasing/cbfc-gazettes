RESERVE BANK OF INDIA 
Department of Banking Development 

NOTIFICATION 

Bombay 1, the 22nd November 1958 

DBD. No. 2710/HB.5-58 — In pursuance of Section 42 of 
the State Bank of Hyderabad Act, 1956 (79 1956) the 

Reserve Bank of India, in consultation with the Board 
of Directors of the State Bank of Hyderabad and with 
the previous approval of the Central Government, hereby 
makes the following amendment to the State Bank of 
Hyderabad General Regulations, 1956; — 

For sub-regulation (1) of Regulation 6, substitute the 
following, namely: — 

"(1) Meetings of the Executive Committee shall ordi- 
narily be held at least once a month, sufficient 
notice being given to the Directors on the Execu- 
tive Committee to attend the meeting.” 

B. VENKATAPPIAH 
Deputy Governor
