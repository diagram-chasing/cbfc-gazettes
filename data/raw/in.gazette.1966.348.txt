4. Meetings of the Corporation and Executive Committee.—

8. Decision by Majority. — All questions at a meeting of 
the Corporation or the Executive Committee shall be decided 
by a majority of voles. In case of equality of volcs, the 
member presiding shall have a second or casting vote. 

9. Quorum. — 8 members shall form a quorum for a meet- 
ing of the Corporation, while 3 members shall form a quorum 
for a meeting of the Executive Committee. If there is no

Committee, as tire case may be, the meeting shall be 
adjourned, At the adjourned meeting, the business of the 
last meeting shall be conducted irrespective of whether there 
is quorum or not. 

10. Place of Meeting. — The meeting of tire Corporation 
and the Executive Committee shall be held at New Delhi or 
at any other place in India, as may be decided by the Chair- 
man of the Corporation or the Executive Committee as the 
case may be.

maintained in which typed proceedings of tho meetings of the 
Corporation shall be kept, A similar record shall be main-

Corporation and the proceedings of the meeting of the Exe-

Executive Committee, as the case may be. for confirmation.
