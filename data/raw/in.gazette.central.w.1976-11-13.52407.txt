REGISTERED STUDENTS 

During the year, 16 ,006 persons got admitted as Registered 
Students , as against 13 , 564 in the previous year. The total 
number of Registered Students , whose names are borne in the 
Students Register has exceeded 65 , 000 . 

The Council noted that quite a substantial number of those 
who take admission as Registered Students , discontinue their 
studies after making a fow attempts at examinations , although 
their names are still borne in the Register till they completo 
seven years of Registration . Thus, the effective number of 
Registered Students is estimated to be around 30 , 000 .
