MINISTRY OF INFORMATION & BROADCASTING 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 7th December, 1978 

SO, 703(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (c) of sub¬ 
section (2) of Section 6 of the Cinematograph Act, 1952 (37 of 1952), the 
Central Government hereby directs that the exhibition of the film entitled 

“The Exorcist (Revised) (English) which has been granted an certificate 

No. 4080 dated the 27th August, 1977 and film entitled “Jadu* Tona” (Hindi) 
which has been granted an 'A* certificate No. 3363 dated the 19tb November, 
1977 by the Central Board of Film Censors, be suspended for a period of two 
months with effect from the date of issue of this Notification.
