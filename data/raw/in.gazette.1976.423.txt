No, 6-76 /G.R. — The Central Government (Ministry of Edu-

ing Regulations for Certificate Course in Id bra 17 Science 
(through Correspondence) : — 

1. L The duration of the course of instruction for the Certi- 
ficate in Library Science shall be one academic year. 

1.2. The examination shall be held once a year ordinarily 
in the month of April, on such date as may be fixed by the 
Syndicate. 

1.3. The dale of examination and the last date for receipt 
of admission forms without and with late fee of Rs. 5/- as 
fixed by the Syndicate, shall be notified by the Registrar. 

2 . A person who has passed one of the following examina- 
tions shall be eligible to join the course 

(i) Higher Secondary Examination of the Panjab Uni- 
versity in any group in second division. 

(ii) Pie- University examination of the Panjab University 
in any group in second division, 

(iii) An examination of another University /Board of 
School Education recognised by the Syndicate as 
equivalent to (i) or (ii) above. 

3. A person desirous of receiving instruction in this course 
shall get himself .'enrolled with the Directorate of Correspond- 
ence Courses and shall pay the following fees : — 

(a) Enrolment Fee — Rs. 5/-. 

(b) Tuition fee— Rs. 300/- (Payable in two instalments 
viz. Rs. 150/- alongwith Enrolment form and Rs. 
150/- up to Dec. 10, with late fee of Rs. 25 the 
second iastalment may be accepted up to Dec, 31). 

(c) Practical training fee— Rs. 50/- (payable alongwith 
1st instalment of tuition fee). 

4. A student who fails to appear or having appeared fails
