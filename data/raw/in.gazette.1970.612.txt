AWARD 

1. The Central Government, being of opinion that an industrial dispute exists 
between the employers in relation to the management of Pure Kustore Colliery 
of Messrs Pure Kustore Collieries Company Limited, Post Offlce-Kustore, District 
Dhanbad and their workmen, by Its order No. 2/235/68-LRII dated the ISth of 
April, 1969 referred to this Tribunal under section 10(l)(d) of the Industrial Dis- 
putes Act, 1947 for adjudication the dispute in respect to the matters specified in 
the schedule annexed thereto. The Schedule is extracted below: — 

Schedule 

"Whether the management of Pure Kustore Colliery of M/s. Pure Kustore 
Collieries Company Limited, Post Office Kusunda, District Dhanbad, 
was justified in refusing employment to Sarvasbrl Pathl Bhar, Kuber 
Jadav, Miners and Jagdish Turi, Winding Engine Khalasi, with effect 
from the 1st January, 1968? If not, to what relief are the workmen 
entitled?” 

2. Employers filed their written statement on 29th July, 1969 and the workmen 
filed their written statement on 10th September, 1969. But It is unnecessary to 
state the respective cases of the parties because the dispute has been amicably- 
settled. 

Parties have filed a joint petition of compromise. According to the terms of 
compromise the concerned workmen S/Sri Pathi Bhar & Kuber Jadav will bo 
reinstated in their original post of miners, Sri Jagdish Turi will be reinstated as 
a Fan Attendant and all the three workmen mentioned above will join their res- 
pective posts as stated above within 7 days from the date or this settlement falling 
which they will have no claim of reinstatement. The management of Pure Kustore 
Colliery has agreed lo pay a lump sum amount of Rs. 200 (Rupees Two hundred) 
only to each of the three concerned workmen. It has also been agreed that the 
period from 1st January, 1968 to the date of their respective joining their duties 
will be treated as if they were on leave without wages, for the continuity of service 
oilly. 

4. The above terms of compromise are fair and reasonable and are accepted. 
Accordingly, I pass an award in terms of the joint petition of the compromise, 
which is annexed with the award. It may now be submitted to the Central Govern- 
ment under section 15 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
