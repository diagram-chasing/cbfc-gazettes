method of recruitment to the post of Research Officer (Mechanical Tabulation ) in 
the Directorate of Economics and Statistics in the Department of Agriculture of 
the Ministry of Food , Agriculture , Community Development and Cooperation , 
namely : 

1. Short title and commencement. - ( 1 ) These rules may be called the Research 
Officer (Mechanical Tabulation ) ( Directorate of Economics and Statistics ) Recruit 
ment Rules , 1970 . 

(2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official 
Gazette . 

2. Applioation . These ruleg shall apply to the posts as specified in column 1 
of the Schedule annexed hereto . 

3 . Numbor , classification , scale of pay . — The number of the said post, its classi 
Acation and the scale of pay attached thereto , shall be as specified in columns 2 
to 4 of the said Schedule . 

4 . Method of recruitment, age limit , qualifications, etc. — The method of recruit 
ment, age limit and other matters relating to the said post shall be as specifed in 
columns 5 to 13 of the Schedule aforesaid : 

Provided that the upper age limit prescribed for direct recruitment may be 
Telaxed in the case of candidates belonging to the Scheduled Castes , Scheduled 
Tribes and other special categories of persons in accordance with the orders of the 
Central Government issued from time to time. 

5. Disqualification . — ( a ) No person , who has more than one wife living or who 
having a spouse living, marries in any case in which such marriage is void by 
reason of its taking place during the ute time of such spouse , shall be eligible 
for appointment to the said post ; and 

(b ) No woman , whose marriage is void by reason of the husband having & 
wife living at the time of such marriage , or who has married a person who has a 
wife living at the time of such marriage, shall be allgible for appointment to the 
said post : 

Provided that the Central Government may , if satisfied that there are special 
grounds for so ordering , exempt any person from the operation of thls rule . 

6 . Power to relax . - Where the Central Government is of opinion that it is 
necessary or expedient so to do , it may, by order , for reasons to be recorded in 
writing and in consultation with Union Public Service Commission relax any of 
the provisions of these rules with respect to any class or category of persons ,

साका०म० 158. - संविधान के अनुच्छेद 309 के परन्तुक द्वारा प्रदत्त शक्तियों का 
प्रयोग करते हुए राष्ट्रपति एतद्वारा खाद्य, कृषि, सामुदायिक विकास और सहकारिता मंत्रालय के कृषि 
विभाग में अर्थ और सांख्यिकी निदेशालय में अनुसंधान अधिकारी ( यांत्रिक सारणीयन ) के पद पर भर्ती 
की परति को विनियमित करने वाले निम्नलिखित नियम बनाते हैं , अर्थात् : -- 

संक्षिप्त नाम और प्रारम्म. -- ( 1 ) ये नियम अनुसंधान अधिकारी ( यांत्रिक सारणीयन ) 
( अर्थ और सांख्यिकी निदेशालय ) भर्ती नियम , 1970 कहे जा सकेंगे । 

( 2 ) ये शासकीय राजपत्र में अपने प्रकाशन की तारीख को प्रवृत्त होंगे . 
2. लागू होना.-- ये नियम इस से उपावद्ध अनुसूची के स्तम्भ में विनिर्दिष्ट पदों को लागू 


3. संख्या, वर्गीकरण , वेतनमाम .. - उक्त पदों की संख्या, उन का वर्गीकरण और उन से संलग्न 
वेतममान ये होंगे जो उक्त अनुसूची के स्तम्भ 2 से 4 तक में विनिर्दिष्ट हैं । 

4. भर्ती की पचति, प्रायु सीमहत एं प्रादि. - - भर्ती की परप्ति, प्रायु समापौर उपप्त 
पद से संबंधित अन्य बातें वे होंगी जो उपरोक्त अनसची के स्तम्भ 5 से 13 तक में विनिर्दिष्ट हैं : 

परन्तु अनुसूचित जातियों और अनुसूचित जन जातियों और अन्य विशेष व्यक्ति-प्रवर्गों के 
प्रभ्यर्थियों की दशा में सीधी भर्ती के लिये विहित उच्चतम प्राय सीमा समय- समय पर निकाले गये 
केन्द्रीय सरकार के प्रादेशों के अनुसार, शिथिल की जा सकेंगी । 


5. निरर्हता - - ( क ) यह व्यक्ति उषत पद पर नियुक्ति का पात्र नहीं होगा जिस की एक से 
अधिक पत्नियां जीवित हैं या जो एक पत्नी के जीवित रहते हुये किसी ऐसी दशा में विवाह करता है जिस 
में ऐसा विवाह इस कारण शून्य है कि वह ऐसी पत्नी के जीवन काल में होता है । 

( ख ) वह स्त्री उक्त पद पर नियुक्ति की पात्र नहीं होगी जिस का विवाह इस कारण शूल्य है 
कि उस विवाह के समय उस के पति की पत्नी जीवित थी या जिस ने ऐसे व्यक्ति से विवाह किया है जिस 
की पत्नी उस विवाह के समय जीवित थी । 

परन्तु केन्द्रीय सरकार का यह समाधान हो जाने पर कि किसी व्यक्ति को इस नियम के प्रवर्तन 
से छूट देने योग्य विशेष प्राधार हैं , यह आदेश दे सकेगी, कि उसे छूट दी जाये । 

6. शिथिल करने की शक्ति - - जहां केन्द्रीय सरकार की राय है कि ऐसा करना आवश्यक या 
समीचीन है, वहां वह उन कारणों से जो लेखबद्ध किये जाएंगे और संघलोक सेवा आयोग से परामर्श करके 
प्रादेश द्वारा व्यक्तियों के किसी वर्ग या प्रवर्ग के बारे में इन नियमों के उपबन्धों में से किसी को भी 
शिथिल कर सकेगी ।

Erplanatory Memorandum 
SUNDT. Inforcement of the Railway Board Secretariat Stenographers Service 

Rules, 1971 with retrospective effect from 1st Day of August, 1869 . 
The Railway Board Secretariat Stenographers Service was constituted 
with effect from the 1st Day of December , 1654 to man all posts of Stenogra 
phers in the Ministry of Railways (Railway Board ) . This service was constituted 
on the lines of the erstwhile Central Secretariat Stenographers Service . 

2 . The Ministry of Home Affairs recognised the Central Secretariat Steno 
graphers Service with effect from 1st August, 1969 by including the following 
four grades for Stenographers in the Central Secretariat : - - 
1. Selection Grade scale Rs. 350 - 900 . – Applicable to Private Secretaries to 

Secretaries / Additional Secretaries / Special Secretaries and Oncers of 

equivalent rank , 1st P .As. to Minister and P . Ss. to Deputy Ministero . 
2. Grade I , scale Rs. 350 - 25 - 850 - EB- 30 - 770. - Applicable to P. Ss . to Joint 

Secretaries and Officers of equivalent rank . 
3. Grade II, scale Rs. 210- 530.--- Applicable to Personal Assistants to Officers. 
4 . Grade III, scale Rs. 130 - 280 . — In lieu of the grades applicable to L . D . C . 

Steno - Typists / U . D . C . Steno - Typists. 
3 . Since the Stenographers in the Ministry of Railways have all along been 
given parity of scales of pay with their conferers in the Central Secretariat, it 
was decided to adopt the above mentioned scales for the Stenographers in the 
Ministry of Railways as well from the same date , viz . 1st August, 1969. 

4 . In pursuance of this decision the Railway Board Secretariat Stenographers 
Service Rules, 1971, have been drawn up and issued to bring the position on par 
as far as feasible with the Central Secretariat Stenographers Service Rules, 1969 . 

5 . It is further stated that the interests of no one would be prejudicially 
affected by reason of the decision to give retrospective effect from 1 - 8 - 1969 to 
the Railway Board Secretariat Stenographer s Service Rules, 1971 .

1 . Short title and commencement: - ( 1 ) These regulations may be called the 
Railway Board Secretariat Clerical Service (Lower Division Grade Competitive 
Examination for Class IV staff ) Regulations, 1971 

( 2 ) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official 
Gazette 

2. Definitions :-- ( 1 ) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires 

( a ) " crucial date” means the first day of January of the year in which the 
examination is held ; 

(b ) " available vacancies " means the vacancies in the Lower Division Grade of 
the Service which are decided to be Alled on the results of an examination ; 

( c ) " examination " means a competitive examination held by the Central Gov 
ernment in the Ministry of Railways for the appointment of Class IV employees 
to the Lower Division Grade of the Railway Board Secretariat Clerical Service ; 

( d ) regularly appointed Class IV employees " means an employee appointed 
in the Ministry of Railways ( Railway Board ) on a long - term basis according to the 
prescribed procedure ; 

( e ) " Scheduled Casteg" and " Scheduled Tribes " shall have the meanings 88 
signed to them by clauses ( 24 ) and ( 25 ) respectively of article 366 of the Cons 
titution of India . 

(2 ) Words and expressions used in these regulations and not defined but de 
fined in the Railway Board Secretariat Clerical Service Rules, 1970 , shall have 
the meanings respectively assigned to them in the said rules. 

3 . Holding of the examination . The examination shall be conducted by the 
Central Government in the Ministry of Railways in the manner notifled from timo 
to time by the said Ministry . 

( 2 ) The dates on which and the places at which the examination shall be held 
shall be fixed by the Central Government in the Ministry of Railways. 

4 . Conditions of eligibility etc .- - ( 1 ) Eligibility - Any permanent or regularly 
appointed temporary Class IV employee who satisfies the following conditions 
shall be eligible to appear at the examination : 

( a ) Length of Service . He should have on the crucial date , rendered not less 
than five years approved and continuous service as a Class IV employee or in any 
higher grade. 

NOTE. — ( 1 ) The limit of five years of approved and continuous service will also 
apply if the total reckonable service of a candidate is partly as a Class IV em 
ployee in the Ministry of Railways (Railway Board ) and partly elswhere in an 
equivalent or higher grade. 

NOTE ... - ( 2 ) Class IV employees who are on deputation to ex - cadre posts with 
the approval of the competent authority will be eligible to be admitted to the 
examination , if otherwise eligible . A class IV employee who has been appointed 
to an ex - cadre post or to another service on transfer , and continues to have a lien 
in the Class IV post for the time being will also be eligible to be admitted to the 
examination , if otherwise eligible. 

(b ) Age . He should not be more than 40 years of age on the crucial date: 

Provided that for the Arst two examinations to be held in accordance with 
these regulations, the upper age limit shall be relaxed upto 45 years ; 

Provided further that the upper age limit may be relaxed in respect of such 
categories of persons as may be notified from time to time in this behalf by the 
Central Government in the Ministry of Railways, to the extent and subject to 
the condition notifed in respect of each category.
