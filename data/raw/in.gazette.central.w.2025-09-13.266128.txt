सदस्य हैं।

गया सदस्य तीन वर्ष के कार्यकाल की शेष अवधि के लिए सदस्य रहेगा।

समिति के सदस्यों का कार्यकाल सामान्यतः इसकी अधिसूचना की तारीख से तीन वर्ष होगा, बशर्ते कि:-- 
1. जो संसद सदस्य समिति के सदस्य हैं, वे संसद सदस्य न रहने पर, इस समिति के सदस्य नहीं रह सकेंगे। 
2. समिति के पदेन सदस्य अपने पद पर कार्य करते रहने तक समिति के सदस्य होंगे; और 


3. यदि किसी सदस्य की मृत्यु हो जाने के कारण अथवा समिति की सदस्यता से त्यागपत्र देने के कारण स्थान रिक्त होता है तो उस 
स्थान पर नियुक्त सदस्य तीन वर्ष के कार्यकाल की शेष अवधि के लिए ही सदस्य होगा।

1. समिति का मुख्यालय नई दिल्‍ली में होगा।

मंत्रालयों/विभागों, राष्ट्रपति सचिवालय, उप राष्ट्रपति सचिवालय, प्रधान मंत्री कार्यालय, मंत्रिमंडल सचिवालय, संसदीय कार्य मंत्रालय,

The Samiti would render advice to the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution and all offices 
under its Administrative Control on matter relating to the progressive use of Hindi for official purpose and on allied 
issues falling within the framework of the policy laid down by the Ministry of Home Affairs (Department of Official 


language). 


3. Tenure 


The term of the Samiti will be for three years from the date of its reconstitution provided that: 


a) A member who is a Member of Parliament ceases to be a member of the Samiti as long as he ceases to be a 


Member of Parliament; 


b) Ex-officio member of the Samiti shall continue as member as long as they hold office by virtue of which they 


are members of the Samiti; 


c) Ifa vacancy arises on the Samiti due to resignation death, etc. of a member, the member appointed in that 


capacity shall hold office for the residual term of three years. 


4. General 


The headquarters of the Samiti shall be in New Delhi. As per the decision taken during the 31st meeting of the 
Kendriya Hindi Samiti constituted under the chairmanship of Honorable Prime Minister, held on 06.09.2018 and 
according to the Department of Official language letter no. 14013/01/2019 dated 21.01.2029, meetings of Hindi 


Salahakar Samiti should be held in Delhi. 


5. Travelling and Other Allowances 


For attending the meetings of the Committee, the non-official members of the Hindi Advisory Committee will be paid 
travelling allowance and daily allowance as per the rates and rules prescribed in accordance with the Department of 
Official Language's Office Memorandum No. II/20034/4/86-O.L.(A.-2) dated 22.01.1987 and as amended from time 


to time by the Government of India.
