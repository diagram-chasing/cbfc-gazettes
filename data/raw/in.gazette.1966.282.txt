member’s name denotes that he is a Follow, and “A" 
after a member’s name denotes that he is an Associate.

also been Indicated. 

An asterisk (*) before a member’s name indicates

OIL AND .NATURAL GAS COMMISSION 

Dehra Dun, the 25th July 1 966 

No. 17( 17) / 65 -Reg . — In exorcise ot the powers con- 
ferred by Section 32 of the Oil and Natural Gas Com- 
mission Act, 1959 (43 of 1959), the Commission hereby 
makes with the previous approval of the Central Govern- 
ment, the following regulations further to amend the Oil 
and Natural Gas Commission (Medical Examination of 
Employees) Regulations, 1965, namely : — 

1. These regulations may be called the Oil and Natural 
Gas Commission (Medical Examination of Employees) 
(First Amendment) Regulations, 1966 and shall be 
deemed to have come into force with effect from the 
25th July 1966, 

2. In the ONGC (Medical Examination of Employees) 
Regulations, 1965 lor the existing regulation 3 (c) the 
following shall be substituted, namely : — 

“3(c) : “Medical Fee" means the fees payable to the 
“Medical Authority” for conducting the medical exami- 
nation but does not include any expenditure incurred on 
the cardiographic examination of the chest, other X-ray 
examination, medical specialists’ fees, hospitalisation 


charges, laboratory fees etc. where such examination or 
hospitalisation is considered necessary by the Medical 
Authority. 

The fees re-imbursable to the employees shall in no 
case, exceed Rs. 16 in the case of medical examination 
by a Civil Surgeon and Rs. 8 in the case of medical 
examination by any Registered Medical Practitioner as 
prescribed under Regulation 3 (b) (ii). 

The rate of fees for medical examination for all cate- 
gories of employees by Medical Boards shall be Rs. 16 
only. 

In the case of employees belonging to group ‘A’ (for- 
merly known as Class IV), the fee reimbursable shall not 
exceed Rs. 2 in each case.” 

3. The existing schedule prescribing the rates of medi- 
cal fee attached to the ONGC (Medical Examination of 
Employees) Regulations, 1965 at page 9, shall be de- 
leted. 

K. K. DHAR
