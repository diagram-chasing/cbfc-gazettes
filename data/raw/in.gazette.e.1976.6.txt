ten per cent of the securities issued and outstanding of such 
company.

■dividend-paying stage whatl not at any time exceed five 
per cent of the total amount of the funds of this scheme. 

(2) Investments in convertible bonds/debentures of com¬ 
panies shall not in the aggiegate exceed fifteen per cent of 
Ihe total amount of the funds of this scheme.

shall b; applied with reference to circumstances at the time 
Ihe investments arc made, and shall not be deemed to have

is in fact exceeded.
