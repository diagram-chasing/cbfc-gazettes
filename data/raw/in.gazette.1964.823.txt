“(2) A candidate shall be declared to have passed

“55, Number of persons to be elected. — The num-

Institute in all constituencies, the resultant fraction 
exceeding half being counted as one and the result- 
ant fraction equivalent to or less than half being 
omitted : — 

Provided that each constituency shall have at 
least one person elected therefrom to the Council.”

send it to the Secretary by name and by registered 
post so as to reach him before the date and hour 
specified in this behalf. A voting paper not ad- 
dressed to the Secretary by name and not delivered 
by registered post shall be disregarded."
