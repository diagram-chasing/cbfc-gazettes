CANTONMENT BOARD 
Meerut Cantonment, the 1978 

No. WHEREAS draft of certain byelaws to 

legulate the recovery and collections of Show Tax on 
Cinema, Cijcus and Theatres was published with the Can- 
tonment Board’s Notice No. 509/T/3183, dated the 14th 
September, 1977, as required by section 284 of the Can- 
tonment Act, 1924 (2 of 1924), for inviting objections and 
suggestions till the J3tJi October, 1977; 

AND WHEREAS the said notice was put od the Can- 
tonment Notice Board on the 14th September, 1977; 

AND WHEREAS no objections or suggestions were re^ 
ccived from the public by the Cantonment Board before the 
said date; 

AND WHEREAS the Central Government have duly 
approved and confirmed the said draft of the bye-laws as 
required by sub-section (1) of section 284 of the said 
Act: 

NOW, THEREFORE in exercise of the powers conferred 
by clause (3) of section 282 and section 283 of the said 
Act, the Cantonment Board, Meerut, hereby makes the 
following bye-laws, namely ; — 

1. Short title and commencement 

(1) These bye-laws may be called the MEERUT CAN- 
TONMENT (Collection and Recovery of Show Tax) Bye- 
laws, 1978. 

(2) They shall come into froce on the date of then 
publication, in the Official Gazette. 

2. Definitions. — In these bye-laws, unless there is any- 
thing repugnant in the subject or context, — 

(1) “Act” means the Cantonments Act, 1924 (2 of 
1924): 

(2) “Cantonment Board" means , the Cantonment Board 
of Meerut; 

(3) “Cantonment Executive Officer” means the Can- 
tonment Executive Officer of the Meerut Canton- 
ment; 

(4) “Proprietor" includes a person responsible for the

2. Inspection of animals for slaughtering. — No animal 
shall be slaughtered in any slaughter house unless it has 
been inspected and passed by the Health Officer, Assistant 
Health Officer, Veterinary Surgeon, Sanitary Superintendent 
or Sanitary Inspector appointed in this behalf by the Can- 
tonment Board. 

3. Matters relating to Inspection. — (1) The Health Offi- 
cer, Assistant Health Officer, Veterinary Surgeon, Sanitary 
Superintendent or Sanitary Inspection appointed in this be- 
half by the Cantonment Board, shall examine every animal 
produced before him for slaughter and satisfy himself that 
the animal is — 

(i) for use as human food; and 

(ii) not diseased : 

Provided that an animal which has met with an accident 
rendering it unfit for further work shall not be rejected 
merely on this account. 

(2) The timings for the inspection of animals shall be 
as under : 

(a) From 2 to 4 P.M. for slaughtering on the same

(b) From 4 to 6 P.M. for slaughtering on the following

cified by the Cantonment Board 

5. Purpose for which slaughter house may he used. — 

(1) (a) Approved animals shall be immediately admitted

sale on the same day. 

TIT — Pigs. — October to March 5 P.M.

care and feed of their animals wffiile in the waiting yard.

7. Prohibition regarding entcrance of certain persons in the 
slaughter house premises — No person affected with tuber-

the slaughter house is not provided. 

9. Disposal of diseased animals, — (1) Any animal pro-

or contagious disease or which may reasonably be suspected

or Sanitary Inspector appointed in this behalf by the Can-

not be destroyed and tbc owner of the animal shall be res- 
ponsible for the maintenance of the animal during the 
period of observation.

without cruelty by its owner. 

(b) Tf compensation is awarded, it shall be assessed in 
accordance with the circumstances but in any case it shall 
not exceed the market value at the time of destruction of the 
nnimal destroyed.

animal produced for inspection which is in a dying condition 
but not so affected as to be dealt with under bye-law 9 shall, 
if the Ilenlth Officer, Assistant Health Officer, Veterinary 
Surgeon, Sanitary Superintendent or Sanitary Inspector 
appointed in this behalf by the Cantonment Board so directs, 
be forthwith seized and disposed of in such a manner as the 
Executive Officer may direct.

No person shall bring any carcass inside the slaughter house 
premises.
