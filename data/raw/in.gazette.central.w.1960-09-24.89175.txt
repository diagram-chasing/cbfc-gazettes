G . S . R . 1123. - In exercise of the powers conferred by section 17 of the 
Cinematograph Act, 1962 ( 37 of 1952 ) , the Central Government hereby exempts 
cinematograph exhibition of Almns in the premises of recognised educational insti 
tutions in the Union Territories of India from the operation of section 10 of the 
said Act, subject to the following conditions, namely : 


1. Only Alms of a predominantly educational nature , certified as such by 

the Central Board of Film Censors, or Alms approved as scientific 
Alms, Alms intended for educational purposes , films dealing with news 
and current events or documentary Alms approved by the Film 
Advisory Board shall be exhibited or permitted to be exhibited , 


2 . No admission fee , except to the extent required to cover expenses, shall 

be charged . 


3 . No person other than students and staff of the Institutions, members of 

the Managing Committees of the Institutions and any guests specially 
invited by the authorities or the Institutions and those enumerated 
under condition ( 9 ) below shall be admitted to the show . 


4 . Only non -inflammable Alms and film strips shall be exhibited or permitted 

to be exhibited . 


5 . The persons organising the exhibition of Alins shall be responsible for 

taking adequate safety measures against fire or any other mishap 
like the collapse of galleries , rooi etc. of the place where such films 
are exhibited . 


6 . No advertiseinent films shall be exhibited or promitted to be exhibited . 


7 . All Institutions which avail themselves of this exemption shall maintain 

a register of the Alms exhibited and comply with any other directions 
that may be issued in this behalf by the Administrations concerned . 
The register shall be open to inspection by duly authorised officers of 
each Administration , 


8 . This exemption shall be valid for a period of one ycar with effect from 

the date of issue of this notifcation , unless revoked earlier.
