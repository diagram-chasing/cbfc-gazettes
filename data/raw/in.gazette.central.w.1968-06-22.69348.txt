It is not dispute that the question of the payment of bonus is one of the items

12 . It is not dispute that the workmen in question have demanded from the

regarding the item of borus. An Industrial Dispute has been defined in Sec

writing refer the dispute or any matter appearing to be connected with or 
relevant to the dispute to the tribunal for enquiry . The Section mere requires

force as indented , and along with each gang of workers the Board deputes two 
supervisors, viz ., the loading mazdoors and the tindal. These two are part of the 
gang. The Stevedore has one Foreman for the entire operation , whether loading
