No. 1/3(1 )/64-/FF7 — The Rules of the Third Inter- 
national Film Festival of India, 1965 are hereby pub- 
lished for public information namely : — 

RULES 

1. The International Film Festival of India is orga- 
nised by the Government of India with the co- 
operation of the Film Federation of India. 

2. The objectives of the Festival are : 

(a) to provide a forum for the participating coun- 
tries to present films of artistic and cultural 
value and a high technical standard; 

(b) to contribute to the development of motion 
picture art and technique; and 

(c) to promote better understanding between 
peoples through mutual knowledge and 
appreciation of good films. 

3. The Festival will be held in New Delhi from 
8-1-1965 to 21-1-65. It will be followed by Film 
Weeks in Calcutta and Madras from 22-1-65 to 
28-1-65 and in Bombay from 29-1-65 to 4-2-65. 

4. Participating countries may enter long and short 
films or films falling within only one of these 
categories. 

Except when otherwise provided by the Com- 
mittee, films entered should : 

(1) have been produced after 1-1-1963;

(2) not have been shown in India; except in the 
case of Indian films; 

(3) not have been entered in any competitive 
International Film Festival; and 

(4) bo consistent with the objectives of the 
Festival. 

5. Each country can enter ; — 

—One long feature film; 

— One short film. 

In case a particular country prefers not to enter 
a long film, it can enter two short films. 

Except when otherwise provided, short ms 
should not exceed 1500 metres in lengtl '.ily

6. Each country should intimate to the i estival 
Directorate before 31st August, 1964, whether it 
will or not participate in the Festival. The 
titles of the films to be entered together with the 
names and addresses of the Producers and tech- 
nical casts should reach the Festival Directorate 
before 30th September, 1964. The prints of films 
should reach the Festival Directorate by the 31st 
October, 1964. 

7. An entry may be made by the Government or 
alternatively by the representative film organisa- 
tion of the country. The entry will be made 
in Form ‘A’ hereto annexed. 

8. In addition to the films entered and accepted, 
the Festival Directorate is entitled to invite with 
the consent of the competent national organisa- 
tions, films which because of their quality would 
constitute a valuable enrichment of the festival 
programme. These invited films will participate 
in the competition. The number of such films 
shall, however, not exceed l/3rd of the total 
number of films presented at the Festival. 

9. All films must be shown in their original lan- 
guages. Films in languages other than English 
and Hindi must have sub-titles in English or 
Hindi. 

10. The Festival Directorate will be responsible for 
the viewing and selection of films for presenta- 
tion at the Festival. The films will be shown in 
the order decided upon by the Festival Directo- 
rate. 

11. Each participating country may be represented by 
a delegation. Delegates not exceeding 2 from

of the guest delegates as also on their travel in

12. The jury of long films shall consist of upto 15 
Indian or foreign personalities. The jury of 
short films shall consist of 5 Indian ot foreign 
personalities. The majority of the jury members 
will be non-Indians. At least l/5tn of the jury 
members shall consist of active film producers. 

13. The votes of the members of the jury will be by 
secret ballot. Decisions of the jury shall be 
taken by the majority of the members voting at 
the first three ballots and by a relative majority 
at the fourth ballot, if any is taken. 

The Director of the Festival may be present at 
the deliberations of the jury. He will not take 
part in the vote. 

14. No person having participated in the production 
or distribution of a film shown at the Festival 
may be a member of the jury. 

15. The jury of long films shall grant : 

—THE GOLDEN AWARD OF THE INTER- 
NATIONAL FILM FESTIVAL OF INDIA, 
1965. 

for the best feature film. 

In addition, the jury may grant : 

—THE JURY SPECIAL PRIZE reserved either 
for a documentary or for another feature film of 
exceptional quality; 

—THE PRIZE FOR THE BEST FEMALE 
ACTING; 

—THE PRIZE FOR THE BEST MALE ACT- 
ING; 

— 2 other prizes to be decided upon by the jury. 
The jury of short films shall grant : 

—THE GOLDEN AWARD OF THE INTER- 
NATIONAL FILM FESTIVAL OF INDIA, 
1965. 

for the best short film. 

In addition, the jury may grant : 

— 2 other prizes which may be decided upon by 
the jury. 

16. Journalists who are members of the Festival jury 
may not send critical reviews of the films sub- 
mitted to their judgment to newspapers before 
publication of the jury’s decision. 

17. Transportation and insurance of prints and publi- 
city material of films from the country of origin 
to the Festival and back will be at the expense 
of the entrant. 

18. Prints of films will be accepted at the risk of 
the participants and, while the Festival Directo- 
rate will take every reasonable care thereof and 
will make every effort to reship the prints to the 
producers within 10 days after the close of the 
Festival and Film Weeks, it cannot accept res- 
ponsibility for the loss or damage to the prints 
while in its possession. 

19. The prints of films entered in the Festival, publi- 
city material and all correspondence relating 
thereto should be addressed to : 

The Director, 

Third International Film Festival of India, 
Ministry of Information & Broadcasting, 
Government of India, 

New Delhi- 1. 

20. Participation in the Festival will be regarded as 
acceptance of these rules. 

21. The Festival Directorate will decide on all matters 
not expressly provided for in these rules and its 
decisions thereon shall be final. The Directorate 
reserves the right to make a relaxation, in special 
circumstances, from any of the provisions of these 
rules in any individual case, with the approval of 
Government.
