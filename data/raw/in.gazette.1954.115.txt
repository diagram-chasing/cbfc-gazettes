BEFORE THE RAILWAY RATES TRIBUNAL, MADRAS 

(Public Notice under Rule 17 of the Railway Rates 
Tribunal Riiles, 1949) 

Complaint No. 3 of 1954 

The Mettur Chemical and Industrial Cor- 
poration Ltd., Mettur Dam R.S. Applicant. 

Versus 

1. The Southern Railway, Madras. 

2. Union of India owning and representing 

Respondent No. 1, abovenamed. Respondents. 

Whereas the applicant abovenamed has complained that 
the cancellation of the station-to-station rate of Re. 0-6-8 
per maund for Salt quoted from Adirampatnam to Mettur 
Dam and the levy of wagon load rate of Re. 0-10-5 per 


maund is unreasonable and has prayed that a station-to- 
station rate not exceeding Rs. 0-6-8 per maund may be 
fixed for Salt from Adirampatnam to Mettur Dam. 

And whereas it is thought that there are numerous 
persons who are not on record but have the same interest 
in the proceedings as the applicant or respondents above- 
named ; 

This public notice is, therefore, given under Rule 17 of 
the Railway Rates Tribunal Rules 1949, so that any person 
who so desires may petition to the Tribunal for leave to 
intervene on the side of the applicant or the respondents 
in the said case, prior to, or at the time it is called for 
hearing, setting forth the grounds for the application and 
the position and the interest of the petitioner in the pro- 
ceedings. 

To any decision given by the Tribunal after this Public 
Notice, explanation (vi) to Section 11 of the Code of Civil 
Procedure, 1908, shall apply. 

Given under my hand and the Seal of the Tribunal this 
10th day of June 1954 at “Adyar House”, Adyar, Madras.
