ANNEXURE 


FUNCTION OF THE COMMITTEE 

The function of the Committee will be to review the position and tender advice in regard to progressive use of Hindi 
for official purpose in the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting and its Attached/Subordinate 
Offices/Corporations/Boards/Media Units etc. in accordance with the policies laid down by the Kendriya Hindi Samiti and 
the Department of Official Language (Ministry of Home Affairs) on the subject. 


TENURE 


The tenure of members of the Committee will ordinarily be three years from the date of constitution of the 
Committee provided:- 


1. Members of the Parliament, who are members of the Committee, shall cease to be Members of the Committee 
as soon as they cease to be Members of the Parliament. 

2. The ex-officio members of the Committee shall continue members so long as they hold office by virtue of 
which they are members of the Committee and 

3. If a vacancy arises in the Committee due to resignation or death of a member, the appointee to that vacancy 
shall hold office for the residual period of the tenure of three years.
