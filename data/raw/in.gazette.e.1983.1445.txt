MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 2nd December, 1983 

S. O. 875(E).—Whereas the Central Government is of the 
opinion that the film "THE ENTITY” (English) as certified by 
the Board of Film Certification contains scenes of horror and 
cruelty having an unsavoury impact on the viewers and scenes 
of nudity of female body and sexual scenes which are offensive 
to human sensibilities and whereas the fiim offends the guidelines 
relating to violence, cruelty, horror, vulgarity and obscenity 
mentioned in para. 2(iii) and 2(iv) of the guidelines issued under 
this Ministry's notification No. F. 5J5|77-F(C) dated 7-1-78 (as 
amended) and thus contravenes the provisions relating to “dec¬ 
ency or morality” and “incitement to the commission of any 
offence” within the meaning of section 5B(1) of the Cinemato¬ 
graph Act 1952 (37 of 1952) (hereinafter called the said Act): 



[ VTI’T II— <TS a (ii ) ] -*TT *T Tp,qX : STOTH tT.H 3 

Therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) 
of sub section (2) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central Gov¬ 
ernment hereby directs that the film entitled )‘THE ENTITY" 
(English) which was granted “A” certificate No, A-5063 dated 
27-5-83 by the Central Board of Film Certification, Bombay, 
shall be deemed to be an uncertified film in the whole of India 
with effect from the date of issue of this Notification.
