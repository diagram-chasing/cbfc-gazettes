Mr. President and Members of the Dargah Committee. 

Ajmer 

I beg to submit my annual administration report on 
the working of the Dargah Administration for the year 
1962-63 for your consideration and needful. 

At the outset, 1 would like to deal with the adminis- 
trative problems. During the year under review, there 
were no troubles with the khadims, except that litigation 
indulged in by them continued and will continue for 
some time, but the Dargah Administration will always 
be on the defensive, as it is it’s duty to protect Dargah 
rights conferred under the Dargah Khawaja Sahib Act, 
1955 and as clarified by the Supreme Court in their 
Judgment of March 1961. The nature of the present 
litigation is extremely intriguing. People who had so- 
called vested interest in Dargah offerings or in its pro- 
perty income prior to the enforcement of the Act of 
1955, have adopted a new and novel way of defeating 
the object of the Act by quietly bringing suits in various 
courts in regard to their supposed rights, but as soon as 
such cases come to notice, prompt action is taken by the 
Dargah Administration and applications are made to the 
Courts concerned requesting that Dargah may be made 
a party in such suits. During the year under review, 
there were no cases of disorder in Dargah and no un- 
desirable open activities were brought to notice except 
that the exploitation of the pilgrims continued unabated. 
This abuse can not be rooted out unless the Act is suit- 
ably amended laying down a penal clause for such 
offences. Propaganda against Dargah Administration 
continued throughout the year through printed literature 
and by personal canvassing. The Dargah administra- 
tion continued its own publicity work by apprising the 
public of the true facts. Our publicity was carried out 
in a proper manner by placing bare facts before the 
public and their co-operation was invited in maintaining 
the dignity of the Shrine, and this had good effect. In 
fact, the behaviour of the local people towards the pil- 
grims, supplemented by our printed literature, is proving 
useful, as reflected by the direct approach of many pil- 
grims with their voluntary contributions towards the 
Dargah fund. Building improvements in the Dargah area 
and the facilities afforded to the visitors, specially during 
Urs, are also creating good impression. I hope that with 
the completion of a new building contemplated for the 
accommodation of the pilgrims much needed amenities 
will be provided to them which is the main problem at 
present.

ANNUAL URS 

The annual urs of 1962 was celebrated as usual from 
29-11-1962 to 4-12-1962. The number of pilgnras who 
visited Dargah during Urs is reported to have not exceed- 
ed one lakh of people. Canopies and Shamyanas provid- 
ed by Dargah administration were sufficient for the needs 
of the pilgrims, A special camp for the stay of pilgrims 
was also erected in Anderkote. Special and extra 
arrangements for the supply of water and lipht were 
made. One way traffic proved useful. Distribution of 
Langar and looting of cooked food from the Degs were 
earned out in an orderly way. Mahaflls in Samakhana 
were conducted in a fitting manner. No political parties 
were allowed to discuss politics during Urs. Medical 
facilities were provided at all hours and efforts were 
made to keep Dargah area clean. Shortage of Urinals is 
a great problem to which Dargah Committee may kind- 
ly give serious attention.
