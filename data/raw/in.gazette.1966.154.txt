Full signing powers in connection with the functions

of the Indian Penal Code in respect of any information

and to sign all papers or documents and generally to act 
on behalf of the Reserve Bank in all proceedings in con- 
nection with or arising out of such complaints.

used in the singular shall, wherever necessary, include

with the current duties of such post.

December 1965.

THE OIL AND NATURAL GAS COMMISSION 

New Delhi, the 18f/» December 1965 

No. 17( 17) /65-Re, y. — In exercise of the powers confer- 
red by Section 32 of the Oil & Natural Gas Commission 
Act, 1959 (43 of 1959), the Commission hereby makes, 
with the previous approval of the Central Government, 
the following regulations : — - 

1. These regulations may be called the Oil and Natural 
Gas Commission (Medical Examination of Employees) 
Regulations, 1965 and shall be deemed to have come 
into force with effect from 17-4-1963. 

2. MEDICAL EXAMINATION FOR APPOINTMENT: 

Every candidate shall be required to undergo a medical 
examination of the appropriate standard by a Medical 
Authority, mentioned in Regulation 3(b), at the time of 
his first appointment to a post under the Commission, 
including appointment on contract or part-time basis, and 
shall be required to produce a Certificate of fitness from 
that Authority. Once a candidate is asked by an autho- 
riy to produce a medical certificate of fitness either for 
entry into the Commission’s service or for any other 
urpose and has actually been examined and declared un- 
t, it is not open to such authority to use its discretion 
to ignore the certificate that has been produced. The 
cases where it becomes absolutely necessary to retain the 
services of such an employee on a temporary basis due 
to certain administrative reasons should be referred to the 
Directorate of Administration and Secretariat, for obtain- 
ing approval of the Commission. 

(i) Provided that the Commission may by order 
exempt either permanently or temporarily any candidate 
or a class of candidates from the production of such 
certificate of fitness. If temporary exemption is granted 
under this regulation, the medical examination shall be 
required to be completed within a period of three months 
from the date of actual joining of the post. 


(ii) Provided further that the following classes of em- 
ployees shall not be required to produce such a Medical 
Certificate of fitness for appointment to a post under the 
Commission. 

(a) An employee of the Commission, who has been 
medically examined by the appropriate Medical 
standurd at the time of initial appointment is 
appointed to another post in the Commission, 
requiring medical examination of an equivalent 
standard; 

(b) An employee of the Commission holding a post 
the maximum of the scale of pay of which is 
Rs. 1,250, or less, who has been examined by 
the Appropriate medical standard at the time 
of initial appointment, and who is promoted to 
another post against a promotion quota of 
vacancies. 

(c) A retrenched employee of the Commission or 
of the Central/ State Government, who is ap- 
pointed to a post in the Commission within 
one year of the date of retrenchment, provided 
he had undergone medical examination of the 
standard applicable to the post, in which he is 
re-employed, prior to his retrenchment. 

(d) A permanent/quasi-permanent Gazetted Officer 
of the Central/State Government, who is ap- 
pointed to a post in the Commission without a 
break in service. 

(e) A permanent or quasi-permanent Class III/ 
Class IV employee of the Central or State 
Government, who is appointed in the Commis- 
sion to a post, the maximum of the scale of 
pay of which is less than Rs. 1,250, without a 
break in service. 

(f) A permanent or quasi-permanent employee of 
the Central/ State Government, who has been 
appoined on foreign service terms in the Com- 
mission and who is subsequently promoted to 
a higher post in the Commission against a pro- 
motion quota of vacancies, or is appointed to a

9. APPEALS : 

(i) If an officer is aggrieved by the findings of the 
Medical Authority, he may, within a period of one month 
from the date on which the findings are communicated 


to him submit a request for being examined by a Medical 
Review Board/ Reviewing Medical Officer, as the case 
may be, and may produce necessary evidence in support 
of his request. 

(ii) Save as provided in regulation 6(vi), if the ap- 
pointing authority is satisfied on the evidence placed 
before it by the candidate/ employee, of the possibility of 
an error of judgement in the decision of the Medical 
Authority, it will be open to the appointing authority to 
allow the re-examination. 

(iii) If any Medical Certificate is produced by the 
candidate/ employee as a piece of evidence about the pos- 
sibility of an error of judgement in the decision of the 
Medical Authority, who had examined him in the first 
instance, the certificate will not be taken into considera- 
tion unless it contains a note by the Medical practitioner, 
who gives the certificate, to the effect that it has been 
given in full knowledge of the fact that the candidate 
has already been rejected as unfit for service by a Medical 
Board, or a Medical Officer as the case may be. 

(iv) If a candidate for a post, the maximum of the 
scale of pay of which is Rs. 1250 or more, is declared 
medically unfit on account of a defect in visual acuity, 
special Medical Board the composition of which should 
include two ophthalmologists. 

(v) If an appeal is preferred under this regulation by 
a persons, who is already in the employment of the Com- 
mission. he shall be retained in service till the findings 
of the Medical Review Board or the Reviewing Medical 
Officer, as the case may be, become available. Further 
action for the termination of the service of the employee 
or his retention in service, shall be taken on the basis of 
the recommendations of the Medical Review Board/ 
Reviewing Medical Officer, as the case may he.

decide each case on its merits. In all matters about the 
interpretation of these regulations the decision of the 
Directorate of Administration shall be final.

Medical Examiner's Report 

The following information is made Cor the guidance of 
the Medical Examiners : — ■ 

The standard of physical fitness to be adopted should 
make due allowance for the age and length of service, 
if any, of the candidate concerned. 


[Part III— Sec. 4 


No person will be deemed qualified for appointment, 
who shall not satisfy the Commission that he has no 
disease, constitutional affection, or bodily informity un- 
fitting him or likely to unfit him for that service. 

It should be understood that the question of fitness 
involves the future as well as the present and that one 
of the main objects of medical examination is to secure 
continuous effective service, and in the case of candidate 
for permanent appointment to prevent early pension or 
payments in case of premature death. It is at the same 
time to be noted that the question is one of the likeli- 
hood of continuous effective service, and the rejection of 
a candidate need not be advised on account of the pre- 
sence of a defect which in only a small proportion of 
cases, is found to interfere with continuous effective 
service. 

Ju the event of a candidate declared ‘unfit’ by a Civil 
Surgeon which is challenged by the candidate, the Com- 
mission may refer the matter to a Board of medical 
exuminers which should normally consist of three mem- 
bers, namely, (i) a physician, (ii) a surgeon, and (in') 
a opthalmologist and should be of comparable status. A 
lady doctor will be co-opted as a member of such Board 
whenever a woman candidate is to be examined. 

The report of the Board should be treated as Confiden- 
tial, 

In cases where a Civil Surgeon or a Board of Medical 
Examiners consider (s') that a minor disability disqualify- 
ing a candidate for service in the Commission can be 
cured by treatment (medical or surgical), a statement to 
that effect should be recorded. There is no objection to 
a candidate being informed to this effect by the appoint- 
ing authority and when a cure has been effected it will 
be open to the authority concerned to ask for another 
medical examination.
