BEFORE THE RAILWAY RATES TRIBUNAL AT 
MADRAS 

(Public notice under Rules 17 and 71 of the Railway Rates 
Tribunal Rules, 1949) 

Application No. 1 of 1954 

Central Government. Applicant. 

Whereas the Central Government has made an applica- 
tion under Section 42(1) of the Indian Railways Act, 1890 
(XX of 1890) that the existing entry “ safflower ” appear- 
ing under " seeds common ” at page 231 of the Indian 
Railway Conference Association Goods Tariff No. 28 be 
deleted and be included in the list of synonyms as charge- 
able under sub-head “ Kusum Seed ” indexed to Oil Seeds 
NOC” and that the entries of Kar Seeds, Kurdee Seeds 
and Coosoomba Seeds shown in the list of synonyms as 
chargeable under sub-head “ Safflower " be also made 
chargeable as “ Kusum Seed ” under the main head “ Oil 
Seeds NOC ”, thereby enhancing the classification of 
Safflower, Kar Seeds, Kurdee Seeds, and Coosoomba Seeds 
from 1st class to 4th class. 

This public notice is, therefore, given so that any person 
who so desires may petition to the Tribunal for leave to 
intervene either in favour of or against the application, 
setting forth the grounds for his application and his posi- 
tion and interest in the proceedings. Such a petition 
should be lodged with the undersigned at the headquarters 
of this Tribunal or sent by post as a registered letter to 
his address and must be received on or before 30th June 
1954 accompanied, by a fee of Rs. 2. 

Given under the seal of the Tribunal, this 10th day of 
May 1954 at “ Adyar House ”, Adyar, Madras 28. 

J. B. RAO 
Secretary 
Railway Rates Tribunal
