New Delhi- 1 , the 3 Ur August 1966 

(Chartered Accountants) 

No,. l-C-4(8)/ 1/66 — In exercise of the powers con- 
ferred by sub-section (1) of Section 30 of the Chartered 
Accountants Act, 1949 (XXXVIII of 1949), the Coun- 
cil of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India 
has made the following amendments to the Chartered 
Accountants Regulations, 1964, the same having been 
previously published and approved by the Central Gov- 
ernment as required under sub-section (3) of the said 
Section. 

In the said Regulations : — 

1. Insert the following as sub-regulation (3) in regu- 
lation 114 and re-number the existing sub-regulation (3) 
as sub-regulation (4) : 

"(3) The accounts of a Regional Council, as main- 
tained by the Treasurer, shall be audited every year 
by a chartered accountant in practice or a firm of 
such chartered accountants appointed by the mem- 
bers of the region at their annual general meeting 
and the accounts, together with the audit report and 
the report of the Regional Council, shall be sent 
to the members in the region at least 14 days before 
the date of the annual general meeting and shall be 
placed for adopting before the annual general meet- 
ing of the members.” 

2. In the re-numbered sub-regulation (4) of regulation 
114:— 

(a) In clause (i), after the words “A copy of and 
before the words ‘‘the report of the”, insert the 
words “the audited accounts and." 

(b) for the existing clause (ii), substitute the 
following : — 

“(ii) The audited accounts of the Regional 
Council and its branches, if any, shall be in- 
corporated in the accounts of the Council for 
the year.” 

3. For the existing regulations 123 and 124, substitute 
the following : 

“123. Annual general meeting 

A General Meeting of the members of the Region 
shall be called between the 15th day of May and 
the 15th day of June every year and it shall be 
called the Annual General Meeting." 

“124. Extraordinary general meeting 

A Regional Council may call such further meet- 
ings of the members of the Region as often as it 
may deem necessary and such meetings shall be 
called Extraordinary General Meetings." 

1. For the existing regulation 125, substitute the 
following : 

“125. Notice of meeting 

At least 14 days' notice of every meet'ng specify- 
ing the day, place and hour of such meeting and in 
the case of special business, the general nature of 
the business, shall be given, In case of the annual 
general meeting, the report of the Regional Council, 
together with Audi lore’ Report and a copy of the 
audited Balance Sheet and Income and Expenditure 
Account for the previous year shall accompany 
the notice." 

5. In regulation 128, after the words “the report of 
the Regional Council”, insert the words "to adopt the 
accounts, to appoint auditors”. 

No. 1-CA (81/2/66 — In pursuance of sub-regulation 
(2) or regulation 59 of the Chartered Accountants Re- 
gulations, 1964. the Council of the Institute of Chartered 
Accountants of India is pleased to make the following 
amendments in the Chartered Accountants Students 
Association Rules. 

In the said Rules ; — 

1. For tha existing clause (v) of rule 26, substitute 
the following : — 

“(v) The annual accounts of the Students’ Asso- 
ciation shall be subject to audit by a chartered 
accountant in practice or a firm of such chartered 


accountants appointed by the Regional Council 
concerned to audit its accounts. The accounts of 
the Students’ Associations and its branches, if any 
shall be incorporated in the accounts of the Central 
Council for the year." 

2. For the existing clause (vi) of rule 26, substitute 

the following : — 

"(VI) As soon as may be practicable at the end of 
each year ending on 31st March, the Students’ Asso- 
ciation Managing Committee shall submit to the 
Central Council and the Regional Council concerned 
a copy of the audited accounts and its report for 
that year and copies of the said accounts and report 
shall be forwarded to all the members of the 
Association.” 

3. For the existing rule 34, substitute the following : 

"34. One general meeting shall be held between the 
15th day of May and the 15th day of June every 
year and that meeting shall be called the annual 
general meeting.” 

4. In rule 37, add the words “to adopt the accounts" 
after the, words “Managing Committee” and before the 
words “and to transact”.
