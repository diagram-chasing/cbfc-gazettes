Redesignation of officers posted in the Development 


Department at Head Office and selected branches in con- 
nection with the financing of Small Scale Industrial Units 
as Field Officers and granting them certain powers and 
responsibilities 

Consequent upon the entrustment of certain discre- 
tionary powers to the Development Officer at Head Office 
and the appointment of Sub-Managers at selected bran- 
ches for ensuring that the liberalised scheme for financ- 
ing the Small Scale Industrial Units can be implemented 
with greater care, vigour and success, the Board of Direc- 
tors of State Bank of Hyderabad at its meeting held on 
the 11th January 1965 resolved that : 

(A) The Junior Officer and/or officers ‘C’ Grade post- 
ed for implementing the liberalised scheme for financing 
of Small Industrial Units at. Head Office and at selected 
branches be designated as "Field Officers"; 

(B) The Field Officers be entrusted with the follow- 
ing duties : 

(a) Survey and resurvey of Small Scale Industrial 
Units functioning in the areas of operation, of 
the branch where they are posted; keeping 
close and continuous contact with the autho- 
rities and associations concerned with the deve- 
lopment of Small Scale Industrial Units; 

(b) Assisting the Manager and the Sub-Manager in 
processing the proposals for advances; 

(c) Inspection in accordance with the instructions 
laid down, of stocks, machinery etc., pledged 
or hypothecated to the bank as also of pro- 
perty, machinery etc., mortgaged to the Bank; 

(d) Performing other routine duties and compiling 
periodical returns and statements and handling 
of its correspondence; 

(e) Assisting the borrowers in compiling the finan- 
cial statements; 

(f) Collecting the required information relating to 
market rates of various items of raw materials 
and finished products held as security against 
loans, advances, overdrafts and other forms 
of accommodation granted to Small Scale In- 
dustrialists and certify the valuations stated in 
the pledge invoices and verify to the extent 
possible the value of tjhc goods stated in the 
other documents of title to goods such as 
Railway Receipts, Track Receipts etc., received 
from Small Scale Industrialists against accom- 
modation granted to them; 

(g) Verify the genuineness of the bills and docu- 
ments of title to goods such as Railway Receipts 
Truck Receipts accompanying them as also the 
clean bills accepted by the Bank for granting 
tho aforesaid accommodations; 

(h) Compile and sign and periodically revise and 
sign the opinion reports that are at present 
compiled and signed by the Head Cashier; 

(i) When the Sub-Manager is on leave or his post 
is vacant, the Field Officer be entrusted with 
the following further duties; 

(i) Signing delivery orders; 

(11) Signing drawing powers on accounts; 

(iii) Retaining the godown keys In his custody 
when not in use.

BEFORE THE RAILWAY RATES TRIBUNAL AT 
MADRAS 

[Public Notice issued under Rule 19(3) and (4) of the 
Railway Rates Tribunal Rules, 1959] 
Complaint No. 5 of 1965 

The Bhopal Sugar Industries Ltd., Schorc, Madhya 
Pradesh — Complainants. 

versus 

The Union of India, represented by tite General Mana- 
ger, Western Railway, Western Railway Buildings, 
Churchgate, Fort, Bombay-1 — Respondents. 

Whereas the Complainants obovenamed have made a 
complaint that they have a Sugai Mil at Sehore in 
Madhya Pradesh for the purpose of manufacturing Sugar 
and other allied product-, that the mill is connected with 
Sehore Railway Station by a siding, th it the said siding 
was constructed at the sole cost of the complainants, that 
the complainants and the then G.eat Indian Peninsular 
Railway entered into an agreement on 5th October 
1948, in respect of its working, maintenance, etc. that 
under the terms of the said agreement the complainants 
had to pay to the respondents ( 1 ) for the maintenance 
of the siding, the actual cost, plus 121% supervision 
charges thereon and (2) for the use of the siding a sid- 
ing charge at the rate of Re. 1 per four wheeled loaded 
wagon and at the rate of Rs. 2 per bogie loaded wagon 
subject to a minimum of Rs. f J .25 per trip that as per 
the said stipulations they had been paying the charges 
on the above basis from the date of the agreement till 
5-2-1963. 

That subsequently the respondents had levised the 
maintenance charges and demanded payment of them in 
two different modes, viz- one, charges for ordinary main- 
tenance of sub-grade work of siding at actual cost plus 
supervision charges at the rate of 123% thereon; two, 
charges for maintenance of permanent way and all struc- 
tures and stationery machinery at 33% of the Capital 


cost of providing permanent way structures and station- 
ary machinery irrespective of the work carried out or 
not, and also a siding charge as may be fixed from time 
to time on the basis of the time taken by the engine 
tor placement and removal of wagon in/or from the 
siding and called upon them to execute a fresh agree- 
ment, failing which, they threatened to disconnect the 
sidmg; that they, to avoid their business coming to a 
vop and having no other alternative, executed a fresh 
agreement on 5-2-1963, that subsequently the mainten- 
ance charges mentioned as (2) above have been further 
revised as 33% of book value or present day cost which- 
ever is more and were increased for the period from 
10-7-1964 to 30-9-1964 to Rs. 1,670.87 and to 
Rs. 8,503.11 for the period from 1-10-1964 to 30-9- 
1965; 

That the respondents have also again revised the siding 
charges to Re. 1 per 4 wheeled loaded wagon and per 4 
wheeled empty wagon, if hauled empty bothways, sub- 
ject to a minimum charge of Rs. 42 per shunt and be- 
gan to levy them from 1-4-1964 that all the said revis- 
ed charges are exhorbitant, arbitrary, unjust and un- 
reasonable, that the siding charges not being the freight 
charges, no surcharge should be levied on the same, that 
the revision by the Respondent of the rent for the land 
occupied by private sidings, within the railway limits 
from Rs. 5 to a sum calculated at the rate of 6% of the 
market value of the same is exhorbitant unreasonable and 
unjustifiable and have prayed for (a) the fixation of 
maintenance and siding charges on the basis of the 
agreement dated 5-10-1948; (b) fixation of siding charge 
for haulage of wagons on the basis of actual time taken 
by the engine for placement or removal of wagon/s 
from the siding without stipulation of minimum charges 
for fixation of such charges not exceeding Rs. 18 for to 
and fro engine trip for placement and removal of the 
wagon/s and to restrain the Respondents from levying 
any surcharge thereon; (c) for refund of the amounts 
collected and/or deposited in. excess of the charges as 
may be fixed by the Tribunal which the Complainants 
have paid and/or may be compelled to pay and for 
costs; 

And whereas it is thought that there may be persons 
w'ho are not on record but have the same interest in the 
proceedings as the Complainants or the respondents 
ubovenamed; 

This public notice is, therefore, given under Rule 
19(3) and (4) of the Railway Rates Tribunal Rules, 
1959. so that any person who so desires, may petition 
the Tribunal within thirty days of the publications of this 
notice for leave to intervene in support of/ or opposition 
to the relief sought in the complaint, or be added on 
the side of the complainants or the respondents setting 
lorth the grounds of the proposed intervention, and the 
position, and the interest of the petitioner in the pro- 
ceedings; or the grounds for being added as a party in 
die said case. Any decision given by the Tribunal after 
this public notice shall apply to all such persons.
