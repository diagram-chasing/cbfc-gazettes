PUBLIC NOTICE NO. 105|45|86-F (I) 

No. 105|34|85-F(1).—In pursuance of clause 2.4. 
of the Policy for Import of feature films and Distribu¬ 
tion and Pricing of Imported Feature Films announc¬ 
ed vide Public Notice No. 112]7|84-F(0, dated 12th 
October, 1984 as amended vide Public Notice No. 
112|7|S4-F(I) dated 1st July, 1985, the Ministry of 
Information and Broadcasting hereby notify the Gui¬ 
delines for the import of foreign feature films by pri¬ 
vate Indian parties on the occasion of 11th Inter¬ 
national Film Festival of India to be held in New 
Delhi from January 10—24, 1987, a ; in the Annexure. 

K. S. BA1DWAN, Jt. Secy. 

ANNEXURE 

GUIDELINES FOR THE IMPORT OF FEATURE 
FILMS BY PRIVATE INDIAN PARTIES ON THE 
OCCASION OF THE 11TH INTERNATIONAL 
FILM FESTIVAL OF INDIA, 1987 

t For the purpose of these Guidelines:— 

(a) “the Committee” means the Contract Appro¬ 
val Committee to be set up by the Corpora- 

• tion under guideline 16; 

(b) “the Corporation” means the National Film 
Development Corporation, Bombay; 

(c) “the Festival” means 11th International 
Film Festival of India to be organised by 
the Directorate of Film Festivals, National 
Film Development Corporation in New 
Delhi from January 10 to 24, 1987; 

(d) “a film” means a foreign feature film ; and 

( e) “a film entered in the Efim Market” includes 
not only a film entered in the" Film Market 
Section of the Festival, but also a film 
screened in various other Sections of the 
Festival. 

2. Films shall be authorised to be imported by pri¬ 
vate Indian parties only and from amongst the films 
screened in an official section or entered in the Film 
Market. 

3. Imports shall be permitted only in regard to 
theatricnl|tclevision rights in India. 

4. The total import of films by private Indian par¬ 
ties shall be restricted to the value of Rs. 25 lakhs in¬ 
cluding royalty, cost of prints, freight charges and all 
other expenses payable in foreign exchange, but ex¬ 
cluding customs duty payable in rupees, in India. 

5. A maximum of 25 films by private Indian parlies 
shall be allowed to be imported. 

6. A film proposed to be imported should meet 
the following requirements regarding quality of films : 


(a) The films should not be violative of any 
Guidelines issued by the Government under 
the Cinematograph Act, 1952; 

00 It should be of aesthetic value; 

(c) It should be cinematically of good standard; 
and 

(d) It should provide clean, healthy entertain¬ 
ment to the audience. 

7. The price per film, including royalty and cost of 
prints, trailers etc, shall be subject to a ceiling of 
Rs. 2.5 lakhs. 

8. A private Indian party shall not be permitted to 
purchase a film from a non-resident Indian. 

9. A seller, if he|she is not the producerlright-holder, 
shall produce documents about the authority vested 
in him|her by the producer|right-holder to sell the 
film in the Film Market, before the Corporation can 
permit himfher to sell the film- 

10. The import of films shall be permitted on out¬ 
right purchase-basis. No import shall be allowed on 
prefir-sharing or on any other basis. Import of addi¬ 
tional prints of films authorised to be imported under 
these Guidelines shall also not subsequently be 
allowed. 

11. The import of films by private Indian parties 
shall continue to be canalised through the Corpora¬ 
tion which shall be paid canalisation fee at the rate 
of Rs. 50,000|- (Rupees fifty thousand only) per film. 

** 

12. The private Indian parties shall make their own 
arrangements for distribution of the imported films 
in India. 

13. The private Indian party permitted to import 
of film shall, within seven days of the first release of 
the film in India, inform the Corporation about the 
date of the first release along with particulars of the 
name of theatre where the film has been released and 
the name of the city, town, etc. and the State where 
the theatre exists. 

14. A private Indian party desirous of importing a 
film under these Guidelines shall enter into negotia¬ 
tions with a foreign party and get hislher contract 
registered with the Corporation in the form prescribed 
by the Corporation, as soon as the contract is en¬ 
tered into, but not later than 24th January, 1987. 

The private Indian party getting his|her contract 
registered with the Corporation will be required to 
pay to the Corporation, at the time of registration of 
the contract, an adjustable . advance of Rs. 5,000 
(Rupees five thousand only) per film. This advance 
will be adjusted against the canalisation fee of 
Rs. 50,0001- payable by the Indian party for import 
of hlslher film. Tn case the private Indian Party does 
not import the film after its clearance within the period 
laid down in guideline 20, the amount is liable to be 
forfeited by the Corporation. Tn a case where a film 
Is not cleard for Import, the advance shall be refund-
