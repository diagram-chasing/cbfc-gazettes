THE INSTITUTE OF COST AND WORKS 
ACCOUNTANTS OF INDIA 

Cost Accountants 
Calcutta-16, the 8th December 1965 

No. 39-CWA (24)/65- — Tn exercise of the powers con- 
ferred by Sub-section (l) of Section 39 of the Cost and 
Works Accountants Act, 1959, (Act No. 23 of 1959), 
the Council of the Institute of Cost and Works Account- 
ants of India has made the following amendments in the 
Cost and Works Accountants Regulations, 1959, the 
same having been previously published and approved by 
the Central Government as required by Sub-section (3) 
of the said Section. 

In the said Regulations : 

I. In clause (i) of sub-regulation (4) of Regulation 
32, for the words “Those who have passed a degree 
examination of any University in the subjects shown in 


Column II of the table below, shall be exempted from 
taking the paper shown in Column I the following 
words shall be substituted, namely : 

“Those who have passed a degree examination of 
any University in the subjects shown in Column II 
of the table below with at least two papers or a 
paper of at least 200 maximum marks in such sub- 
jects, shall be exempted from taking the paper 
shown in Column I 

II. In the third proviso to sub-regulation ( 1 ) of Regu- 
lation 33, after the words “to the effect that subsequent 
to his passing the Intermediate Examination he has 
undergone or is undergoing satisfactorily a course of 
postal or oral tuition for the Final Examination the 
following words shall be added, namely : 

"provided that in the case of a candidate who, 
whether before or after passing the Intermediate 
Examination, has undergone or is undergoing oral 
tuition for an integrated course of Intermediate and 
Final Examinations imparted by Government or 
Government-sponsored Institutions run on whole- 
time basis, due regard shall be had for such tuition 
for the purposes of the certificate above referred to; 
and”. 

III. In clause (ii) of sub-regulation (4) of Regulation 
35, after the words “Those who have passed a degree 
or post-graduate diploma examination of any University 
in the subject Statistics", the following words shall be 
added, namely : 

“with at least two papers or a paper of at least 
200 maximum marks, ”, 

IV. In sub-regulation (2) of Regulation 114, for the 
sentence “A person so co-opted shall hold office until 
the dissolution of the Regional Council", the following 
sentence shall be substituted, namely : 

“A member so co-opted shall hold office till the 
expiry of the duration of the said Regional Coun- 
cil.” 

V. In sub-regulation (2) and sub-regulation (3) of Re- 
gulation 120, for the sentence “A person so co-opted 
shall hold office until the dissolution of the said Regional 
Council”, the following sentence shall be substituted, 
namely : 

"A member so co-opted shall hold office till the 
expiry of the duration of the said Regional Coun- 
cil.” 

VI. In Regulation 120, after sub-regulation (3), the 
following new sub-regulation (4) shall be added, name- 
ly : 

“(4) If an elected or a co-opted member of a 
Regional Council changes his professional address 
from the regional constituency from which he was 
elecied or to which he belonged at the time of co- 
option, as the case may be, to any other constitu- 
ency, such member shall cease to be a member of 
the Regional Council and the vacancy so caused 
may be filled up by co-option of another member 
by the Regional Council concerned. A member so 
co-opted shall hold office till the expiry of the dura- 
tion of the said Regional Council.” 

S. N. GHOSE 
Secretary
