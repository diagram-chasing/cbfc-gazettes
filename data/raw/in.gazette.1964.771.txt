ed on the 6th September 1964 by way of additions and 
alterations made thereto as set out in the following resolu- 
tions of the Council ;

'in the absence of the Chairman of the Council’

B — New rules under Sec. 17(3) and rules in Part III 

Resolution No. 61/1964 

Resolved that the Rules of the Council in Part 111 be 
and are hereby amended by the addition of rules framed 
under Section 17(3) of the Act and other alterations us 
set out hereunder : — 

1. In Part HI, in lieu of the heading — 

“Rules under Sections 20, 21 and 49 read with other 
Sections of the Act”. 

Substitute — 

“Rules under Sections 16, 17, 18, 20, 21 and 49 
read with other Sections of the Act”, 

2. In Part HI, Chapter I, in lieu of existing rules 1 to 
3, substitute tho following : 

CHAPTER I 

A — Seniority in State Rolls 

1. Seniority of Vakils, Pleaders and Attorneys : The 
seniority of a Vakil, Pleader or Attorney who was enrolled 
as an Advocate immediately before the appointed day, 
or who is enrolled after that day, shall be determined 
according to the date of his enrolment as Advocate. 

2. Seniority of Senior Advocates of the Supreme 
Court : The Seniority of a Senior Advocate enrolled be- 
fore the appointed day shall be determined in accord- 
ance with the date of his enrolment as an Advocate. 

B — -Common Roll 

3. The Council shall prepare and maintain a common 
roll of Advocates which shall comprise the entries made in 
all the State Rolls and the names of all Advocates entitled 
as of right to practise in the Supreme Court immediately 
before the appointed day, but whose names are not entered 
in any State Roll. 

4. The Common Roll shall consist of two parts, the 
first part containing the names of Senior Advocates and 
the second part containing the names of other Advocates, 

5. The names of Senior Advocates on the State Rolls 
and of all Senior Advocates of the Supreme Court whose 
names are not entered on any State Roll shall be entered 
in Part I of the Common Roll. 

6. The seniority of a Senior Advocate enrolled before the 
appointed day shall be determined in accordance with the 
date of his enrolment as an Advocate. 

7. The names of Advocates entered in Part II of the 
State Rolls and the names of Advocates enrolled by the 
Supreme Court before the appointed day (not being Senior 
Advocates), whose names are not entered in any State 
Roll shall be entered in Part H of the Common Roll, 

8. The seniority of an Advocate whose name is entered 
in a State Roll shall be determined in accordance with the 
seniority in that roll. 

9. The seniority of an Advocate not being a Senior 
Advocate of the Supreme Court, enrolled by the Supreme 
Court before the appointed day, but whose name is not 


entered in any State Roll shall be determined in accordance 
with the date of his enrolment as an advocate of the 
Supreme Court. 

10. Subject to the provisions of rule 8, in the case of 
an Advocate whose name was entered on the rolls of more 
than one High Court, the date of enrolment in the High 
Court in which, he was first enrolled shall be taken as the 
date for determining the seniority notwithstanding the 
fact that his name was removed from that roll by reason ot 
his enrolment in another High Court. 

11. If the date of enrolment of two or more persons is 
the same, the seniority shall be determined by the dale 
of birth. 

12. All alterations and additions communicated to the 
Council under Section 19 by a State Council shall be 
carried out in the Common Roll from time to time. 

13. Disputes as to Seniority : If any dispute arises, with 
respect to the seniority of any person in the Common Roll, 
it shall be referred to the Council and its decision shall 
be final. 

C — Transfer under Section 18 

14. Any person whose name is entered on any State 
Roll may make an application to the Council for transfer 
in the form prescribed for the transfer of his name from 
that Roll to the Roll of any other State Council. The 
application shall be accompanied by (i) a certified copy 
of the entry in the State Roll relating to the applicant and 
(ii) a certificate from the State; Bar Council stating that 
his Certificate of Enrolment has not been recalled and 
that the applicant is entitled to practise on the date of bis 
application. The Council may order the transfer after an 
enquiry.

“I request a transfer of my name to the roll of 
the Bar Council of ” 

substitute the words, 

“I request a transfer of my name to the toll of 
the Bar Council of. . . . for the reasons set out 
by me at the end of the form hereunder.” 

4. (a) In Part III, Chapter IV, the*, number T be 
inserted before the heading — 

“Disciplinary Committee of the Council"; 

(b) The number ‘2’ be inserted before the words, “The 
following procedure” 

(c) For clause (2) of rule 2 so numbered, beginning 
with the words, “The Disciplinary Committee" and ending 
with the words, “fix a date for hearing of the case”. 

Substitute — 

“If the Disciplinary Committee does not summarily 
reject the complaint, and in all cases referred to 
it by the Council suo moto, a date shall be fixed 
for the hearing of the case as provided for in 
these rules.” 

(d) In clause (5) of rule 2 so numbered, In lieu of the 
words, 

“all relevant documents”, substitute the words— 

“such other documents as the President of the Com- 
mittee may direct”.

Electoral Roll

Roll shall not be entered on the Electoral Roll if, on the

authority suspending him from practice; 

(b) he is an undischarged insolvent;

an Election Tribunal, provided however that such 
disqualification shall not operate beyond the

E— NEW RULES UNDER SEC. 49(i) — PART VI 
Resolution No. 64/ 1964 

Resolved that the Rules of the Council be and are 
hereby amended by the addition of the following rules 
framed under Section 49(i) of the Act as Part VI of the 
Rules, 

Part VI 

Rules under Sec. 49 (i) of the Act 

General principles for the guidance of the State Councils 
and supervision and control by the Council 

General Principles : 

Elections — • 

1. Election of members to State Councils shall be by 
secret ballot and not by postal ballot. 

2. Election of members shall be published in the State 
Gazette. 

3. It is desirable to fix a deposit for every candidate 
standing for election which is liable to forfeiture in case 
the candidate is unable to secure at least l/8th of the 
quota fixed for election. 

4. The retirement of elected members of State Coun- 
cils by rotation shall be by casting lots. 

5. All .election disputes shall be decided by Tribunals 
constituted by State Councils. 

Funds - — 

6. Funds of the State Councils must first be deposited 
in the State Bank of India or other scheduled bank before 
any money could be- — expended and disbursements shall 
ordinarily be made through cheques unless the amount 
involved is small. 

7. The funds should be invested in Government or 
other securities specified in Section 20 of the Trusts Act. 

8. They shall also maintain a Provident Fund for their 
employees. 

9. State Councils shall be at liberty to accept donations 
in cash or kind for any of the purposes of the Act. 

10. Accounts shall be audited by a Chartered Accoun- 
tant once a year. 

Books and Registers — 

11. Every State Council shall maintain the following 
books as a minimum requirement : 

(a) Minutes Book; 

(b) Attendance Register for the staff; 

(c) Leave Register for the staff; 

(d) Acquittance Register; 

(e) Day Book and Ledger; 

(f) Receipt Books; 

(g) Financial Assistance Register; 

(h) Provident Fund Account; and 

(i) Property Register. 

Staff — 

1 2. The State Councils shall maintain the following staff 
for their minimum requirements ; 

(a) Secretary; 

(b) Accountant-cum-Mannger; 

(c) Stenographer; 

(d) Clerk; 

(e) a Peon or attender. 

Enrolment — 

13. Every candidate seeking enrolment as an advocate 
shall solemnly affirm and subscribe to the following dec- 
larations ; 

(a) ‘I shall uphold the Constitution and the Laws;’ 

(b) ‘I shall faithfully discharge every obligation cast 
on me by the Act and the Rules.’ 

14. Every person applying to bo enrolled as an Advo- 
cate under the Act shall be bound to state the truth in 
furnishing information to the State Council concerned 
touching the matter of his enrolment. 

Supervision and Control — 

15. The Council may call for periodical returns, sta- 
tistics, or information whenever found necessary.
