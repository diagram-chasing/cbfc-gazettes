NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 28th March, 2011 

S.O. 666(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred 
by sab-section (i) of Section 3 of the Cinematograph Act, 
1952 (37 of 1952) read with rule 3 of the Cinematograph 
(Certification) Rules, 1983, the Central Government is 
pleased to appoint Ms. Leela Samson as Chairperson of 
the Central Board of Film Certification in an honorary 
capacity from 1 st April, 2011 for a period of three years or 
until further orders, whichever is earlier.
