IMPERIAL BANK OF INDIA 


NOTICE 

Bombay, the 17 th May 1955 

Notice is hereby given that the Principal Register and 
the Branch Registers of the Imperial Bank of India will 
be closed for transfer of shares from Thursday, the 16th 
June 1955 to Thursday, the 30th June 1955, both days in- 
clusive. 

In terms of Section 6(l)(a) of the State Bank of India 
Act, 1955, all shares in the capital of the Imperial Bank of 
India shall be transferred to, and shall vest In, the Re- 
serve Bank of India, free of all trusts, liabilities and 
encumbrances on the “appointed day” which has been 
fixed for the 1st July 1955 by the Government of India 
Notification No. F. B(8)-F.I./55, dated the 14th May 1955. 
Section 9(1) ibid provides that every person who imme- 
diately before the 1st July 1955 is registered as a holder 
of shares in the Imperial Bank of India shall be entitled 
to compensation in accordance with the provisions con- 
tained in the First Schedule. 

All purchasers of Imperial Bank of India shares are 
therefore advised to lodge with the Bank for registration, 
instruments of transfer in their favour along with the 
relative share certificates, not later than the 15 th June 
1955. 

S. K. HANDOO 
Managing Director
