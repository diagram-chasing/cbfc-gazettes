Government of India in the Ministry of Petroleum and 
Natural Gas No. S.O. 2685 dated the 13th October, 1990 
issued under sub-section (1) of section 3 of the Petroleum 
and Minerals Pipelines (Acquisition of Right of User in

said Act), the Central Government declared its intention to 
acquire the right of user in the lands specified in the Schedule 
appended to that notification for the purposes of laying 
pipe line for the transport of petroleum :

sub-section (i) of Section 6 of the said Act has made his 
report to the Central Government.

the said report is satisfied that the right of user in the lands

should be acquired.

sub-section (1) of section 6 of the said Act, the Central 
Govt, hereby declares that the right of user in the land 
specified In the Schedule appended to this notification are 
hereby acquired.

section (4) of the said Section, the Central Government 
hereby directs that tho right of user in the said lands shall 
instead of vesting in the Central Government, vest, free 
from all encumbrances, in the Indian Oil Corporation
