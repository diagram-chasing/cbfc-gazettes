Definitions : 

1. In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires,— 

(a) “Board” means the Central Board of Film Certification; 

(b) “Directorate” means the Directorate of Film Festivals, National Film Development Corporation Limited; 

(c) “Festival” means the 33rd National Film Festival, 1986; and 

(d) “Producer” includes an individual producer, a Production Company, a Government Department or a 
Government recognised Institute. 

Organisers : 

2. The festival is being organised by the Directorate of Film Festival (hereinafter referred to as the 
“Directorate”), National Film Development Corporation Ltd. (a Government of India undertaking), on behalf 
of the Government of India. 

Aims: 

3. The Festival aims at encouraging the production of films of aesthetic excellence and social relevance 
contributing to the understanding and appreciation of the film cultures of different regions and promoting the 
integration and unity of the nation. 

Sections : 

4. The Festival will have the following competitive sections : 

(i) Feature fims; and 

(ii) Non-Feature films.

Eligibility : 

10. Only those persons whose names are on the credit titles of the films shall be eligible for the awards. 

11. The following films shall be eligible for entry into the two competition sections : 

(a) A film produced in India, which was certified by the Board between 1st January, 1985 and 31st December, 
1985 (both days inclusive), except a film which had been entered for an award in the 32nd National Film 
Festival of India, 1985. 

(b) A film produced in 1985 either by Doordarshan or by others, provided it has been accepted for telecast 
by Doordarshan, with or without certification by the Board. 

(c) A film produced in 1985 by a film institute recognised by the Government of India with or without certi¬ 
fication by the Board. 

12. Notwithstanding anything contained in regulation 5, remake of a film that has already won an award in 
a particular category shall not be eligible for entry in the festival in that category. Furthermore, dubbed version 
of a film or revised version of a film shall also not be eligible) for entry in the Festival. 

13. (1) Entries for feature films section shall be in a guage of 35 mm or 16 mm only. 

(2) Entries for non-feature films section shall be in a guage of 35 mm or 16 mm or can also be in the form of 
video tapes on 1/2" or 3/4" (Pal/U-Matic). 

Last date for receipt of entry : 

14. The last date for receipt of entry in the Directorate is 24th February, 1986. 

Procedure for entry of film : 

15. (1) A film shall be entered only by its producer. 

(2) A film can be entered for all the awards or for one, or more awards in a section. 

16. The producer should submit his/her entry in Form ‘EF-I’, in duplicate. The form must be complete 
in all respects and should be accompanied by all the material mentioned in regulation 17, failing which the entry is 
liable to be rojected by the Directorate. 

17. (1) Along with the entry form the following shall also be furnished by the producer : 

(a) an undertaking in Form UF-I 

(b) An undertaking in Form UF-II 

(c) A non-refundable entry fee of Rs. 100 per film, in the form of a crossedj Demand] Draft in favour of 
“NFDC Ltd.” payable at the Indian Bank, New Delhi. 

(2) In tho case of a feature film, the following shall also bo furnished along with the entry form : 

(a) Twenty-five copies of tho synopsis in English; and 

(b) short biographical sketches of the producer, director, screenplay writer, loading and supporting artists 
cameraman, audiographer, editor, art director, costume designor, lyricit, music director and playback 
singers along with two photographs each. 

Provided that if a him is being entered for specific award(s), biographical sketches and photographs 
should relato to the persons who are to be considered for the award(s) for which entry has been made. 

(c) Twenty five copies of Lyrics each in original language alongwith twentyfive copies of its English Trans¬ 
lation if the producer desires his film to be considered for the Best Lyric Award. 

(3) In the case of non-feature film, the following shall be furnished along with the entry form : 

(a) Fifteen copies of the text of the commentary in tho original language along with fifteen copios of its 
translation in English; and 

(b) Short biographical sketches of tho producer, director, animator (in tho case of animation films), and 
Cameraman (in tho case of news film) along with two photographs each.

Procedure for selection : 

19. (1) Awards in the competition sections will bo decidod by two juries, one for featuro films and the other

(2) The Jury for feature films will be composed of a Chairman and not more than 9 members distinguished 
in the field of cinema, other allied arts and humanities who are qualified to judge the artistic and technical merits of 
feature films. 

(3) The Jury for non-feature films will be composed of a Chairman and not more than 4 members distinguished 
in the field of cinema and humamities and qualified to judge the artistic and technical merits of specialised non- 
feature films. 

(4) While considering the films entered for the awards at (v) and (vii) of regulation 5, the Chairman of the 
Feature Film Jury shall associate a person nominated by the Department of Family Welfare and Ministry of Welfare, 
respectively, as an expert. 

20. (1) The Chairman of the Feature Film Jury, at his discretion, may constitute panels from amongst 
the members of the Jury. 

(2) The Chairman of the feature film jury and non-feature film Jury may seek advice of experts in specialised

NATIONAL AWARD FOR THE BEST WRITING ON CINEMA 1986 

REGULATIONS 


Organisers : 

1. The Awards will be organised by the Directorate of Film Festivals (hereinafter referred to as the 
"Directorate”), National Film Development Corporation Limited (a Govt, of India undertaking) on behalf of the 
Government of India. 

Aims : 

2. To encourage study and appreciation of cinema as an art form and dissemination of information and 
critical appreciation of this art form through publication of books, articles, reviews etc. 

Awards : 

3. The following award s will be given : 

(a) Best Book on Cinema—Rajat Kamal and a cash prize of Rs. 5,000 to the author of the book. In case 
of joint authors the cash prize will be equally divided among the recipients. 

(b) Best Film journalist—Rajat Kamal and a cash prize of Rs. 5,000. 

Eligibility : 

4. (1) Any book on cinema published iu India in any language between 1st January and 31st December, 
1985 (both days inclusive) is eligible for the award for “Best Book on Cinema”. 

(2) Translations, anthologies, abridgments and edited or annotated works are not eligible. 

5. The award for “Bost Film Journalist” will be given to the best film journalist of the year 1985 for critical 
studies, reviews or articles published in Indian newspapers, magazines and journals during the year in any language. 
Last Date for receipt of entries : 

6. The last date for receipt of entries for both the awards is 24th February, 1986. 

Procedure for entry : 

7. Entry can be submitted in the caso of award for Best Book on Cinema, by its author or publisher and 
in the case of award for Best Film Journalist by the journalist, himself or by the publisher/cbitor of the newspaper/ 
magazine/journal.
