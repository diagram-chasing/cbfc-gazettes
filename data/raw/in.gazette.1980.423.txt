No. l-CA(118)/80.— The following draft of certain amend-

sub-sections (1) and (3) of Section 30 of the Chartered 
Accountants Act, 1949 (Act XXXVIII of 1949), is published 
for information of all persons likely to be affected thereby and 
notice is hereby given that the draft will be taken up for 
consideration on or after 10th May, 1980. 

Any objection or suggestion which may be received from 
any person with respect to the said draft before the date 
specified will be considered by the Council of the Institute of 
Chartered Accountants of India, New Delhi. 

In the said Regulations : 

i. At the end of Regulation 32B, add the following 
sub-regulation (4) :■ — 


“(4) Every articled clerk shall, before entering into service 
as an articled clerk, open an account in his own 
name with a branch of the bank to be specified by 
the member engaging him, and the stipend payable 
to the articled clerk, in accordance with sub-regula- 
lion (2) or (3) hereof; as the case may be, shall bo 
deposited by the member every month in such 
account. Notification of any change in the bank 
account shall be sent by the member to the Institute 
in the appropriate Form within 30 days of the 
change."

clause (b) and before clause (c), the following 
clause be inserted 

“(bb) That he will pay to the articled clerk a minimum
