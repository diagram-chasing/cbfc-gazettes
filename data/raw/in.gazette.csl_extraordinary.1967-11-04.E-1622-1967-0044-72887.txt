the acknowledgement receipts, Ext, B and B / 2 along with which the 2 charge 
sheets were sent by registered post . Nabin Patra said that as he was ill at The 
time he did not peruse the contents of those covers and that subsequently when 
he looked at his papers he could not find any chargesheet . He said that he 
received the notice of enquiry in respect of the second chargesheet, the notice 
of enquiry being Ext. , but that he did not attend the enquiry fixed for 29th 
January , 1985, at Bhanora because he did not receive the chargesheet. Since 
however Nabin Patra signed the acknowledgement receipts, Ext . B and B2, it 
must be held that he had received the two chargesheets ; clearly he had omitted 
to reply to them as he was trying to avoid the transfer to Bhanora, Ext. Dis 
the copy of the second chargesheet dated 11th January , 1965 , containing the 
recommendation of the manager for dismissal on the basis of the report of the 
enquiring officer and the order of the C . M . E . approving of the order of dismissal. 
In view of all this evidence , oral and documentary , the order of dismissal might 
be held to be yalid if in the meantime the manager of Bejdih had not 
treated Nabin Patra as still employed at Bejdih colliery and so waived the. 
transfer order . In view of this conduct of the manager of Bejdih 
Nabin Patra might not unreasonably conclude that the order of transfer was no 
longer in force. That being so, it must follow that the order of dismissal by the 
manager of Bhanora was not justified in the circumstances, 

7, Shri S . N . Banerjee for the union has sought to justify the conduct of Nabin 
Chandra Patra in not availing of the chance given to him to join at Bhanora by 
12th April, 1965 , stating that Nabin Patra was entitled to suitble accommodation 
at Bhanora before he acted on the order of transfer. In such a caşe , even 
though a workman may claim to be provided with suitable accommodation he 
should first obey the order of transfer . It is not however necessary to consider 
this point in detail, because we are not concerned in this case with the non 
compliance with the order to join at Bhanora by 12th April, 1965 . The order of 
dismissal, dated 8th February , 1985 , passed by the manager of Bhanora colliery 
dismissing Nabin Patra having been held to be unjustified because at the time 
when the proceedings were going on Nabin Patra was being tacitly treated by the 
management as still employed at Bejdih colliery, there must be an order of 
reinstatement; but in view of the conduct of Nabin Chandra Patra I do not think 
that he is entitled to any pay for the period of non - employment. Although at 
the time when he received the order of transfer Nabin Chandra Patra was 111 
it does not appear that the illness was very serious. Moreover there was un 
conscionable delay in raising the dispute before the Conciliation Omcer . 

8 . My award therefore is that the transfer of Shri Nabin Chandra Patra, 
Lamp. Issue clerk , from Bejdih colliery to Bhanora colliery as ordered on 13th 
November, 1994 was justified ; but the subsequent dismissal with effect from 8thi
