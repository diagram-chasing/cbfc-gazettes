Complaint No. 1 of 1964 
The Tinplate Co . of India Ltd , Calcutta - Complainant. 

vs 
The Union of India owning and representing the South 
Eastern Railway , Calcutta - - Respondent. 

Whereas the complainant above named has made a 
complaint under Section 41 ( 1 ) ( a ) and ( c ) of the In 
dian Railways Act, that have a siding known as Tin 
plate Siding , taking off from the Tata Industrial Siding 
at Tatanagar on the South Eastern Railway, that the said 
respondent railway has notified through their Special 
Foreign Rates Circular No . 2 ( A ) of 1960 that a haulage 
charge of Rs. 9 .56 DP . per wagon is leviable in respect 
of goods traffic booked to and from the Tinplate Siding 
und moving over the Tata Industrial Siding at Tatanagar 
and in that said circular it has been specially mentioned 
that the additional charge of Rs. 2 .53 per wagon notified 
therein , will not be leviable in the case of traffic booked 
to and from the Tinplate Company of India , that subse 
quently the respondent South Eastern Railway adminis 
tration by their telegram No . RG -46 / 268 dated 9 - 5 - 1963 
has ordered the levy of additional siding charge of 
Rs. 2 .53 in respect of traffic booked to and from Tin 
plate Siding also , that the said additional levy of 
Rs. 2 .53 over and above the aforesaid haulage charge 


of Rs. 9. 56 is illegal, unreasonable and without any 
authority agd has also resulted in prejudice contraven 
ing Section 28 of the Indian Railways Act, and has 
prayed for orders directing the said respondent railway 
administration to stop the levy of the additional charge 
of Rs. 2 .53 per wagon over and above the haulage 
charge of Rs. 9 . 56 per Wagon and to refund the said 
additional charge levied on and from 9 -5 - 1963 with 
costs ; 

And whereas it is thought that there may be persons 
who are not on record but have the same interest in the 
proceedings as the complainant or the respondent above 
named ; 

This public notice is, therefore, given under Rule 
19 ( 3 ) and ( 4 ) of the Railway Rates Tribunal Rules 
1959 , so that any person who so desires may pctition 
the Tribunal within 30 days of the publication of this 
notice for leave to intervene in support of or opposition 
to the relief sought in the complaint or be added on 
the side of the complainant or the respondent, setting 
forth the grounds of the proposed intervention and the 
position and the interest of the petitioner in the pro 
cecdings or the grounds for being added as a party in 
the said casc . Any decision given by the Tribunal after 
this Public Notice shall apply to all such persons .
