‘On Account’ payments of Rs. 2,58,000 and Rs. 1.50,000 
have been made during the year to the Ahmedabad Tex-

and the Cotton Textile Export Promotion Council, 
Bombay, respectively towards grants-in-aid and, accord- 
ing to the conditions of the grant, these are subject to 
finalisation on the basis of the actual audited expenditure 
for the year. The audited accounts of both the institutions 
have since been received and are stated to be under 
scrutiny by the Cotton Textiles Fund Committee. A fur- 
ther ‘On Account’ payment of Rs. 2,00.000 has been made 
to the ATIRA on this account as grant-in-aid for the year 
1954-55, on 9th June 1955 at the specific instance of the 
Govt, of India. Early action may be taken to arrive at the 
net amounts due to the Institutions as grants-in-aid for 
the year 1954-55 and to finalise the ‘On Account’ pay- 
ments,
