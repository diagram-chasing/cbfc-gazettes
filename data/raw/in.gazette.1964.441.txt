No. M-Pres./M. — In supersession of this Secretariat Noti- 
cation No. 19-Pres./52, dated the 1st February, 1952, and all 
other subsequent Notifications on the subject, the Ordinances 
relating to the "TERRITORIAL ARMY DECORATION” and 
the “TERRITORIAL ARMY MEDAL" are republished for 
general information. 

The President is pleased to Institute two awards to be 
designated the “TERRITORIAL ARMY DECORATION" and 
the ^TERRITORIAL ARMY MEDAL" and to make, ordain 
and establish the following Ordinances which shall be deemed 
to have effect from the fifteenth day of August in the year 
One Thousand Nine Hundred and Fifty One. 

Territorial Army Decoration 

Firstly. — The decoration shall be styled and designated the 
“TERRITORIAL ARMY DECORATION". 

Secondly. — The decoration shall be oval In shape, made of 
silver. On the obverse it shall have a lotus wreath along the 
rim, a five-pointed star in gold silt embossed in the centre 
and tho State Emblem in gold gilt resting on the upper point 
of the star. On the reverse it shall have a lotus flower with 
buds and leave* embossed in the centre and tho words 
*r<TT % faytT ” in the form of an arch above it. 

Thirdly. — The decoration shall be suspended from a silver 
bar brooch, with the word "TERRITORIAL” embossed 
thereon, by a silk ribbon one and a quarter inches in width. 
Tliff ribbon shall be divided into 5 stripes by 4 white vertical 
lines. The stripe in the centre shall be orange and the other 
four stripes blue. The width of the orange stripe shall be 
double that of a blue stripe. The orange colour denotes 
renunciation and sacrifice, blue devotion and white purity. 

Fourthly. — The decoration shall be conferred by the 
President. 

Fifthly. — The decoration shall be an award to commissioned 
officers of the Territorial Army for 20 year* meritorious 
service of proved capacity. The qualifying service shall be 
reckoned in accordance with the conditions hereinafter 
mentioned. 

Sixthly. — The following service shall count as two-fold 
qualifying service : — 

(a) The period of commissioned service rendered in any 
capacity in a theatre of war during World War II 
by an officer of the late Indian Territorial Force or 
the late Auxiliary Force (India) serving on the 3rd 
September, 1939. 

(b) Embodied commissioned service in India during 
World War n in the case of an officer of the late 
Indian Territorial Force or the late Auxiliary Force 
(India) serving on the 23rd September, 1939. 

Seventhly. — The following service shall count as single 
qualifying service : — 

(a) Commissioned service in the Indian Territorial 
Force; on the active list of the Auxiliary Force 
(India); in the Indian Army Reserve of Officers 
during the period 3rd September, 1939 to 2nd 
September, 1945 (only the actual period of Army 
service will count); and in the Regular Indian Army, 
the Indian Navy, or the Indian Air Force during 
the period 3rd September, 1939 to 2nd September, 
1941, provided that the officer was commissioned 
before or between these dates and subsequently 
obtained a commission in the Territorial Army. 

(b) Service rendered in ranks below commissioned ranks 
in a theatre of war during World War II. 


(c) Embodied service In India in the ranks of the late 
Indian Territorial Force or Auxiliary Force (India) 
during World War II. 

Eighthly. — Any period during which an officer of the 
Territorial Army may have served on the active list — as a 
Junior Grade Officer of the late Indian Territorial Force or 
in the ranks of the late Indian Territorial Force or the 
late Auxiliary Force in India, and during the period 3rd 
September, 1939 to 2nd September, 1945 as a Junior Com- 
missioned Officer or equivalent rank and/or in the ranks of 
the Regular Indian Army, the Indian Navy or the Indian Air 
Force — shall count as half qualifying service. 

Ninthly. — No period of service in the force in which an 
officer qualified for the Efficiency Decoration shall be reckon- 
ed as qualifying service for the Territorial Army Decoration. 

Tenthly. —Service requisite to qualify for the decoration 
shall not necessarily be continuous service. 

Eleventhly. — An officer already in possession of the Effi- 
ciency Decoration or any long service and good conduct or 
efficiency medal and clasp shall be eligible to receive the 
Territorial Army Decoration and to wear both, provided that 
he has completed the full periods of qualifying service for 
both awards and that no qualifying service towards one 
award is permitted to count towards the other. 

Twelfthly. —The names of those officers upon whom the 
decoration may be conferred shall be published In the 
Gazette of India and a Register thereof kept. 

Thirteenthly. — The miniature decoration which may be 
worn on certain occasions by those to whom the decoration 
is awarded shall be half the size of the decoration and a 
sealed pattern of the said miniature decoration shall be 
deposited and kept. 

Lastly. — The President may cancel and annul the award 
of the decoration to any person, and thereupon his name shall 
be crazed from the Register and he shall be required to 
surrender the decoration; but it shall be competent for the 
President to restore the decoration when such cancellation 
and annulment has subsequently been withdrawn. Notice of 
cancellation or restoration in every case shall be published in 
the Gdzette of India. 

Territorial Army Medal 

Firstly. — The medal shall be styled and designated the 
“TERRITORIAL ARMY MEDAL”. 

Secondly. — The medal shall be oval In shape, made of 
silver, with a fixed fitting for the ribbon In the form of two 
leaves and a scroll on which shall be embossed the word 
"TERRITORIAL”. On the obverse It shall have embossed 
the State Emblem and on the reverse the Inscription 
flTT % The award of a clasp to tho medal shall 

be denoted by a bar with the Ashoka Chakra embossed in 
the centre to be worn on the ribbon of the medal. 

Thirdly. — The medal shall be suspended by a ribbon of 
dark blue silk, one and a quarter Inches In width. It shall 
be divided into six equal stripes by five vertical lines. The 
central vertical line shall be orange in colour and the remain- 
ing four lines shall be white. The orange colour denotes 
renunciation and sacrifice, blue denotes devotion and white 
purity. 

Fourthly — -The medal shall be awarded bv the President 
to Junior Commissioned Officers, Non-Commissioned Officers 
and men of the Territorial Army for 12 years efficient service 
with a minimum of 12 trainings and who are recommended. 

Fifthly. — A clasp, to be worn on the ribbon of the madal, 
shall be awarded to those who having been awarded the 
medal complete a total of 18 years efficient service and a 
further clasp shall be awarded after completing 24 years 
service with a minimum of 18 and 24 trainings resnectively. 
When the ribbon is worn without the medal, the grant of 
clasns shall be denoted by the wearing on the ribbon of 
small silver rosettes, one or more according to the number 
of clasps awarded. 

.Sixthly.-— The qualifying service shall be reckoned in accor- 
dance with the conditions hereinafter mentioned, provided 
none of the service has been previously counted as qualifying 
for the grant of any long service, good conduct or efficiency 
medals or clasps. 

Seventhly. — The following service shall count as two-fold 
qualifying service ; — 

fa) The period of service rendered In anv capacity In a 
theatre of war during World War II by a member 
of the late Indian Territorial Force or Auxiliar

Eleventhly,— Qualifying service must have been rendered 
continuously except : — 

(a) during the period 3rd September, 1939 to 2nd 
September, 1945, or 

(b) when in different authorised auxiliary Army, Naval, 
or Air Forces not in the same portion of the Indian 
Republic provided that the break does not exceed 
twelve months, or 

(c) when in the late Indian Territorial Force/ Auxiliary 
Force (India) provided the break is due to no fault 
of the individual and that the break does not 
exceed six months. 

Twelfthly. — The following service although inadmissible as 
qualifying service save in the circumstances described in 
clause eighthly, sub-clause (a) (iii), shall not be considered 
as a break in the qualifying service : — 

(a) Service in the Regular Indian Army, the Indian 
Navy, the Indian Air Force or the Reserves of such 
forces. 

(b) Intervals between' service in the Regular Indian 
Army, the Indian Navy, the Indian Air Force or the 
Indian Territorial Force during the period 3rd 
September, 1939 to 2nd September, 1945. 

(c) Service of members of the Territorial Army whilst 
released from military service for the purpose of 
being employed on work of national importance, 
which wifi be defined as occasion arises, during the 
period of embodiment. 

(d) Service in the Auxiliary Force (India), or Auxiliary 
Force (India) Reserve, except as specified in sub- 
clause Ta) (ii) of clause eighthly , 

(e) Periods of desertion or absence without leave of 
members of the Territorial Army during embodiment 
or training either in camps or barracks, provided that 
they continue to serve after their offence has been 
dealt with. 


Lastly. — The President may cancel and annul the award 
of the medal to any person, and thereupon his name shall be 
erazod from the Register and he shall be required to surrender 
the medal; but it shall be competent for the President to 
lestore the medal when the cancellation and annulment has 
subsequently been withdrawn. Notice of cancellation or 
restoration in every case shall be published in the Gazette of 
India.
