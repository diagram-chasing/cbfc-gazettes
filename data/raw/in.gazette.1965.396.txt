by Section 45 of the Air Corporations Act, 1 95 3 (27 of

ployees’ Provident Fund (Amendment) Regulations. 
1965.

lations, 1954 (hereinafter referred to as the said Regu- 
lations), after sub-regulation (3) of Regulation 12, the 
following sub-regulations shall be inserted, namely : — 

“(4) A retired Government servant employed by the 
Corporation may also be permitted to become a 
member of the Fund from the date of his employ- 
ment by the Corporation or from the date he ceases
