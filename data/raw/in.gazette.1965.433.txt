No. l7(26)/65-Reg, — In exercise of the powers con- 
ferred by Section 9 read with clauses (b) and (c) of Sub-

Commission Act, 1959 (43 of 1959), in order to provide 
for the procedure in regard to the transaction of business 
at its meetings including quorum necessary therefor and 
the time and place of such meetings, the Oil and Natural 
Gas Commission hereby makes, with the previous ap- 
proval of the Central Government the following regula- 
tions namely : — 

1. (a) These regulations may be called the Oil and 

Natural Gas Commission, Transaction of Busi- 
ness) Regulations, 1965. 

(b) They shall come into force with effect from 5th 
August 1965. 

Definition r 

2. In these regulations, unless context otherwise 
requires : — 

(a) “Act” means the Oil & Natural Gas Commis- 
sion Act, 1959. 

(b) “Commission” means the Oil and Natural Gas 
Commission established under the Act. 

(c) “Government” means the Central Government. 

(d) "Secretary” means the Secretary to the Com- 
mission for the time being. 

(c) “Chairman” means the Chairman of the Com- 
mission for the time being. 

(f) “Member" means a Member of the Commis- 
sion duly appointed by the Government for 
the time being. 

(g) “Committee” means a Standing Committee or 
any other Committee of the Commission. 

Procedure to be followed at the Meeting of the Commis- 
sion 

3. (a) The Commission shall ordinarily meet once in 

every two months either at Dehra Dun or at 
Delhi. 

(b) The time, place and date of the meeting shall 
be notified by the Secretary with the prior 
approval of the Chairman. 

(c) In the absence of the Chairman any Member 
chosen by the Members from amongst them- 
selves shall preside at a meeting of the Com- 
mission. 

(d) The quorum to constitute a meeting of the 
Commission shall be one-third of the total 
number of members of the Commission for 
the time being or two such members which- 
ever is higher. 

Agenda and Notes 

4. (a) The Agenda and Notes for every meeting shall 

be prepared by the Secretary in consultation 
with the Chairman and in the manner prescrib- 
ed by the Commission from time to time, 

(b) The Agenda and Notes for every meeting shall 
ordinarily be circulated to the Chairman and 
Members at least 7 days before the date of the 
meeting. 

(c) Any item which is not included in the Agenda 
already circulated shall not be taken up for 
consideration at a Meeting of the Commission.
