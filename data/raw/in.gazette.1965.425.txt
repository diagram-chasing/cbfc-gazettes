Board. of the powers conferred by Sec- 
tion 21 of the Unit Trust of India Act, 1963, the 
Unit Scheme 1964, as made by the Board at its meeting 
held on the 12th May 1964 be and is hereby amended 
as follow's : 

(1) Add to clause 8 of the Unit Scheme, 1964, a 
new sub-clausc ( 5 ) as follows ; 

“(5) Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in 
sub-clause f2), (3) and (4), when the Trust is 
satisfied that, in the interests of the Trust and of 
the unit-holders, it is necessary or expedient to do 
so, it may vary the sale price or the repurchase 
price or both, as arrived at under sub-clause (2) or, 
as the case may be, sub-clause (3), to such extent 
as it may deem fit, having due regard to the re- 
sources which are, or may become, available to it 
for this purpose.”

1964, for paragraph (b ) , the following shall he substi- 
tuted : 

“(b) The Trust shall not be under an obligation to 
sell or repurchase units 

(i) on such days as are not working days; 
and 

(ii) during the period when the register of 
unit-holders is closed in connection with 
(as notified by the Trust) the annual clos- 
ing of the books and accounts.” ”
