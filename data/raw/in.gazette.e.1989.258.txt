NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 19th July, 1989 

No. 801[ 11 [89-FC.—In terms of powers conferred by proviso to Rule 3 (2) 
of the Cinematograph Act, 1952 (37 of 1952) and in continuation of the 
notifications of even number dated February 17, 1989 and May 18, 1989, the 
term of Office of Prof. M. R. Vanamali as Acting Chairman of Central Board 
of Film Certification is hereby extended by a further period of 3 months, i.e. 
upto October 20, 1989 (AN).
