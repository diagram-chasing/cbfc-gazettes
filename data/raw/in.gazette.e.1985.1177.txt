New Delhi, the 4th June, 1985 
NOTIFICATION 

S.O. 444(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub¬ 
section (1) of section 3 of the Cinematograph Act, 1952 read 
with rule 6 of the Cinematograph, (Certification) Rules, 1983, 
the Central Government hereby appoints Kura. A. M. Nadkami 
to look after the current duties of the post of the Chairman, 
Central Board of Film Certification during the absence on leave 
of Shri Bikrarn Singh from 3-6-85 to 16-6-1985.
