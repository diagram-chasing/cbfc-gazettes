2 . After the written statements of the parties were received , the matter was 
Axed for hcaring but could not be heard because of the applications made on 
behalf of the workmen . The matter was adjourned like this at the request of the 
workmen no less than four times. At the time of the last adjournment, I had 
specifically mentioned that the adjournment was granted as a last chance and 
no further adjournment would be granted . When the matter came up for hearing

3 . The matter relates to the termination of services of a haulage / pump khalasi 
named Lal Bahadur Singh . It appears that this workman was transferred from 
one place of working in the same colliery to another in April 1964 and on his 
failure to carry out the transfer , his services were terminated . 


4 . The management have every right to transfer one worker from one place 
of duty to another and unless the transfer is not bona fide or is made to victi 
mise him or is a case of unfair labour practice , etc ., or unless it involves 
loss to him , the tribunal would not be entitled to interfere in the matter , 


5 . From the cvidence of the manager , it appears that the transfer of this work 
man was in normal course . The transfer has been in the same post and on the 
same emoluments and in the same colliery ; only the place of duty is changed 
The new place of duty is not far from the old place of duty . The manager has 
denied that it was a case of victimisation or that the transfer was as a result of 
trade union activities . I believe him . The transfer was therefore , justifica . 

6 . As the workman refused to carry out the transfer and took the matter to 
conciliation , some more time was granted for carrying out the transter and still 
as the transfer was not carried out, à chargeshect was scrved and an enquiry 
held and as a result of the enquiry the workman s services have been terminated 

7 . The workmen in the written statement have alleged that the enguiry was 
a farce and was in compete violation of principles of natural justice . These 
allegations are very vague. It is not shown as to what principles were violatec 
and how the enquiry was a farce . T have gone through the enquiry papers and 
I find that the enquiry was held in the presence of the workman who was giver

amined at least one witness . In any case , the facts about the refusal to carry out

3 ( 1 ) : The first thing I have to do is to examine the above statement of the
