2. The aforesaid regulations are hereby amended to the extent indicated below 

(I) For the existing regulation 7(2), the following shall be substituted namely:— 

7(2) A film in 16 mm, 35 mm or in a wider gauge made in any Indian language and certified by the Central Board of 
Film Certification as a feature film, featurette/short fiction irrespective of their length are eligible for feature film 
category. 

(II) For the existing regulation 7(3)(i) the following shall be substituted namely:— 

7(3) For the non-feature films section, only those films that are certified by the Central Board of Film Certification as 
Documentary/News reel/non-fiction irrespective of their length are eligible for non-feature film category 

(III) The existing regulation 7(3)(ii) shall be deleted.
