TENURE 


The tenure of members of the Committee will ordinarily be three years from the date of notification of the 
Committee provided that : 


1. Members of the Parliament , who are members of the Committee , shall cease to be Members of the Committee as 
soon as they cease to be Members of the Parliament . 


The ex - officio members of the Committee shall continue as members so long as they hold office by virtue of which 
they are members of the Committee and 


3. If a vacancy arises in the Committee due to resignation or death of a member , the appointee to that vacancy shall 
hold office for the residual period of the tenure of three years .
