MINISTRY OF INFORMATION & BROADCASTING 
NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 17th December, 1983 

S.O. 915(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sec¬ 
tion 9 of the Cinematograph Act, 1952 (37 of 1952) and in 
continuation of Ministry of Information and Broadcasting Noti¬ 
fication No. 806/21/83-F(C) dated 17-6-83 (published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) dated 17-6-83) the Central Government hereby extends the 
period of exemption from the provisions of sub-rule (1) of rule 
28 of the Cinematograph (Certification) Rules, 1983 relating to 
deposit of a print/video-copy of the film in respect of films certi¬ 
fied by the Central Board of Film Certification, for three months 
from 17-12-83, subject to the condition that the applicant shall 
deposit a shooting script of the film to the Central Board of 
Film Certification.
