Explanatory Note o/i the above Amendments 

The following is the explanatory note on the amend- 
ments proposed in Notification No. CWR(2)/69, dated 
the 8th May 1969. This note is intended only to clarify 
the intention of the Council underlying these amendments 
and should not be construed as limiting or amplifying the 
scope of these proposed amendments in any manner what- 
soever. 

T. Under the existing Regulation 5 an Associate who is 
not in practice as a Cost Accountant should for the pur- 
pose of advancement to Fellowship, have held a position 
as Chief Cost Accountant or an equivalent appointment 
in an organisation approved by the Council, It is now 
proposed to provide in addition for recognition of equiva- 
lent experience otherwise acquired. 

II, HI, VI & VII. Under the existing Regulations a 
candidate registered as a student on or after 1st January 
1965 is required to produce from the Director of Studies. 
Coaching Administration, a Certificate of Completion of 
postal or oral tuition in respect of Intermediate or Final 
Examination before he can he admitted to the Inter- 
mediate or Final Examination as the case may be. It is 


now proposal that if a candidate satisfactorily completes 
the postal or oral tuition relating to a Group or Groups 
of the Intermediate or Final Examination, h u will be issu- 
ed with Completion Certificates group-wise but. in their 
sequenciaJ order to enable him lo seek, admission in the 
examination in that Group or those Groups. 

IV, V, VIII & IX. According to the existing Regula- 
tions, the present syllabus relating to Intermediate and 
Final Examinations will cease to be operative after Janu- 
ary 1971 and will be replaced by Revised Syllabus from 
luly 1971 examinations. It is now proposed that (a) the 
present syllabus will be extended up to and inclusive of 
July 1973 examinations, (b) candidates registered as stu- 
dents up to 31st March 1970 will be eligible to take the 
Intermediate Examination under the present syllabus until 
July 1973 examination and if they fail, they will be re- 
quital to appear and pass the Intermediate Examination 
under the Revised Syllabus from January 1974, (c) candi- 
dates registered as students on or after 1st April 1970 
will be required to lake their Intermediate Examination 
under the Revised Syllabus from July 1971, (d) those 
who pass the Intermediate Examination prior to April 
1970 will be eligible to take the Final Examination under 
the present syllabus until July 1973 examination and if 
they fail, they will be required to appear and pass the 
Final Examination under the Revised Syllabus 'from 
January 1974, and (e) those who puss the Intermediate 
Examinaiion after April 1970 will be required to take 
their Final Examination under the Revised Syllabus.
