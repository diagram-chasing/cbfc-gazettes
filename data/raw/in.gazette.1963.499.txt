No. GM/61-26 — In exercise of the powers conferred by 
section 45 of the Air Corporations Act, 1953, Air-India, 
with the previous approval of the Central Government, 
.lereby amends the Air-lndia Employees’ Provident Fund 
Regulations, 1954, as follows, namely: — 

(1) The following two amendments shall be made in 
Regulation 2: — 

Firstly, clause (b) shall be deleted in its entirety and 
the following new clause (b) substituted therefor, 
remely : — 

“(b) Subject to the proviso hereto, 'Pay’ means basic 
pay and includes, where admissible. Command 
Pay, Efficiency Bonus, Navigator’s Licence Pay, 
R/T Allowance, Overseas Operations Allowance 
and Jet, 'llowance earned by an employee while 
on duty or on leave with pay in accordance with 
the terms of contract of employment, and any 
other item or items of emoluments which the 
Corporation may, with the approval of the 
Central Gevernment specifically direct to be so 
included in any particular case. 

Proviso —' The inclusion of R/T Allowance 
and Overseas Operations Allowance in 'Pay’ 
comes into effect from 1st January 1960 and 
that of Jet Allowance from 1st April 1961 in 
respect) of Pilots and from 1st April 1962 in 
respect of Navigators and Flight Engineers.' 1 

Secondly, the words “temporary or” occurring in 
clause (f) shall be deleted. 

(2) The following new sub-regulation (3) shall be added 
to Regulation 11: — 

“(3) While submitting the annual report to the 
Central Government as required under Regu- 
lation 24 the Board shall indicate in the said 
report the expenditure incurred by the Corpo- 
ration on the administration of the Fund.” 

(3) The existing Regulation 12 shall be deleted and the 
following new Regulation 12 substituted therefor, 
-'amely : — 

“12. Members of Fund—(\) Every employee of the 
Corporaiotn who is confirmed in his service shall 
be required to become a Member of the Fund 
from the date on which these Regulations come 
into force, or on his confirmation in service, 
whichever is later. 

(2) A temporary employee who has completed one 
year’s continuous service and is eligible in all 
respects for confirmation shall, if he so elects, 
be permited to become a member of the Fund, 
and these Regulations shall thereupon apply to 
him to the same exent as they apply to perma- 
nent employees. 


(3) Notwithstanding, anything contained in clause 
(f) of Regulation 2 or in any Service Rules or 
Regulations of the Corporation fer the time 
being in force, the Corporation may, in its 
discretion, permit an employee employed on 
special contract of service to become a member 
of the Fund on such terms as the Corporation 
may decide in each individual case.” 

(4) Sub-regulation (1) of Regulation 15 shall be deleted 
and consequential amendments made where necessary in 
the other provisions of the Regulations. 

(5) The following further provisos shall be added to sub- 
clause (iff) of sub-regulation (1) of Regulation 19: 

“Provided further that if such policy matures or 
otherwise falls due for payment before the 
employee ceases to be in the service of the Cor- 
poration, the amount received or receivable 
thereunder shall be utilised to reimburse the 
members account to the extent of the amount 
drawn therefrom or the amount received against 
the insurance policy, whichever is less, and the 
balance, if any, made over to the beneficiary 
under the policy. 

Provided further that if a memoer, having with- 
drawn any amounts under the foregoing sub- 
ciause (iii), elects to discontinue such with- 
drawals, the total amount so withdrawn by him 
for payment of insurance premia shall be 
reimbursed to his account in the Fund and the 
insurance policy assigned to or deposited with 
the Trustees shall not be reassigned or returned 
to him, as the case may be, unless the amount 
withdrawn is so reimbursed.” 

(6) Clause (iii) of sub-regulation (1) of Regulation 20 
and the proviso thereto shall be deleted. 

(7) Regulation 24 shall be deleted and the following 
new Regulation 24 substituted therefor: — 

“24. Annual Report and Accounts — Within six 

months of the close of each financial year, the 
Board shall submit to the Central Government 
and the Corporation the accounts of the Fund 
for the last preceding financial year together 
with a report on the working of the Fund during 
that year.” 

(8) The expression “and of the Commissioner of Income- 
tax, Bombay City, I” occurring in Regulation 25 shall be 
deleted.
