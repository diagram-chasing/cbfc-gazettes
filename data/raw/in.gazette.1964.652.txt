No. l-C4(35)/64 — The lollowing draft of certain amendments to the Chartered Accountants Regulations, 1949, which are proposed 
to be made in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-Sections (1) and (3) of Section 30 of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 (XXXVIII 
of 1949), is published for information of all persons likely to be affected. Notice is hereby given that the draft will be taken up for con- 
sideration on or after the 23 rd March 1964. 

In this connection, a reference is also invited to the Institute's Notification No. l*CA(30)/63 dated 10th June 1963 published in Part 
111 Section 4 of the Gazette of India dated 22nd Juno 1963, wherein comments have been invited on the draft of the newly codified Regula- 
tions under the Chartered Accountants Act which are proposed to be substituted in the place of the existing Regulations. The newly codi- 
fied Regulations are at present awaiting Government approval and are expected to be brought into force shortly. 

The amendments now being notified have been drafted both in respect of the existing Regulations as well as the codified Regulations 
awaiting Government approval. If by the time the views on the suggested amendments arc considered the codified Regulations are approved 
by the Government, then the amendments would be carried out in those Regulations and not the existing Regulations. 

Any suggestions which may be received from any person with respect to the said draft before the dale specified will be considered 
by the Council of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, New Delhi.
