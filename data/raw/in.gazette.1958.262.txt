0. The agreement in question was entered into outside conciliation proceedings.

10, Shri Acharya urges that as bonus had been paid in 1955 and 1956 in terms

in view of the statutory restriction contemplated bv Insurance Act and the general

be a condition of service, irrespective of the trading results of the industry. Other 
terms contained in the agreement of 10th June 1955 which relate to basic wages, 
and dearness allowance became conditions of service as soon as the agreement was 
acted upon.
