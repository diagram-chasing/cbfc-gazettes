MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING 

NOTIFICATION 

New Delhi, the 15 th December 1971 

8.0. 14(E). —In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (c) of sub-section 
(2) of section 0 of the Cinematograph Act, 1952 (37 of 1952), the Central Govern¬ 
ment hereby directs that the exhibition of the film entitled “JOI BANGLA DESH" 
(Hindi) which has been granted a ‘U’ Certificate No. 64340, dated 26th October, 
1971 by the Central Board of Film Censors, be suspended for a period of two 
months with effect from the date of this Notification.
