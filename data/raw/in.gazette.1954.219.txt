Application for Grading. — During the year 1953-54 five 
applications for grading and three applications for re- 
grading were received. Of these five were graded. 

Grant of Export Certificate. — For the period under 
review. Certificates for Shipment Coal were issued for a 
total quantity of 20,63,306 tons as against 27,43,729 tons 
during the previous year. It may be observed that there 
has been a drop of 6,80,423 tons during the year under 
review owing to lack of enquiries from foreign buyers. 
Recently a few of the graded collieries have not taken 
that amount of care in eliminating the shale, stones and 
other rejectables. Tt is essential that coal for export 
should strictly comply with the certificate, granted by the 
Indian Coal Grading Board so that India’s standard may
