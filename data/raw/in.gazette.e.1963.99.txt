STATE BANK OF INDIA 

NOTICE 

Bombay, 2nd December, 1963 

In terms of Section 29(1) of the State Bank of India 
(Subsidiary Banks) Act, 1959, the State Bank of India, 
after consulting the Board of Directors of the State Bank 
of Indore and with the approval of the Reserve Bank of 
India, have reappointed Shri O. S. Srivastava as the 
General Manager of the State Bank of Indore for a period 
of three years with effect from the 1st January, 1964. 

B. MUKERJI 
Managing Director.

ferred under Rule 6 of the Chartered Accountants 
Students' Association Rules, the Council of the Institute 
of Chartered Accountants of India hereby notifies as 
under :— 

Whereas according to rule 34 of the Chartered Account¬ 
ants Students’ Association Rules the Annual General 
Meeting of the Members of the Eastern India Chartered 
Accountants Students’ Association was required to be held 
in the month of September 1963.

the Members of the Eastern India Chartered Accountants 
Students’ Association be held by 31st day of December

and validly held.
