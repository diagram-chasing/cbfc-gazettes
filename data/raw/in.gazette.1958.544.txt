AWARD 

1. These seven complaint petitions are taken up together as they are to be dis- 
posed of on the same common ground. Each of the petitioners had alleged that 
his or her service was terminated by the Company without assigning any reason 
and also without permission of this Tribunal during the pendency of the above 
reference. The Management in each case have contended that it was only the 
workmen concerned who had either absented without leave or over-stayed-leave 
and did not turn up to work and, therefore, they had to terminate their services. 

2. It is admitted that even after terminating the' services of these workmen, 
the Management have not applied for approval of their action by the Tribunal 
as required by the amended Section. 33 of the I.D. Act which came into force 
from March last and these acts of the Management of terminating the services 
of the petitioners were subsequent to that. As the law stood prior to the amend- 
ment of Section. 33, it was obligatory on the Management to take prior permission 
of the Tribunal to discharge workmen connected in a dispute but after amend- 
ment, no such prior permission is necessary except in certain cases but the 
Management were bound to have applied for approval of their action and then 
only their action cannot be questioned. In this case, admittedly no such ap- 
proval was obtained and, therefore, the action of the Company in each of these 
cases violated the provisions of Sec. 33 and as such, the workmen concerned have 
good grounds to complain. On this ground alone, the action of the Management 
can be held to be a violation of Sec. 33 of the I.D. Act but in this case wi bout 
entering into the merits of the allegations cf either party, the representatives ap- 
pearing on either side agreed that these cases should be amicably settled and Ihe 
Management would take back the workmen on the following terms: — 

1. Workmen in each of these cases shall be reinstated to his or her post by 

the Management; and 

2. His or her absence up-to-date of reinstatement shall be treated as leave 

on loss of pay but continuity of their service shall not be broken. 

3. These terms are very reasonable for the other workmen who are not re- 
presented by Shri S. Mukherjee as well, and in the interests of industrial har-
