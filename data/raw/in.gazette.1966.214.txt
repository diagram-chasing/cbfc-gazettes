No. GM/ 58-19 — In exercise of the power* conferred 
by sub-section (1) of section 45 of the Air Corporation* 
Act, 1953, Air-India, with the previous approval of the 
Central Government, hereby maxes the following regula- 
tions, namely : — 

1. Short title and application. — (1) These regulation* 
may be called the Air-India Employees’ (Miscellaneous 
Loans) Regulations, 1966. 

(2) They shall apply to all employees of the Corpora- 
tion in India and abroad. 

2. Definitions.— In these Regulations, unless the con- 
text otherwise requires, — 

(a) “Corporation” means Air-India; 

(b) "General Manager" means the General Mana- 
ger of the Corporation; 

(c) "Employee" means a person in the service of 
the Corporation, but does not include a person 
employed on a part-time or casual basis. 

3. General Conditions. — (1) The grant of loans and 
advances under these Regulations shall be solely at the 
discretion of the appropriate authority and no employee 
shall be entitled to claim any benefit under these Regu- 
lations as a condition of service or as a matter of right. 

(2) Not more than one loan shall be granted for the 
**me purpose. 

(3) In sanctioning any loans under these Regulations, 
the sanctioning authority shall take into account the capa- 
city of the employee to repay the loan having regard to 
his monthly emoluments and his liability to reoay any 
other outstanding loans that he may have taken from the

THE BAR COUNCIL OF INDIA 

New Delhi, the 16 th March 1966 

The Executive Committee of the Bar Council of India 
has at its meeting dated 26th February 1966 approved 
the following rules : — 

RESOLUTION No. 3/1966 (E.C.) 

RESOLVED that the following Gratuity Rules (fram- 
ed as required under Rule 11, Chapter V, Part II of the 
Rules of the Council) for the benefit of the employees of 
the Bar Council of India be and are hereby approved ; — 

Gratuity Fund Rules 

1. These rules shall be called the gratuity rules for 
the employees of the Bar Council of India. 

2. In these rules, in the absence of anything repugnant 
to the context : — 

(i) “Gratuity fund’’ shall mean the fund created 
and maintained under these rules for the pay- 
ment of gratuity to the employees of the Coun- 
cil. 

(ii) “Employer” shall mean the Bar Council of 
India. 

(iii) "Employee” shall refer to the permanent em- 
ployees of the Bar Council of India. 

(iv) “Salary” includes dearness allowance, and does 
not include any other allowance. 

3. A permanent member of the staff who has com- 
pleted three years of permanent service in the Council 
shall be paid at the end of his service, by retirement or 
otherwise, gratuity equal to 3/ 4ths of the months salary 
last drawn by the member, for each completed year of 
his permanent service. 


Provided that in no case shall the gratuity exceed the 
amount so calculated for a period of 15 months. 

Provided also that no gratuity shall be payable in the 
case of a member of the staff who has been dismissed 
for misconduct. 

4. If a permanent member of the staff who has com- 
pleted three years’ qualifying service dies while in ser- 
vice such member or members of his fam'ly as may 
have been nominated, or his heirs, and legal representa- 
tive as the case may be, shall be paid gratuity at the 
rate of 1 month’s salary for each completed year of ser- 
vice of the employee provided that the maximum amount 
payable shall not in any case exceed 3/4ths of the month- 
ly salary (last-drawn) for a period of 15 months. 

Provided that the Executive Committee may, in any 
particular case, in its discretion pay a larger sum than 
was payable to the employee not exceeding, however, the 
maximum amount that may be paid as aforesaid under 
these Rules. 

5. The Bar Council of India shall have and maintain 
a special fund called the “Gratuity Fund”. 

6. The Bar Council of India shall (a) pay to the Fund 
referred to in Rule 5 within a month from the date on 
which these rules are approved, an amount equal to 
8-1/3% of the salary ot each of the employees of the 
Council for the period of his service till the date when 
these rules come into force, and (b) also pay to the 
said Fund not latter than the 1st of May of each subse- 
quent year 8-1/3% of the salary of each employee as 
on the" 3 1st March of each subsequent year. 

7. The amount referred to in Rule 6 shall be deposit- 
ed in a separate Post Office Savings Bank Account or in 
a special account to be opened for the purpose in any 
Scheduled Bank approved by the Executive Committee. 

8. Tile Fund referred to in these Rules shall be vested 
in two Trustees one of whom shall be the Chairman of 
the Bar Council of India and the other the Vice-Chair- 
man thereof, 

9. Separate accounts shall be maintained for the Fund 
showing the amounts contributed by the Council, the 
investments made thereof, the interest accrued and the 
amount in the Fund at the end of each financial year, 
the payments made to the employees and such other 
particulars as are necessary. 

10. These Rules shall come into force on the 1st of 
March 1966. 

A. N. VEERARAGHAVAN 
Secretary 
Bar Council of India
