1. Indian Panorama, 1986 

1.1 The Indian Panorama, 1986 (hereinafter 
referred to as “the Indian Panorama") will consist 
of a maximum of 21 full-length feature films, with 
English sub-titles and a maximum of 21 short films,

Panorama, 1985.
