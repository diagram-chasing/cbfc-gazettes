National Awards for Family Planning Films 

No. 1-19/69 -MEM, — -The following rules are notified for 
regulating the national awards far family planning films, 
1969 

1. These Rules shall be called ‘Rules for the National 
Awards for Family Planning Films’. 

2. The object of these, awards is to encourage the produc- 
tion, distribution and exhibition of suitable films for pro- 
pagating the norm of a small, happy family and motivating 
the people to adopt family planning. 

3. The following categories of awards will be available 
under these Rules : — 

(1) Best feature film on Family Planning for propagating the 
norm of a small family. 

(i) First Prize — Rs. 60,000 (Rupees sixty thousand 
only) to th* Producer.

Director.
