(c) fees or commission if they are authorised emoluments of an appoint-

(ii) special pay other than that referred to in clause (d) above; 

(ill) pay drawn in temporary or officiating appointments;

drawn in higher tenure appolntment(s) whether held in substan-

appointment(s) does not qualify for the grant of a special addi- 
tional pension.
