(Public Notice under Rule 19(3) and (4) of the Rail- 
way Rates Tribunal Rules, 1959). 

Complaint No. 2 of 1964. 

Harinagar Sugur Mills Ltd. — Complainants, 

Vs. 

The Union of India owning the North Eastern Railway, 
and represented by the General Manager, North Eastern 
Railway, Gorakhpur. — Respondent. 

Whereas the complainants above named have made a 
complaint that they have an Assisted Siding at Harinagar 
Railway station for the use of their sugar mills, that in 
respect of it in or about 1933, they entered into an agree- 
ment with the then Bengal and North Western Railway, 
that the said agreement had provided that they should 
pay for the u^e of the said siding a sum of Rs. 629.68 nP 
in two half-yearly instalments, that they had been pay- 
ing the said amount from November 1933, till March 
1960, that the respondent on and from April I960 had 
revised the said agreement, and enhanced the charges 
payable by the complainant in respect of the said Assis- 
ted Siding to Rs. 267,62 nP for each half year towards 
interest on capital and cost of maintenance of permanent 
way, plus the amount calculated on the basis of Re. 1/- 
per four wheeled wagon hauled over the siding, subject to 
a minimunv of Rs. 14 per shunt towards siding charges, 
and began to levy the enhanced amount on the revised 
basis; 

Lhat on or about January 1963, the Government of 
India took over the complainant mills under the Indus- 
tries (Development and Regulation) Act, and managed 
the same, that thereupon the complainants moved the 
Punjab High Court, and obtained orders quashing the 
order of the Government of India taking over the com- 
plainant-mills, that the Government of India during the 
period of their management of the complainant-mills 
agreed to pay the aforesaid revised charges and entered 
into an agreement to that effect with the respondent, that 
the said agreement is a nullity and does not bind the 
complainant; 

That the revised siding charges levied on and from 
April 1960 are unreasonable and unlawful, and have 
prayed for the siding charges fixed on the basis of the 
aforesaid 1933 agreement, or such other siding charges 
as are deemed reasonable, and prayed further for givmg 
effect to such order from the date of the complaint and 
for costs. 

And whereas it is thought that there may be persons 
who are not on record, but have the same interest in 
the proceedings as the complainants or the respondent 
abovenamed, 

This public notice is, therefore, given under Rule 
19(3) and (4) of the Railway Rates Tribunal Rules, 
1959, so that any person who so desires, may petition the 
Tribunal within 30 days of the publication of this notice 
for leave to intervene support of or opposition to the 
relief sought in the complaint, or be added on the side 
of the complainants or the respondent, setting forth the 
grounds of the proposed intervention, and the position, 
and the interest of the petitioner in the proceedings; or 
the grounds for being added as a party in the said case, 
Any decision given by the Tribunal after this public no- 
tice shall apply to all such persons.

this 29th day of July 1964, at “Adyar House 1 ’, Raja
