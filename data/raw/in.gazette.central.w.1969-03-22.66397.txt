discharge or dismissal of a workman , the Industrial Court has the power to 
enquiry whether tho order made by the employer has been passed mala fide

( 2 ) If the Anding of the Tribunal is that the impugned order has been passed mala

( 5 ) The Tribunal cxceeds its jurisdiction if it proceeds to examine elaborately tho

of Gujarat, Maharashtra , Andhra Pradesh , Madras, Mysoro and Kerala for a public 
purposo being a purpose connected with the relief and rehabilitation of displaced persons 
including payment of compensation to such persons. 

Now , therefore , in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 12 of the Displaced 
Persons (Compensation and Rehabilitation ) Act, 1954 (44 of 1954 ), it is notified that the 
Central Government has decided to acquire and hereby acquires the evacuee properties 
specified in the Schedule hereto annexed . 

A . SCHEDULE 
All Properties in the States of Gujarat, Maharashtra , Madras , Mysore, Andhra Pradesh 
and Kerala which have been allotted to the share of the Custodian in partition or have 
vested in the Custodian under Section 11 of the Evacuee Interest (Soparation ) Act, 
1951, as a result of adjudication by the Competent Officer under the provisions of the 
said Act upto 31st December, 1968 and in respect of which no appeals have been fled , 
and if filed , have been rejected by the Appellate Officer .
