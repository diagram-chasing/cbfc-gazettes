MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING 
PUBLIC NOTICE 

New Delhi, the J 7th March, 1998 

No. 303/3/98-F(F). —Reference is invited to this Minstry's Public Notice No 303/10/86-F(F) dated 24-11 -87 notiiy- 
ing the National Film Festival Regulation as subsequently ainended/notified from time to time. 

The aforesaid regulations are hereby amended to the extent indicated below : 

(1) For the existing regulation 2 the following shall be substituted, namely : 

"2. The Festival aims at encouraging the production of films of aesthetic and technical excellence and social 
relevance contributing to the understanding and appreciation of cultures of different regions of the country 
in cinematic form and thus also promoting integration and unity of the nation." 

(2) After the existing regulation, 10 the following new Regulation shall be inserted, namely ; — 

" 10 A. The producer/prodction company shall ensure that the names indicated in the entry form tally with the 
credit titles appearing in the film. A certificate to this effect shall be furnished by the producer. " 

(3) For the existing regulations 11(b) and 11(c) the following shall be substituted, namely : — 

"(b) Films in 16 mm, 35 mm or in a wider gauge made in any Indian language and certified by the Central 
Board of Film Certification as a feature film or featurette are eligible for feature film category. In the case of 
award for Best Children's Film only such films shall be eligible as have been certified by the Board as 
Children's films. 

The questiou whether a film is a feature film or non-feature film shall be decided by the Feature Film Jury 
and its decision shall be final. Therefore, doubtful cases shall be referred to the Feature Film Jury for a 
decision. 

(c) For the non-feature films section, only those films that are certified by the Central Board of Film Certi¬ 
fication as documentary/newsreel/non-fiction are eligible for non-fearute film category'. Short fiction films 
like students' diploma films shall also be considered under this category," 

(4) After the existing regulation 11 (e) the following new clause shall be inserted, namely : 

"(f). Films submitted should be in their original first CBFC (Central Board of Film Certification) certified 
version except film(s) produced by Film Institute(s) recognised by Government of India as per provisions of 
clause (d) above." 

(5) In regulation 16(1)—(i) for the existing sub-clause (c), the following shall be substituted, namely : — 

"(c). A non-refimdable entry fee of Rs. 2000/- for feature film and Rs. 500/- for non-feature film in the form 
of a crossed Demand Draft in favour of "Directorate of Film Festivals" payable at the State Bank of India, 
New Delhi." 

(ii) For the existing sub-existing sub-clauso (d), the following shall be substituted, namely 
"(d) Two certified copies of the censor certificate." 

(6) In regulation 16(2), for the existing sub-clause (a) the following shall be substituted, namely :— 

“(a) Thirty five copies of the detailed synopsis in English comprising of 3 to 4 pages on standard A-4 size 
bond paper.” 

(7) In regulation 16(3), for the existing sub-clause (a) and (b) the following shall be substituted, namely ;—. 
“(a) Fifteen copies of synopsis in English in not more than two pages on standard A-4 size bond paper.” 

(b) Short biographical sketches of the producer, director, music director, animator (in the case of animation 
films), and cameraman (in the case of news films) alongwith two photographs each."

"21 A. Any person who is related either by blood or marriage to any of those whose work is to be adjudged 
shall not also be eligible to serve on these jury panels." 

For the existing regulation 23, the following shall be substituted, namely :— 

"23. The quorum for the meeting of the jury, both in the case of feature and non-feature films, shall be 75% 
of the members of the concerned jury and the Chairman."
