The parties agreed that my decision in this case shall

5. It was common ground between the parties that

resumed frem the flist shift of 15th June, 1971. Thus

the strike, but the Union contended that the strike was. 
rather forced on the workmen by an assault committed

lenienev In regard to the deductions made from the
