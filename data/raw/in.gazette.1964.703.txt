(Public Notice under Rule 19(3) and (4) of the Rail- 
way Rates Tribunal Rules, 1959). 

Complaint No. 1 of 1964 

The Tinplate Co. of India Ltd, Calcutta— Complainant, 
vs 

The Union of India owning and representing the South 
Eastern Railway, Calcutta — Respondent. 

Whereas the complainant above named has made a 
complaint under Section 41 (1) (a) and (c) of the In- 
dian Railways Act, that have a siding known as Tin- 
plate Siding, taking off from the Tata Industrial - Siding 
at Tatanagar on the South Eastern Railway, that the said 
respondent railway has notified through their Special 
Foreign Rates Circular No. 2(A) of 1960 that a haulage 
charge of Rs. 9.56 nP. per wagon is leviable ip respect 
of goods traffic booked to and from the Tinplate Siding 
and moving over the Tata Industrial Siding at Tatanagar 
and in that said circular it has been specially mentioned 
that the additional charge of Rs. 2.53 per wagon notified
