HONEY GRADING AND MARKING RULES, 1955 . 


1. Short title . - (1) These rules may be called the Honey Grading and Marking 
Rules , 1957 " . 


( 11) They shall apply to " extracted " Apis indica honey , 

2 . Grade designations . - The grade designations to indicate the characteristics 
and quality of honey shall be as set out in column ( i) of Schedule I. 

3 . Definitlon of quality . ( ) The quality of honey as indicated by the grades 
shall be all set out against each designation in columns ( 2 ) , ( 3 ) and ( 4 ) of 
Schedule 1 . It shall also possess the physical and chemical characteristics as 
mentioned against each grade in Schedule II. 

( it ) Whenever the Agricultural Marketing Adviser to the Government of 
India is satisfied that 70 per cent or more of the honey produced in any particular 
area or season is from a specific floral origin , he may fix such special characteris 
tics as he may consider necessary to define such honey . 

4 . Grade designation mark . The grade designation mark snall consist of a 
label or bandrole bearing the design and colour set out in Schedule III, specifying
