Dungar station to the Phallodi quarries has been com 
structed and is maintained by the complainants at their 
own cost, that from the year 1953 till about 1958, the 
respondent used only one locomotive for movement of 
empty wagons to the quarry sidings and the loaded 
wagons from the quarry sidings to the cement factory 
at Sawai Madhopur, that the respondent was levying 
siding charges for hauling the wagons between 
Rawanjna Dungar station and the quarries, at the rate 
of Rs. 70/- per trip based on the time taken by a 
single locomotive, that from the year 1958 traffic in 
limestone increased and the respondent railway started 
using an additional locomotive for hanking purposes 
only, that while no additional charges were levied for 
the use of the second locomotive till July, 1961, on or 
about 14th July, 1961, the respondent advised that the 
siding charges have been increased to Rs. 138/- per 
trip payable from the date on which the second loco- 
motive had begun to work, that the second locomotive 
is being used by the respondent railway for its own 
convenience since a single locomotive can haul upto 
80 loaded wagons and that there is no justification for 
increasing the shunting charges, that in spite of repre- 
sentations, the respondent advised in August 1965, 
that siding charges will be levied for two engines, but at 
80% only and demauded payment of Rs, 2,56,210.50 
by 1-9-1965.
