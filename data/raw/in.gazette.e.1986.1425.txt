MINISTRY OF HUMAN RESOURCE DEVELOPMENT 
(Department of Culture) 

New Delhi, the 13th August, 1986 

S.O. 489 (E) .—Whereas the Central Government is of the opi¬ 
nion that the film ‘ANDHERI RAAT MEIN D1YA TERE HAATH 
MEIN’ (Hindi) which has been granted ‘UA’ certificate with cuts 
by the Central Board of Film Certification in pursuance of the order 
of the Film Certification Appellate Tribunal, contains vulgar 
dialogues and visuals oliending human sensibilities and derogatory 
to womanhood ; and whereas the film thus violates the guideline 
in para 2 (iv), of the guidelines issued under the Ministry of In¬ 
formation and Broadcasting Notification No. F. 5I5I77-FC dated 
7-1-1978 (as amended). 

Therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of 
sub-section (2) of section 6 of the Cinematograph Act 1952 (37 of 
1952), the Central Government hereby directs that the film entitled 
‘ANDHERI RAAT MEIN 1JIYA TERE HAATH MEIN’ which was 
granted ‘UA’ certificate No. 133-UA dated 5-5-1986 by the Central 
Board of Film Certification, Madras shall be deemed to be an un¬ 
certified film in the whole of India with effect fiom the date of issue 
of this Notification.
