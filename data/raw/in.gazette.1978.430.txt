Short Title and Commencement 

1. (1) These bye-laws may be called the Lansdowne 

Cantonment (regulation of Job Porters) bye-laws, 
1978. 

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their 
publication in the official gazette. 

Definitions 

2. In these bye-laws, Unless the context otherwise requites, - 

(a) “Licensing Officer’’ means the Executive Officer; 

(b) /"Porter” includes any person who carries packages, 
loads, or works as carrier by the job, and casual 
labourer, 

(c) “Licensee" means a person duly licenced under these 
bye-laws. 

Area within wldch a Porter may operate 

3. No person shall ply or work for hire as a porter within 
the limits of the Lansdowne Cantonment, unless he has taken 
out a licence under these bye-laws and such licence is opera- 
tive under bye-law 9 : 

Provided that these bye-laws shall not apply to any porter 
hired for journey which is commenced beyond the Canton- 
ment limits. 

Application for Grant of Licence 

4. Every application for a licence shall be made to the 
Licencing Officer. 

Power to refuse Grant of Licence 

5. The Licencing Officei may refuse to grant a licence — • 

(a) When the applicant is reported by the Police autho- 
rities to be a suspicious or bad character, or 

(b) When the applicant is a person whose licence has 
within the two preceding years been cancelled for 
breach of any of these bye-laws. 

Price of Metal Badge 

6. Every applicant for a licence shall, immediately on the 
issue of a licence to him, pay to the Licencing Officer, a sum 
of rupee one on account of cost of a metal badge, which shall 
be given to him by that Officer, together with a table of the 
current rates specified under these bye-laws : 

Provided that if the actual cost Incurred by the Board on 
the purchase of the metal badge exceeds rupee one, it may 
revise the said price of metal badge to the extent not exceeding 
the actual cost incurred by it on its purchase. 

Licence and Metal Badge when to be delivered back 

7. Upon the expiration, suspension or cancellation of a 
licence, the Licensee shall be bound to deliver up to the 
Licencing Officer his licence and badge. | 

Licence Fee 

8. The fee payable by each licensee shall be 0,50 paise per 
month; 

Provided that in the case of a labourer employed by any 


contractor, the monthly fee payable shall be recoverable 
through the employer of such labourer. 

Currency of Licence 

9. A licence shall not be operative — 

(a) after the 31st March next following the date from 
which it is expressed to lake effect; 

(b) in favour of any person other than the person to 
whom it has been granted and described in the 
licence; 

(e) unless the licensee is carrying his badge exposed to 
view. 

Circumstances under which Licence is liable to be cancelled 

10. Every licence shall be liable to cancellation in any of 
the following events, namely, if the licensee — ■ 

(a) is found to have, when applying for his licence, 
given a false name, parentage, or address or failed 
to deliver up any licence or badge as required under 
bye-law 7; 

(b) has transferred his licence or badge to any other 
person; 

(c) fails to produce his licence or badge for Inspection 
when so required by any Magistrate, Police Officer, 
the Executive Officer or Revenue Inspector or Sani- 
tary Inspector of the Board authorised in this behalf 
by the Executive Officer; 

(d) fails, within the next 24 hotirs, to take to the police 
station in-charge thereof, any lost or unclaimed 
property coming into his possession; 

(e) without leasonnble cause refuses to hife himself; 

(f) uses insulting or abusive language, while under con- 
tract of hire. 

(g) fails to produce his table of rates when required to 
do so, by any person having occasion to check any 
claim or demand made by him for remuneration; or 

(h) without sufficient reason prevents or causes to pre- 
vent any other licensee from entering into a contract 
of hire.

cancellation of Licence 

11. The Licencing Officer, may, after issuing a show cause 
notice and after being satisfied that the cause shown is un- 
satisfactory or when no cause is shown within the period speci- 
fied in the said notice, suspend or cancel a licence which has 
become liable for cancellation under bye-law Iff. 

Explanation. — The cancellation of his licence shall be no bar 
to the licensee being prosecuted for any offence punishable 
under these bye-laws, which he may have committed.

12. An appeal shall lie to the Board from any order of the
