MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING 
New Delhi, the 1st October, 1984 
PUBLIC NOTICE 

No. 303|12|'84-F(F):—The Government of India in the 
Ministry of Information and Broadcasting hereby notify that in 
the Indian Panorama, 1985 Regulations (hereinafter referred to 
as the “Regulations”) for the Indian Pondorama Section of the 
10th International Film Festival of India to be held in New 
Delhi from 3rd January, 1985 to 17th January, 1985 notified in 
the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part I, Section I dated 22nd 
August, 1984, the following changes shall be made:*— 

(i) -The 1st sentence of regulation 3.1 of the Regulations 
may be substituted by the following :— 

“3.1, All films in 35 mm or in any wider gauge, viz. 
cinemascope, 70 mm etc. made in any Indian lan¬ 
guage and certifld by the Central Board of Film 
Certification between 1st November, 1983 and 15th 
October, 1984 both days inclusive) are eligible 
for entry in the Indian Panorama.”
