8 . Shri P . Madhava Rao appearing for the opposite party , has argued that this 
application was not maintainable because , the applicant s suit in the City Clvil 
Court was still pending and has not been withdrawn . He stated that to entertain 
this application during the pendency of the civil suit filled by the appicant in the 
Bombay City Civil Court would amount to encouraging multifarious legal pro 
ceedings I am inclined to accept this contention . It is clear that the applicant 
has not withdrawn his civil suit Aled by him in the City Civil Court and in my 
opinion , to entertain during the pendency of the proceedings of that suit, this 
application would be encourage multifariousness of legal proceedings and dilatory 
legal , tactics. The order of transfer was made on 4th May, 1965 when admittedly , 
the industrial dispute Ref. C .G . I. T , 84 of 1964 was pending before this Tribunal 
Yet the applicant did not Ale this application against the alleged - punishinent by 
way of transfer till as late as on 28th April, 1966 during which period he was 
prosecuting the civil suit which he Aled in the City Civil Court. Though there 
is no time limit specified for a complaint under section 33A , it must nevertheless 
be Aled at the earliest. In my opinion , by Aling this application almost a year after 
the date of the order of transfer dated the 4th May, 1965 , the applicant has not 
acted bonafidely or with deligence. Even now , he has not given up presecuting 
the proceedings in the civil suit which he has filed in the City Civil Court. On the 
basis of this , to entertain this application which has been filed almost one year 
After the order of transfer and more than one month after the industrial dispute 
in Ref . No. C .G .I. T , 84 of 1964 had been settled , would be to put a premium on 
multifarious legal proceedings and encourage unjustified delay , which conduct 
cannot be considered to be bonafide, I , therefore, also uphold the second objection 
of the bank and hold that this application is not maintainable. 

9. In the result , the stay order till 30th May , 1966, made in this application on

1966 . The application is dismissed as not maintainable .
