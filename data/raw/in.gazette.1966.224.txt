[Public Notice issued under Rule 19(3) and (4) of the 
Railway Rates Tribunal Rules , 1959] 

Complaint No. 8 of 1965 

The Motipur Sugar Factory Private limited — 

Complaimint. 

vs. 

The Union of India owning the North Eastern Rail- 
way and represented by its General Manager, Gorakh- 
p u r . — R e spend cn t. 

Whereas the complainants above-named have filed a 
complaint under Section 41(1) (c) of the Indian Rail- 
ways Act, 1891), stating that they have a sugar mill serv- 
ed by Molipur Railway Station on the North Eastern 
Railway, that they entered into an agreement with the 
then Bengal and North Western Railway on or about 
28-11-1934 undertaking to bear the capital cost of land 
and cost of laying the permanent way etc., for the assist- 
ed siding and also to pay 10 per cent of the Railway’s 
capital outlay towards interest, maintenance end haulage 
charges over the assisted siding, that the charges on this 
basis were finally fixed at Rs. 802.2.0 per half year, that 
the system was in force from November 1934 till about 
March 1960, that the respondent unilaterally fixed with 
effect from 1-4-60 the interest and maintenance charges 
at Rs, 681.81 per half year and siding charges at Rs. 1.50 
per four-wheeled wagon which were further increased 
from 1.9.62 to Rs. 1.74 per four-wheeled wagon pins an 
additional levy at 5% on every rupee, that under threat 
of closure of (he siding facilities, the complainants had 1o 
sign a new agreement on or about 5.7.1963 fixing the 
interest cpid maintenance charges at Rs f 2884.56 p plus a 


siding charge of Rs. 1.84 per four-wheeled wagon with 
effect from 1.9.62, that the Railways are under obliga- 
tion to place the wagons at the junction of the private 
siding with the assisted siding and no separate charge for 
haulage over the assisted siding Can be levied and that 
the siding charges are unreasonable. 

And whereas the complainants have prayed for (1) 
fixation of interest and maintenance charges and haulage 
charges on the basis of the 1934 agreement or such other 
basis as is reasonable (2) giving effect to the reliefs from 
the date of the complaint and (3) awarding costs to the 
complainants. 

And whereas it is thought that there may be persons 
who are not on record but have the same interest in the 
proceedings as the complainant or respondent above- 
named. 

This public notice is therefore given under Rule 19(3) 
and (4) of the Railway Rates Tribunal Rules, 1959, so 
that any person who desires may petition the Tribunal 
within 30 days of the publication of this notice, for 
leave to intervene in support of or opposition to the 
reliefs sought in the complaint or be added on the side 
of the complainant or the respondent setting forth the 
grounds of the proposed intervention, the position and 
the interest of the petitioner in the proceedings or the 
grounds for being added as a party in the said case. Any 
decision given by the Tribunal after this public notice 
shall apply to all such persons. 

Given under my hand and seal of the Tribunal this 
28th day of May 1966 at “Adyar House”, Rajah Anna- 
malaipuram., Madras-28.
