BEFORE THE RAILWAY RATES TRIBUNAL AT 
MADRAS 

(Public notice under Rule 19(3) and (4) of the Railway 
Rates Tribunal Rules, 1959) 

Complaint No. 2 of 1960 

Messrs. K. Abdul Rahman Sahib & Co.. Madras-7 — 
Complainant. 

Vs. 

The Union of India owning and representing the Southern 
Railway, Madras — Respondent. 

Whereas the complainant abovenamed has made a com- 
plaint that the method of charging firewood booked in 
wagonloads from Moragpur, Samalpatti and Mettur Dam 
stations on the Southern Railway to Madras, on fixed 
weights based on the floor area of the wagons used has 
caused inconvenience to consignees of firewood, that the 
said method of charging is an unjustifiable discrimination 
against the complainant and others importing firewood 
from the said stations, and have prayed that the respon- 
dent Railway be directed to charge freight on actual 
weight subject to the notified minimum weight for charge, 
as was done prior to July 1959. 

And whereas it is thought that there are numerous per- 
sons who are not on record but have the same interest 
in the proceedings as the complainant or respondent 
abovenamed. 

This public notice is, therefore, given under Rule 19(3) 
and (4) of the Railway Rates Tribunal Rules. 1959, so that 
any person who so desires may petitioh the Tribunal with- 
in 30 days of the publication of this notice for leave to 
intervene in support of or opposition to the relief sought 
in the complaint, or be added on the side of the com- 
plainant or the respondent, setting-forth the grounds of 
the proposed intervention and the position and the interest 
of the petitioner in the proceedings or the grounds for 
being added as a party in the said case. Any decision 
given by the Tribunal after this public notice shall apply 
to all such persons. 

Given under my hand and the seal of the Tribunal, 
this 16th day of June 1960, at “Adyar House”, Madras-28. 

Seal of the Tribunal. (Sd.) N. SESHACHALAM 

Secretary
