NOTIFICATION 

Calcutta 16, the 18th August 1960 

No. 19-CWA(l)/60 — In pursuance of sub-section (3) of 
Section 19 of the Cost and Works Accountants Act, 1959, 
the list of members of the Institute of Cost and Works 
Accountants of India as on 1st day of April 1960, is here- 
by published for general information. 

Explanatory Note 

There are two classes of members, namely, Fellows 
and Associates. They are entitled to use the letters 
“F.I.C.W.A.” and “A.I.C.W.”, respectively, after their 
names. There are two lists, one arranged alphabetically 
and the other topographically according to Regions. In the 
topographical section the names of places in each Region 
and the names of members have been arranged alphabe- 
tically. 

In the alphabitical section the number shown against 
each name of a member is the Membership Number of 
that member. 

”F” after a member’s name denotes that he is a 
Follow, and “A” after member’s name demotes that he 
is an Associate. 

In the alphabetical section, the name of the place of
