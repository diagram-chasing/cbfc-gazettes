2 . On the usual notices being issued , the President, Chhattisgar Colliery

3 . The case on behalf of the workmen is that on 23rd April, 1955 there was 
al strike in all the three North , South and West Jhagrakhand Collicries and it 
was withdrawn on the night of 24th April, 1955 . The work was resumed on 
the morning of 25th April , 1955 but when the workmen Shri Netram , Shri Bagar

do so. In fact they did not join the strike and even otherwise they were neither 
chargc - shected nor any inquiry was held against them . Shri Kanaiha Lal, 
Underground Apprentice was charge - sheeted and he had given his reply but 
the inquiry was held during his absence und in the result he was wrongfully 
locked out from 23rd May , 1955. All these four workmen should therefore be 
reinstated with full back wages and other benefits . 

4 . The case on behalf of the Management is that Shri Bagar Sai and Shri 
Sukhdco who worked as Troileymen at North Jhagrakhend Collicry were absent

As regards Shri Kanailal, though he had received the charge - sheet and sent his

entitled to any relief.
