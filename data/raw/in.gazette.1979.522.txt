No. 25-CA(21)/74 — Pursuant to Regulation 16 of the Char- 
tered Accountants Regulations, 1964, it is hereby, notified that 
Shri Jagdish Chand Tandon, A. C. A., J. C. Tendon & Co. 
15/247A, Civil Lines, Kanpur (Membership No. 7544) having 
been found guilty of professional misconduct by the High Court 
of Judicature at Allahabad and the High Court on 6th August, 
1979 having ordered that his name be removed from membership 
of the Institute for a period of five years, the Council of the Insti- 
tute has accordingly removed his name from the Register of 
Members for a period of five years with effect from the date of 
the said order, viz. 6th August, 1979.
