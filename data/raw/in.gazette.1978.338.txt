6. Provision for Wafer Supply, Urinals, Latrines and Bath 
Rooms ;■ — 

(1) Every person who erects or re -erects a building, the

Every building proposed to be erected, or re-erected and

Every building proposed to be newly erected shall have a

No building shall be erected or rc-crcctcd for the purpose

(a) such site has been declared by the appropriate

(c) it is situated at a distance of not less than 30

No house to be erected or rc-crcetcd shall have more than 
3 storeys or shall have a height exceeding 15 metres from 
the plinth level;

Where an upper storey is to be constructed, the walls on

and not less than 45 centimetres in thickness if the height

For single storey buildings the outer walls shall not be 
less than 35 centimetres in thickness,

All walls shall be made of stone or bricks in lime or 
cement,

granted only where further ground extension is impossible; 

Provided that the restriction regarding height of 4 Metres 
may be relaxed by the Board in respect of existing buildings 
on which upper storeys are proposed to be constructed; 

Provided further that steps for ingress into or egress 
from a building shall be provided within the plinth area 
itself, 


Haves above doors and windows may be allowed to extend 
to an extent of not more than 0.5 metre beyond the outer 
walls. 


20. Provision for Sloping Roof : — 

A sloping roof may be allowed to extend to an extent 
not. more than 0.5 metre from the outer wall, provided, that 
SLitlicient arrangements has been made for the carriage- of 
storm water from the roof to a drain.

No construction work shall be commenced unless a notice

No building erected or re-erected shall be occupied unless

The plan sanctioned by the Board shall be adhered to in 
every detail :

Provided further that any unforseen internal deviation

necessitated by requirements of buildings and, of which 
previous notice has been tendered to the Hxecutive Officer 
by the owner, shall be deemed to have been regularised if 
the owner submits a final plan on completion of the work 
showing clearly the deviation.

(2) No latrine shall hi loss than 1,5 metre in length and
