Medical Examiner s Report 
The following information is made for the guidance of 
the Medical Examiners : 

The standard of physical fitness to be adopted should 
make due allowance for the age and length of service , 
if any , of the candidate concerned , 


No person will be deemed qualified for appointment, 
who shall not satisfy the Commission that he has no 
disease , constitutional affection , or bodily informnity un 
fitting him or likely to unfit him for that service, 

It should be understood that the question of fitness 
involves the future as well as the present and that one 
of the main objects of medical examination is to secure 
continuous effective service , and in the case of candidate 
for permanent appointment to prevent early pension or 
payments in case of premature death . It is at the same 
time to be noted that the question is one of the likeli 
hood of continuous effective service, and the re 
a candidate need not be advised on account of the pre 
sence of a defect which in only a small proportion of 
cases , is found to interfere with continuous effective 
service . 

In the event of a candidate declared unfit by a Civil 
Surgeon which is challenged by the candidate, the Com 
mission may refer the matter to a Board of medical 
examiners which should normally consist of three mem 
hers, namely , ( i) a physician , (ii ) a surgeon , and ( ii) 
a opthalmologist and should be of comparable status . A 
lady doctor will be co -opted as a member of such Board 
whenever woman candidate is to be examined , 

The report of the Board should be treated as Confiden 
tiul, 

In cases where a Civil Surgeon or a Board of Medical 
Examiners consider (s ) that a minor disability disqualify 
ing a candidate for service in the Commission can be 
cured by treatment (medical or surgical ) , a statement to 
that cffcct should be recorded . There is no objection to 
a candidate being informed to this effect by the appoint 
ing authority and when a cure has been effected it will 
be open to the authority concerned to ask for another 
medical examination ,
