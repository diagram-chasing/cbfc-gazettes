13 . On behalf of the union however certain rulings have 
been cited to show that the management cannot make 
discrimination granting diffcient holidays to different em 
ployees of the saine establishment and they are reported 
in Vol. 5 , S . C .LJ. pages 2909 and 2925 ag also Vol. 7 , 
S . C . L . J . page 614 . But all thesc rulings do not apply 
the facts of the present case . These rulinga would indicate 
that the management framed two Standing Orcler s granting 
different period of holidays to employees appointed prior 
to a certain date and to emplovecs appointed after a certain 
dute . It was held that two such Standing Orders granting 
different conditions of servicc are illegal and hence it WAS 
set aside . But in this particular case there are no such two 
kinds of standing orders. As some of the staff of the State 
Collieries were taken over by the N . C . D . C . and those staff 
under the Civil Service Ruleg were getting 18 days leave , 
hance their right was protected u / s 14 of the Coal Mines 
Nationalisation Act and they were allowed to get the privi 
leges of 18 days lcave . But the taken over staff of private 
collierics or new recruits who were entitled to only 7 days 
leave under the Coal Wage Board recommendations or the 
National Coal Wage Agrcement were given and are heing 
given 7 days leave only and It cannot be said that there has 
been any change in the condition of their service simply 
because for certain period they got 18 days leavc due to 
mistake of the manganment. The management had a right 
to rectify the said mistake and the mistake cannot be taken 
to be a conditions of service . Thus there has been no dis 
crimination on the part of the management and the concern 
ed workmen in the above circumstances are not cntitled to 
18 days festival holidays as demanded by them .
