BYE-LAWS 

1. Short title and commencement-, — (1) These bye-laws 
may be called the Fcrozepur Cantonment (Regulation of 
the use or occupation of any street or public place by 
Itinerant Vendors or by other persons) Bye-laws, 1979. 

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their pub- 
lication in the Official Gazette. 

2. Permission for temporary use or occupation of Street 
or Public Place and Fees Chargeable for such use or occu- 
pation.— No itinerant vendor, hawker, pedlar or other person 
shall use or occupy any street or public place for the sale of 
articles or the exercise of any calling or the setting up of any 
booth or stall within the Cantonment, without first obtaining 
a permit from the Executive Officer in the Form prescribed 
in the Schedule without the payment of the fee prescribed as 
under : — 

(i) shop-board fee at the rate of Rs. 2.00 per month 
or part thereof for temporary occupation of street 
upto 5 Sq. metres or part thereof; 

(ii) wooden kiosk fee at the rate of Rs. 30.00 per month 
or part thereof for temporary occupation of street 
or any other public place in the Cantonment for 
covering area measuring 6 sq. metres or less and 
Rs. 30.00 per month for further increase in the 
area upto 12 sq. metres; 

(iii) (a) ground rent at the rate of Rs. 2.00 per month 

or part thereof for temporary occupation of 
street upto 5 Sq. metres or part thereof for de- 
positing building material for carrying out re- 
pairs to buildings; 

(b) ground rent at the rate of Rs. 3.00 per month 
or part thereof for temporary occupation of 
street upto 1 Sq. metre or part thereof for all 
other purposes; 

(iv) stallage fee at the rale of Rs. 15.00 per month for 
permanent parking of all stationary rehries; and 
Rs. 5.00 per month or part thereof for parking re- 
hircs after 1600 hours or for a short period daily 
at one place; 

(v) hoarding fee at the rate of Rs. 0J25 paise per month 
or part thereof per 0.10 Sq. metre for setting up 
hoarding or advertisement sign boards on Govern- 
ment land or land vested in the Board. 

3. No fees chargeable in certain cases : — In the case of 
temporary occupation of any public place for the purpose of 
a marriage or funeral or any religious gathering, no fees shall 
be chargeable when the written permission of the Executive 
Officer is obtained for such occupation. 

4. Appeal In case of refusal of permission b\ Executive 
Officer : — In case the nermission for tempornrv occupation 
of street or vacant land is refused by the Executive Officer, 
the person concerned shall be entitled to prefer an appeal 
against such refusal to the President, Cantonment Board, who 
shall then decide the matter. 

5. Permit not to interfere in the entry to and exit from 
shops, etc — The permit granted under bye-law 2 shall not 
intcrefere with the free entry or exit from shops and other 
buildings, 

6. Mode of Payment -Every person from whom the fees 
prescribed under bye-law 2 is chargeable, shall pay the same 
in the Office of the Cantonment Board and obtain a receipt 
thereof, 

7. Instructions regarding sanitaty conditions etc, to be car- 
ried out hy the person concerned : — Every person to whom 
a permit is granted under byc-law 2 shall keep his booth or 
stull in clean and sanitary conditions and shall carry out all 
Instructions given by the Executive Officer as regards clean- 
liness and Sanitation.
