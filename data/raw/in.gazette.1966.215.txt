No. l-CA(8)/66 — -The following draft of certain 
amendments to the Chartered Accountants Regulations, 
1964, which it is proposed to make in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (3) of Section 
30 of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 (Act 
XXXVIII of J949), is published for information of all 
persons likely to be affected thereby and notice is hereby 
given that the draft will be taken up for consideration on 
or after the 28 th May 1966, 

Any objection or suggestion which may be received 
from any person with respect to the said draft before the 
date specified will be considered by the Council of the 
Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, New Delhi. 

In the said Regulations : — 

I. Insert the following as sub-regulation (3) in regula- 
tion 114 and re-number the existing sub-regulation (3) 
as sub-regulation (4) : 

“(3) The accounts of a Regional Council, as main- 
tained by the Treasurer, shall be audited every year 
by a Chartered Accountant in practice or a firm of 
such Chartered Accountants appointed by the mem- 
bers of the region at their annual general meeting 
and the accounts together with the audit report arid 
the report of the Regional Council, shall be sent to 
the members in the region at least 14 days before 
the date of the annual general meeting and shall be 
placed for adopting before the annual general meet- 
ing of the members,” 

II. In the re-numbered sub-regulation (4) of regula- 
tion 114,— 

(a) in clause (i), after the words “A copy of” and 
before the words “the report of the", insei t 
the words “the audited accounts and”. 

(b) for the existing clause (ii), substitute the fol- 
lowing : — 

“(ii) The audited Accounts of the Regional 
Council and its branches, if any, shall be in- 
corporated in the accounts of the Council for 
the year.” 

III. For the existing regulations 123 and 124, substi- 
tute the following : — 

“123. Annual general meeting. 

A General Meeting of the members of the Region 
shall be called between the 15th day of May and 
the 15th day of June every year and it shall be call- 
ed the Annual General Meeting.” 

“124. Extraordinary general meeting. 

A Regional Council may call such further meet- 
ings of the members of the Region as often as it 
may deem necessary and such meetings shall be 
called Extraordinary General Meetings." 

IV. For the existing regulation 125, substitute tho 
following 

“125, Notice of meeting. 


At least 14 days’ notice of every meeting specify- 
ing the day, place and hour of such meeting and in 
the case of special business, the general nature of 
the business, shall be given. In case of the annual 
general meeting, the report of the Regional Council 
together with Auditors’ Report and a copy of the 
audited Balance Sheet and Income and Expenditure 
Account for the previous year shall accompany the 
notice.” 

V. In regulation 128, after the words “the report of 
the Regional Council”, insert the words “to adopt 
the accounts, to appoint auditors".
