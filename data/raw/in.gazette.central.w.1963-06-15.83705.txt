S . O . 1607 . - In exercise of the powers conferred by section 157 of the Cus 
toms Act , 1962 (52 of 1962 ) , the Central Board of Revenue hereby makes the 
following regulations, namely : 


1. Short title . These regulations may be called the Imported Goods ( Tran 
shipment by Air ) Regulations, 1963. 


2 . Application , — These regulations shall apply to the transhipment of import 
ed goods, other than goods imported by post , by an aircraft other than a foreign 
going aircraft . 


3 . Conditions governing transhipment. No transhipment shall be allowed 
under these regulations unless the owners of the aircraft or of the imported 
goods execute a bond , in such form and with or without surety , as the proper 
officer may require binding themselves for an amount which shall be equal to 


( a ) the value of the goods, in the case of producer goods; and 


( b ) the market price of the goods in the case of consumer goods. 


4 . Imported goods transhipped to be sealed - ( 1 ) The proper officer shall, 
before the goods are transhipped , place all small packages containing the goods 
in durable bags and geal the bags with the Customs Department seal in the 
presence of an accredited representative of the owners of the aircraft : 


Provided that the packages containing the goods which cannot be placed 
inside a bag may be sealed separately . 


( 2 ) The bags and the materials required for sealing the bags under sub 
regulation ( 1 ) shall be provided by , and at the cost of the owners of the aircraft. 


5 . Terms of the bond to be executed . - - The terms of the bond shall be that if 
the person executing the bond produces to the proper oficer, within fifteen days 
or within such extended period as such officer may allow , a certificate issued by 
the proper ofcer at the customs airport of destination that the goods have been 
landed at that airport, the bond shall stand discharged ; but otherwise an amount 
equal to the value or, as the case may be, the market price of the goods in res 
pect of which the said certificate is not produced shall stand forfeited , 


8 . General bond Notwithstanding anything in these regulations, the proper 
officer may permit owners of aircraft or imported goods to enter into a general 
bond in such form and with such surety or security or both as the proper officer 
may deem fit in respect of the transhipment of goods effected by the owners of 
the aircrafts or on behalf of such owners of goods.
