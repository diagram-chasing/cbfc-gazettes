I. Norms of affiliation of Law Colleges laid down by

1. A law college shall only be located at a place where

No. 49/1979 dated 31st March and 1st April, 1979. 

3. The approval of affiliation of a new college to a Uni- 
versity would be granted by the Bar Council of India only 
if it is an independent law college and not a department of 
a multi-faculty college, 

4. BUILDING
