RESERVE BANK OF INDIA 


NOTIFICATION 
Bombay, the 1 9th June 1954 

No. 10— Shri M. K. Kundu, officiating Banking Officer, 
Reserve Bank of India, Department of Banking Operations, 
Delhi reverted as Staff Assistant with effect from the close 
of business on 10th May 1954. 

2. On return from leave Shri T. K. N. Panikker is posted 
as Assistant Accountant, Reserve Bank of India, Madras 
with effect from the close of business on 18th May 1954 
vice Shri M. V. Ranganathan proceeded on leave. 

3. The services of Shri K. Varnasy, Deputy Chief Officer, 
Reserve Bank of India, Department of Banking Opera- 
tions, Bombay have been placed at the disposal of the 
Government of Assam, with effect from the close of busi- 
ness on 20th May 1954. 

4. Shri P. V. Ramachandran, Assistant Accountant, 
Reserve Bank of India, Exchange Control Department, 
Bombay proceeded on leave with effect from 24th May 
1954. 

5. On return from leave Shri S. D’Souza is reposted as 
Banking Officer, Reserve Bank of India, Department of 
Banking Operations. Trivandrum with effect from 31st 
May 1954. 

6. Shri M. K. Kundu is reappointed to officiate as Bank- 
ing Officer, Reserve Bank of India, Department of Banking 
Operations, Delhi with effect from 1st June 1954 vice Shri 
Jogindra Singh proceeded on leave. 

7. Shri P. G. Mendonce is appointed to officiate as Assist- 
ant Accountant, Reserve Bank of India, Exchange Control 
Department, Bombay with effect from 2nd June 1954. 

8. On return from leave Shri N. B. Sen is reposted as 
Assistant Accountant, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt 
Office, Calcutta with effect from the close of business on 
2nd June 1954 vice Shri R. N. Mozumdar proceeded on 
leave. 

9. On return from leave Shri D. K. Damle is posted as 
Assistant Accountant, Reserve Bank of India, Exchange 
Control Department, Calcutta with effect from the close 
of business on 4th June 1954 vice Shri S. K. Dutta Gupta 
reverted as Superintendent. 

10. On return from leave Shri P. S. Mokashi is reposted 
as Deputy Chief Officer, Reserve Bank of India, Depart- 
ment of Banking Operations, Bombay with effect from 
11th June 1954. 

11. On return from leave Shri T. K. Ramasubramaniam 
is reposted as Chief Officer, Reserve Bank of India, Depart- 
ment of Banking Development, Bombay with effect from 
14th June 1954 vice Shri M. S. Nadkarni reposted as 
Deputy Chief Officer. 

N. SUNDARESAN 

Deputy Governor
