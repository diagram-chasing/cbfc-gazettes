2. Applicability : 

These Regulations shall apply to all employees of the 
Corporation regularly appointed under Regulation 7(1) of 
the Food Corporation of India (Stalf) Regulations, 1971, 
but shall not apply to : — 

(a) persons employed on Casual or part time basis; 

(b) Persons employed on special contract; 

(c) employees appointed on an ad hoc basis under Rgu- 
lation 7(3) of the Food Corporation of India 
(Staff) Regulations; 1971, 

(d) Employees who have been permitted to opt for the 
retirement benefit schemes of the Central Govern- 
ment or of any body other than the Food Corpo- 
ration of India and have so opled; and 

(e) Employees who arc covered by the provisions of the 
Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972. 

4. Circumstances : 

(1) Gratuity may be granted in the following circum- 
stances, namely : — 

(a) on the termination of the employee’s services 
under Regulation 19 of the Food Corporation 
of India (staff) Regulations, 1971; 

(b) on retirement under Regulations 22 of the 
Food Corporation of India (staff) Regulations, 
1971; and 

(c) on death, 

(2) Gratuity shall not be admissible to an employee 
who has resigned from the service of the Corpora- 
tion or who has-been discharged from service during 
his period of probation or whose services have been 
terminated an a measure of disciplinary action, 

(3) Except in the case of death, gratuity will be admis- 
sible to an employee only after completion of 5 
year’s qualifying service in the Corporation. 

Explanation 1. — Qualifying service means service render- 
ed to the Corporation by an employee after com- 
pletion of 18 years of age except period of service 
rendered as an apprentice and extra-ordinary leave 
without any leave salary.

the Central Government, In exercise of the powers con-

objectives.” 

The Interim Report of ICCAP in February 1976 has recom- 
mended the formation of an International Federation of 
Accountants (1FAC). If the conclusion of the bodies to 
whom the Report has been sent is positive, the recommen- 
dation will be submitted to the meeting of the heads of dele- 
gations at the Eclevenlh International Congress in Munich 
in October 1977. Ail affirmative note at that meeting will 
result in the establishment of IFAC in place of ICCAP.

(1ASC) has so far finalised and promulgated accounting 
standards for the following : 

Disclosure of Accounting Policies. 

* Valuation and Presentation of Inventories in the con- 
text of Historical Cost System. 

* Consolidated Financial Statements. 


The drafts' which are now exposed for comments and for 
which standards arc expected to be promulgated before long 
are on the following ; 

E4 — Depreciation Accounting in the Context of the His- 
torical Cost System. 

E5 — Information to be disclosed in Financial Statements. 

E6 — Accounting Treatment of Changing Prices. 

E7 -Statement of Source and Application of Funds. 
RELATIONSHIP WITH SISTER INSTITUTES 

The Committees that were constituted to negotiate with the 
Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and the Institute 
of Company Secretaries of India have been continuing their 
dialogues for exploring possibilities of closer co-operation 
between these professional bodies. The agreement which was 
earlier reached with the Institute of Company Secretaries of 
India had to be reviewed and revised due to subsequent 
changes in the syllabus of that Institute. The revised agree- 
ment is now in the process of implementation. 

PUBLIC RELATIONS 

Continuous elforts are being made to publicise the poten- 
tiality of cost and management, accounting in all economic 
activities. Apart from circulation of literature in concerned 
circles, personal contacts are also being made from time to 
lime both at central and regional levels. While it is not 
possible to measure the achievements in absolute terms, the 
Council is of the view that the results have been quite satis- 
factory.
