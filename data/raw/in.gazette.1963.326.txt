DIRECTIONS REGARDING MANUFACTURE OF ORNAMENTS BY 

OUTWORKERS 

S.O. 2954. — In pursuance of sub-rule (1) of rule 120T of the Defence of India 
Rules, 1962, the Administrator hereby issues the following directions for carrying 
out the purposes of Part XIIA of the said rules, in so far as they relate to the 
manufacture of ornaments not exceeding 14 carats in purity by outworkers on 
behalf of dealers licensed under the said rules, namely: — 

1. In these directions, the word "rule” or "rules”, wherever It occurs, shall 
denote the rule or rules in Part XIIA of the Defence of India Rules, 1962, and the 
expression "proper officer” shall mean the officer of Central Excise having 
jurisdiction over the area in which the dealer’s place of business is situated. 



7 5 THK GAZETTE OF INDIA : OCTOBER 19, 1963/ASVINA 27, 1885 [Part II- 


2. For the purposes of these directions, an out-worker shall be deemed to be a 
person who, not being a certified goldsmith,— 

(a) undertakes, for a consideration, the work of manufacture of ornaments 

as defined in clause (f) of rule 126A on behalf of any dealer or dealers 
licensed under the rules, outside the licensed premises; and 

(b) whose name and address have been furnished in the application for 

licence in Form Gold Series No, 1 submitted by any such dealer or is 
duly notified in writing to the proper officer thereafter: 

Provided that if any outworker becomes at any time a certified goldsmith, he 
shall not be entitled to receive any gold from any licensed dealer or dealers in 
pursuance of these directions, 

3. Every dealer licensed under the rules, who has furnished particulars of 
outworkers attached to him in the application for Licence in Form Gold Series 
No. 1 or does so thereafter and who intends to get ornaments manufactured by 
such out-workers, shall arrange for a copy of these directions to be supplied or 
read out to every such outworker and shall obtain from him an undertaking 
(which may be either in writing by the outworker or accepted by him by affixing 
his thumb impression) agreeing to the terms and conditions imposed by these 
directions and shall, in addition, maintain a ledger in Form A annexed to these 
directions. 

4. A dealer shall not issue ornaments or any other articles in finished form to 
an outworker for being polished or converted into other ornaments or articles 
and shall not issue to an outworker primary gold- 

fa) of a purity in excess of 14 carats; and 

(b) of a quantity In excess of the quantity actually required for manu- 
facture of ornament or ornaments or pieces of ornament or ornaments 
for which an order is placed with him, provided, however, that an 
outworker Bhall not at any time have in his possession, custody or 
control any quantity of primary gold in excess of 100 grammes 
belonging to any one dealer. 

6. No issue of primary gold shall be made to an outworker until the previous 
supply has been satisfactorily accounted for. 

6. Each outworker shall be given by the dealer a Pass Book in Form B 
annexed to these directions, in which each page shall be assigned a printed serial 
number and bear the stamp of the proper officer. 

7. The Pass Books issued by a dealer to his outworkers shall be assigned a 
running serial number and a record thereof shall be maintained in Form C annexed 
to these direc lions, 

8. The Pass Bok shall at all times remain in the custody of the outworker until 
it is resumed by the dealer for one or more of the following reasons; namely;— 

(a) the grant of a certificate to the outworker recognising him as a certified 

goldsmith; 

(b) the cessation of work by the outworker for any other cause whatsoever; 

(c) the termination of relationship between the dealer and the outworker; 

(d) the issue of instructions by a proper officer withdrawing the facilities 

available under these directions, so far as any outworker is concerned; 

(e) exhaustion of the Pass Book necessitating the issue of a new one. 

Whenever a Pass Book is resumed for any of the reasons mentioned at (a) to 
(dl above, the primary gold issued to and in the custody of the outworker shall be 
obtained from him by the dealer. 

All Pass Books shall be preserved by the dealer for a period of not less than 
five calendar years, following the one in which the pass book is resumed or 
replaced and shall be available for inspection. 

9. Whenever an outworker ceases to work on behalf of a dealer or his relation- 
ship with a dealer is terminated, an Intimation to that effect shall be furnished by 
the dealer to the proper officer within three days of such cessation or termination.

10. The dealer shall be responsible for making entries in the Pass Book 
immediately on completion of each transaction relating to the issue of primary 
gold to, or receipt of primary gold from, the outworker. The dealer shall not 
deliver any primary gold to an outworker, unless the Pass Book has been produced 
by the outworker and all its relevant columns have been duly filled in. 


11. An outworker working for more than one dealer shall— 

(1) keep the stock of primary gold received by him from each such dealer 
and the ornaments made from that stock separately from the stock 
of primary gold received by him from any other dealer and the orna- 
ments made from such primary gold and 

(ii) ensure that the total quantity of primary gold received by him from all 
dealers and not accounted for in the form of ornaments returned to 
such dealers shall not be in excess of 250 grammes at any time.

Central Government.

ment) Rules, 1963.

following rules shall be inserted, namely:—

Pension) Rules, 1903.
