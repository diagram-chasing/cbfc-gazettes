CUM -RETIREMENT GRATUITY ) REGULATIONS, 

1967 
In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 45 of 
the Food Corporations Act, 1964 ( 37 of 1964 ) and with 
the previous sanction of the Central Government, the 
Food Corporation of India makes the following Regulu 
tions , namely : 
1. Short till , and co :nmencement. 
( 1 ) These Regulations may be called the Food Cor 

poration of India (Death -cuin -Retirement Grt 

tuity ) Regulatiors, 1967 . 
( 2 ) They shall come into force with effect from the 

1st Janugry , 1966 
2 . Applicability 

These Regulations shall apply to all employees of the 
Corporation regularly appointed under Regulation 12 ( 1 ) 
of the Draft Food Corporation of India ( Staff ) Regula 
tions, 1965 , but shall not apply to : 

( a ) Persons employed on a casual or part- time basis ; 
(b ) Persons emploved on special contract; 
( c ) Employees appointed on an ad hoc basis under 

Regulation 12 ( 4 ) of the Draft Food Corpora 

tion of India ( Staft ) Regulations, 1965 ; and 
( d ) Employees who have been permitted to opt for 

the retirement benefit schemes of the Central 
Government or of any body other than the 

Food Corporation of India and have so opted . 
3 . Conditions 

A gratu ty not exceeding the amounts mentioned in 
Regulation 5 , may be paid to an employee or to the 
other person or persons on whom the right to receive the 
gratuity is conferred under Regulation 7 , for good , effi 
cent and faithful service of such employee 

4 . Circunıstances 

( 1) Gratuity may be granted in the following cir 
cumstances, namely : 
( a ) on the termination of rhe employees service 

uuder Regulation . 22 of the Draft Food Corpo 

ration of India (Staff ) Regulations, 1965; 
(b ) on retirement under Regulation 24 of the Draft 

Food Corporation of India ( Staff ) Regulations , 

1965 ; and 
( c ) on death . 
( 2 ) Gratuity shall not be admissible to an employee 
who has resigned from the service of the Corporation or 
who has been discharged from service during his period 
of probation or whose services have been terminated as 
a measure of d sciplinary action . 

( 3 ) Except in the case of death , gratuity will be ad 
missible to an employee only after completion of 5 years 
qualifying service in the Corporation . 
Explanation 1 : Qualifying service means service render 

ed in the Corporation by an employee 
after completion of 18 years of age ex 
cept period of service rendered as an ap 
prentice and extraordinary leave without 

any leave salary . 
Explanation 2 : Service rendered in the Government or 

any public or private undertaking by an 
employee before his joining service of 
the Corporation may be declared by the 
Managing Director to be deemed , in whole 
or in part to be qualify ng service in the 
Corporation provided that there was no 
break in service before joining the Cor 
poration .

( 3 ) The amount of gratuity payable in case of death 
in accordance with the scales laid down in sub -Regulation 
( 2 ) shall be reduced by the amount of the contributions 
made by the Corporation together with interest thereon 
standing to the credit of the employee in the Contributory 
Provident Fund Account maintained in accordance with 
the provisions of the Food Corporation of India (Contri 
butory Provident Fund ) Regulations, 1967. 

(4 ) ‘Emoluments for the purpose of these Regulations 
shall mean the average monthly pay including special pay , 
leave salary and subsistance grant drawing during the 36 
months preceding the date of quitting service and shall be 
subject to a maximum of Rs. 1,800 per month . 

6 Nominations 

( 1 ) Every employce shall make a nomination on com 
pletion of 5 years of service in such one of the forms A 
to D given in Annexure as may be appropriate conferring 
on one or more persons of his family , the right to receive 
the gratuity in the event of death while in service or 
after quitting service but before the actual payment of 
the gratuity is made, indicating the shares payable to each 
member . In case of an employee having no family , the 
nomination may be made in favour of a person or persons 
or a body of persons incorporated or not. A nomination 
made under this Sub -Regulation shall be sent to the AC 
counts Officer in the Head Office . 

( 2 ) For the purpose of the Regulations family shall 
include the following : 

( i) wife in the case of a male employee; 
( ii ) husband in the case of a female employee ; 
( iii ) Son ; 
( iv ) unmarried daughter; 

(v ) widowed daughter ; 
(vi) brothers below the age of 18 years , and un 

married and widowed sisters; 
( vii ) father; 
( viii ) Mother ; 
( ix ) Married daughter ; 
( x ) Children of a pre -deceased son ; and 
(xi) widow of a pre -deccased son . 


NOTE : Son and Daughter shall include step -sons and 

adopted sons and step -daughters and adopted 
daughters respectively ; and brother and ‘ sister 
shall include step -brother and step - sister respec 

tively . 
( 3 ) An employee may at any time cancel the nomina 
tion by sending a notice in writing to the Accounts Offi 
cer in the Head Office provided that the employee along 
with such notice, sends a fresh nomination made in accor 
dance with Sub - Regulation ( 1 ) . 

( 4 ) Every nomination made and every notice of cancel 
lation given by an employee , to the extent that it is valid , 
shall take effect on the date on which it is received by 
the Accounts Officer . 

7 . Persons to whom the gratuity is payable 

( 1 ) Except in cases where the gratuity becomes payable 
on death , or, having become otherwise payable , the em 
ployee dies before it is actually paid , the gratuity shall be 
payable to the employee himself . 

( 2 ) In the event of death while in service or after quit 
ting service but before the actual payment of gratuity is 
made , the gratuity shall be payable to the person or per 
sors or body of persons nominated to receive such gratuity 
in accordance with Regulation 6 . 

( 3 ) In the event of the contingency referred to in Sub 
Regulation ( 2 ) and there being no nomination , the gra 
tuity will be paid in the manner indicated below : 
(a ) If there are one or more surviving members of 

the family as given in ( i) to ( iv ) of sub -Regula 
tion ( 2 ) of Regulation 6 , it may be paid to all 

such members — in equal shares; and 
(b ) if there are no such surviving members of the 

family , but there are one or more surviving 
members of the family as given in (v ) to ( xi ) 
of Sub -Regulation ( 2 ) of Regulation 6 . the 
gratuity may be paid to all such members in 

equal shares. 
(4 ) In the event of the contingency referred to in 
Sub - Regulation (2 ) and there is neither a valid nomi 
nation nor any relation constituting a family within the 
meaning of Sub -Regulation ( 2 ) of Regulation 6 the gra 
tuity that becomes payable shall be credited to the funds 
of the Corporation , and become the property thereof . 


8 . Competent authorities 


The competent authority for sanctioning a gratuity 
under these Regulations shall be as follows : 
(a ) If the amount of gratuity does 
not exceed Rs. 10 ,000 

Secretary 
( b ) If the amount of gratuity exceeds 
Rs . 10 ,000 

Managing Director 


9 . Interpretation 

If any doubt arises relating to the interpretation of 
these Regulations, the matter shall be referred to the 
Managing Director, whose decision thereof shall be final.
