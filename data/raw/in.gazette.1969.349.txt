wrote a post card dated the 21st November, 1966 (Ex. E.4) to the company explaining

7- Tho workman returned to Revdenda on 21st November, 1966 from Bombay at

8. The workman went to tho office on 29th November, 1966 for getting his salary.

leave to that extent was not to his credit at that time. The workman was given company’s 
letter dated 28th November, 1966 (vide Ex. E-6). 


9. On 7th December, 1966, a departmental enquiry was held in the presence of the 
workman by Shri Shah. Chief Accountant of tho company (vide enquiry papers Ex. E-7 
to II) Tho enquiry was completed on tho same day. The Enquiry Officer submitted 
his re^it (Ex E-11) on 30th December, 1966 holding that the charge of remaining
