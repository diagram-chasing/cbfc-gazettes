STATE BANK OF HYDERABAD 

NOTIFICATION 

Hyderabad- A.P., the 3rd May 1963 

No. SBH/GN/1963/15 — The following Notification is 
hereby issued: 

The Board of Directors ol the State Bank of Hydera- 
bad at its meeting held on the 8th April 1963 resolved 
that the Cfficers posted at the Head Office of the Bank to 
assist the Heads of Departments be designated as a rule 
by reference to the designations of the respective Heads 
of Departments. Accordingly the present designations 
of the following officials of Head Office will stand renam- 
ed as under: — 

(i) The Assistants in the Advances Department, 
Head Office will be designated as “Asstt, Superin- 
tendent, Advances Department”. 

(li) The Assistant in the Staff Department will be 
designated as “Asstt. Superintendent, Staff De- 
partment”. 

(iii) The Assistant in the Premises, Dead Stock & 
Stationery Department will be designated as 
“Asstt, Superintendent, Premises, Dead Stock & 
Stationery Department”. 

(iv) The Assistant in the Development Department 
will be designated as ‘‘Asstt. Development Offi- 
cer''. 

(v) The Assistant in the Legal Department will be 
designated as “Asstt. Law Officer”. 

By order of the Board 

SATYA DEV 
General Manager

Calcutta 16, the 13 th May 1963 

No. CWR(2) / 63 — The following draft of certain amend- 
ments to the Cost and Works Accountants Regulations, 
1959, which it is proposed to make in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (3) of Section 
39 of the Cost and Works Accountants Act, 1959 (Act No. 
23 of 1959), is published for information of all persons 
likely to be affected thereby and notice is hereby given 
that the draft will be taken up for consideration on or 
after the 15th June 1963. 

Any objection or suggestion which may be received 
from any person with respect to the said draft before 
the date specified will be considered by the Council of 
the Institute of Cost and Works Accountants of India. 

In the said Regulations: 

I. In Regulation 141, for the sentence “Twenty mem- 
bers shall form the quorum for a meeting”, the follow- 
ing shall be substituted, namely, “Ten members shall 
form the quorum for a meeting”. 

II. In Form I of the Schedule to the said Regulations, 
after Item 6 and before paragraph 2, the following shall 
be inserted, namely, 

“7. In case the applicant was previously admitted as 
a Registered Student of the Institute or of the 
dissolved company, and if at any time such Re- 
gistration was cancelled, discontinued or other- 
wise terminated — 

(a) The date of previous Registration. 

(b) The former Registration Number. 

(c) The reasons for cancellation, discontinuance, 
etc. 

III. In Form I of the Schedule to the said Regulations, 
for the existing paragraph 2, the following shall be subs- 
tituted, namely, 

“I hereby declare that the particulars furnished 
above are true to the best of my knowledge and 
belief and should it at any time be proved that 
the said particulars were untrue, I agree to my 
registration being cancelled without any obliga- 
tion on the part of the Institute to refund any fee 
or subscription paid by me to the Institute. I 
also hereby undertake that if enrolled as a Re- 
gistered Student of the Institute, I will be bound 
by the provisions of the Cost and Works Account- 
ants Act, 1959, and the Regulations framed there- 
under or that may hereafter from time to time 
be made pursuant to the said Act.” 

The 16 th May 1963 

No. OWR(3)/63 — The following draft of certain amend- 
ments to the Cost and Works Accountants Regulations, 
1959, which it is proposed to make in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (3) of Section 
39 of the Cost and Works Accountants Act 1959 (Act No. 
23 of 1959), is published for information of all persons 
likely to be affected thereby and notice is hereby given 
that the draft will be taken up for consideration on or 
after the 20th June 1963. 

Any objection or suggestion which may be received 
from any person with respect to the said draft before 
the date specified will be considered by the Council of 
the Institute of Cost and Works Accountants of India. 

In the said Regulations: 

I. In Regulation 31 for the paragraph beginning with 
the heading “ Examination under Regulation 32(3)” and 
ending with the words “Examination in all Groups toge- 
ther — Rs. 40”, the following shall be substituted, namely: 

“Examination under Regulation 32(3) 

Examination in or exemption from any one 
Group ... ... ... Rs. 17 

Examination in all Groups together ... Rs. 45” 


II. In Regulation 34 for the paragraph beginning with 
the heading “Examination under Regulation 35(3)” and 
ending with the words “All Groups together — Rs. 60”, the 
following shall be substituted, namely: 

“Examination under Regulation 35(3) 

Any one Group .. .... .. Rs. 27 

All Groups together .. ... Rs. 65” 

III. For the existing sub-regulation (1) of Regulation 41, 
the following shall be substituted, namely: 

“(1) A list of successful candidates in an examination 
shall be published in the Journal of the Institute 
in such manner as the Council may direct. The 
names of candidates obtaining distinction in the 
examination shall be indicated in the list. Each 
candidate shall be individually informed of his 
result and also of the marks obtained by him in 
the paper or papers of the examination in which 
he appeared.” 

IV. In sub-regulation (5) of Regulation 41, after the 
existing proviso to that sub-regulation, the following se- 
cond proviso shall be added, namely: 

“Provided further that this sub-regulation shall not 
apply in respect of the examinations to be held 
after December 1963.”
