position and tender advice in regard to progressive use of

TENURE 

The tenure of members of the Committee will ordinarily 
be three years from the date of constitution of the Committee 
provided:— 

1. Members of the Parliament, who are members of 
the Committee shall cease to be members of the 
Committee as soon as they cease to be members of 
Parliament, 

2. The ex-officio members of the Committee shall 
continue members so long as they hold office by 
virtue of which they are members of the Committee 
and, 


3. If a vacancy arises in the Committee due to 
resignation or death of a member, the appointee to 
that vacancy shall hold office for the residual period 
of the tenure of three years.
