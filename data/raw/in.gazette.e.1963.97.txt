Calcutta- 16, the 22 nd November 1963 

No. 39-CWM(18)/63—In exercise of the powers con¬ 
ferred by Sub-section (1) of Section 39 of tne Cost and 
Works Accountants Act, 1959 (Act No. 23 of 1959), 
the Council of the Institute of Cost and Works Account¬ 
ants of India has made the following amendments in 
the Cost and Works Accountants Regulations, 1959, the 
■same having been previously published and approved by 
the Central Government as required by Sub-section (3) 
•of the said Section : 

In the said Regulations : 

I. In sub-clause (i) of sub-regulation (4) of Regula¬ 
tion 32, the following words shall be deleted, namely : 

“not more than four calendar years before the com¬ 
mencement of the examination.” 

II. In sub-clause (ii) of sub-regulation (4) of Regula¬ 
tion 35, the following words shall be deleted namely : 

"not more than five calendar years before the com¬ 
mencement of the examination.” 

III. For the existing sub-regulation (1) of Regula¬ 
tion 41, the following shall be substituted, namely : 

"(I) A list of successful candidates in an 
examination shall be published in the Journal 
of the Institute in such manner as the Council 
may direct. The names of candidates obtaining 
distinction in the examination shall be indicat¬ 
ed in the list. Each candidate shall be indivi¬ 
dually Informed of his result and also of the 
marks obtained by him in the paper or papers 
of the examination in which he appeared : 

Provided that in any case where it is found 
that the result of an examination has been 
affected by error, malpractice, fraud, improper 


conduct, or in any other way, the Examination 
Committee shall have the power suitably to 
amend such result and to make such declara¬ 
tion as the Committee considers necessary.” 

IV. In sub-regulation (5) of Regulation 41, after the 
existing proviso to that sub-regulation, the following 
second proviso shall be added, namely : 

“Provided further that this sub-regulation 
shall not apply in respect of the examinations 
to be held after December 1963.” 

V. The existing Regulation 50 shall be marked as sub¬ 
regulation (1) of Regulation 50 and the following sub¬ 
regulation shall be added as sub-regulation (2) of Regu¬ 
lation 50, namely : 

“(2) Information as to whether a candidate’s 
answers in any particular paper or papers of 
any examination have been examined and valu¬ 
ed will be supplied to the candidate on his 
forwarding within a month of the declaration 
of the said examination results, an application 
accompanied by a fee of Rs. 10 for any or all 
papers. The fee is only for verifying whether 
a candidate’s answers in any particular paper 
or papers have been examined and valued, and 
not for the revaluation of answers. The marks 
obtained by the candidates in individual ques¬ 
tions or in sections of a paper cannot, in any 
circumstances, be supplied. If as a result of 
such verification, it is discovered that there has 
been either an omission to examine or value 
any answer or answers or there has been a mis¬ 
take in the totalling of the marks, the fee for 
verification shall be refunded in full to the 
applicant.”
