MINISTRY OF INFORMATION & BROADCASTING 

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 14th June 1954 

S.B.0.1946.—In exercise of the power.s conferred by section 3 of the Cinema¬ 
tograph Act, 1962 (XXXVII of 1952) and rule 3 of the Cinematograph (Censor¬ 
ship) Rules, 1961, the Central Government hereby appoints until further orders 
Sri B. D. Mirchandani, I.C.S., Secretary to the Government of Bombay in the 
Home Department as a member of the Central Board of Film Censors and also to 
be the Chairman thereof in addition to his own duties with effect from the after¬ 
noon of 14th June, 1964, vice Sri C. M. Agarwala. 

[No. 11/4/54-P.C.] 
D. KRISHNA AYYAR, Under Secy.
