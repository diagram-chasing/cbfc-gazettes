Explanation 

Ihe terms ’producer' and ‘director’ used herein will be 
construed as referring to the producer or director, as the

certi^ed by the Central Board of Piim Censors. 

2. (2) Government may, at its discretion, give the cash 
prizes in the form of National Defence Certificates and

3. The Awards will be made annually on a date to be 
fixed by Government. 

4. All Indian films certified for public exhibition by the 
Central Board of Film Censors and all filmstrips certified by 
the producers as produced in the preceding calendar year will 
be eligible for entry, which may be made by the producer or 
any other duly authorised person in the Form contained in

Provided that a film entered as a Children’s Film shall not

Officer of the Central Board of Film Censors or to any other

(i) a print of the film or the filmstrip as the case may 
be; and 

(li) a copy of the synopsis, together with an English 
rendering thereof and a copy of the songs, if any, 
together with their free translation in English or 
Hmdi. 

The entrant of a film selected for viewing by the Central 
Committee will submit at his cost the specified number of 
copies of the detailed dialogue script in English or Hindi as 
well as the publicity matenol relating thereto in addition to 
the print and synopsis of the film, 

6. The decision of the Government of India whether a film 
is eligible to be entered for the Awards and whether any film 
is a feature film, documentary film, educational film or 
children’s films for the purpose of entry for the Awards will 
be final. 

The maximum length of a children’s film for the purpose 
of entry for the Awards shall be 3400 metres in 35 mm or 
1360 metres in 16 mm*

not be eligible for consideration for the Awards. The Pro-

may enter only one of the versions of that film. 

8. Entries for the Awards will be invited by Govermnont 
during a period to be notified, relaxablc in exception^ cases

9. All transport costs on the consignment and return of the

submitted, it cannot accept responsibility for loss or damage

Hindi (including Urdu and Hindustani) Marathi, Punjabi,

aesthetic and technical standard of the films.

npto five feature films which have a high and aesthetic and
