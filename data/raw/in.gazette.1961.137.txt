MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi 2, the 15 th May 1961 

No, 4/4/60-FI — The rules of the International Film 
Festival of India, 1961, are hereby published for public 
information, namely : — 

Pules 

1, The Festival is organised by the Government of 
India with the cooperation of the Film Federa- 
tion of India. 

2. The object of the Festival is to provide a forum 
for the participating countries to present films of 
artistic and cultural value and a high technical 
standard and contribute to the development of 
motion picture art and technique. 

•3. The Festival will be held in New Delhi from 27th 

October to 2nd November 1961, in Calcutta from 
1st to 7th November, in Madras from 6th to 12th 
November and in Bombay from 11th to 17th 
November 1961. 

4. The Festival will be non-competitive, Souvenirs 
will be presented in respect of films accepted for 
participation in the Festival through the official 
delegation 

5. Films will be admitted for presentation in the 
following categories, namely : — 

(a) Feature films 

(b) Short films, including documentary, informa- 
tional, educational and scientific 61ms, cartoon 
films, puppet films etc. 

6. Only films in 35mm width will be eligible for 
participation. 

7. Each participating country will be entitled to 
send a maximum of two films in each of the two 
aforesaid categories, viz., feature and shorts The 
length of shorts entered bv a country should not 
exceed, 1,500 metres in all. 

8. An entry may be made by the Government or 
alternatively by the representative film organisa- 
tion of the country. The entry will be made in 
Form ‘A’ hereto annexed. 

9. Only such films as have been produced or released 
on or after 1st January 1960, will "be eligible for 
entry. Films in languages other than English or an 
Indian language, must have sub-titles in English 

or Hindi. 

10. Films entered in the Festival should not have 
been shown in India prior to the Festival. • There 
Is no bar to the entry of a film shown at a festival 
in any other country. 

11. Each participating country should notify its in- 
tention to participate in the Festival to the 
Festival Directorate before 15th May 1961. 
Entries to the Festival should reach the Festival 
Directorate by 1st July 1961. A print of the film 
entered, along with two copies of its synopsis 
and other publicity material in English or Hindi, 
should' reach the Festival Directorate by 1st 
August 1961. 

12. The Festival Directorate will be responsible for 

the viewing and selection of films for presenta- 
tion at the Festival. 

13. Each participating country may be represented 
by a delegation. Delegates, not exceeding twm 
from each country— one of them shall be a promi- 
nent artiste— will be the guests of the Festival 
Directorate. Expenses on the board and lodging 
of the guest delegates as also on their travel in 
India will be borne by the Festival Directorate. 


The travel expenses from their country to India 
and back will, however, be borne by the delegates 
themselves. Particulars of the delegates attend- 
ing the Festival should be communicated to the 
Festival Directorate by 1st September 1961, in 
Form ‘B’ hereto annexed. 

14. Transportation and insurance of prints and 
publicity material of films from the country of 
origin to the Festival and back will be at the ex- 
pense of the entrant. 

15. Prints of films will be accepted at the risk of the 
participants and, while the Festival Directorate 
will take every reasonable care thereof, it cannot 
accept responsibility for the loss of or damage to 
the prints while in its possession. 

16. The print of films entered in the Festival, publi- 
city material and all correspondence relating 
thereto should be addressed to: — 

The International Film Festival Directorate, 
Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, 
Government of India, 

New Delhl-2. ‘ 

Telegraphic address: “Information”, New Delhi. 

17. Participation in the Festival will be regarded as 
acceptance of these rules. 

18. The Festival Directorate will decide on all mat- 
ters not expressly provided for in these rules and 
its decisions thereon shall be final. The Directo- 
rate reserves the right to make a relaxation, in 
special circumstances, from any of the provisions 
of these rules in any individual case, with the 
approval of Government.
