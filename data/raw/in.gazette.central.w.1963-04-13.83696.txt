PART 1 


Sanitary requlrements.. 

1 . The premises where the cold storage is situated shall be clean and ade 
quately lighted . It shall be cleaned , if so required by the Licensing Officer or 
any officer authorised by him , by lime washing or washing or painting or dis 
infecting or de -adourising . 

2 . The holder of the licence shall not store any product ( s ) which will be 
repugnant or alien to the smell of the foodstuff ( s ) stored in the cold storage. 
The name ( s ) of the foodstuff ( s ) stored in a particular cold storage shall be 
exhibited prominently on a board fixed at the entrance of the cold storage . 

3. Adequate exhaust arrangement shall be provided to expel the stale air 
from the cold storage. 

4 . All yards , out houses , stores, and all approaches of the premises where 
the cold storage is installed shall be kept clean and sanitary , 

5 . No person suffering from any infectious or contagious disease shall be 
allowed to work in the cold storage. Arrangements shall be made for exami 
nation of the employees working in the Cold Storage once a year to check that 
they are free from any infectious , contagious, or other diseases and they shall 
be inoculated and vaccinated against the enteric group of diseases once a year 
and against small -pox once in two years and a certificate, thereof shall be kept 
for inspection . In casc of any epidemic, all such employees shall be inoculated . 

6 . The employees working in the cold storage shall be provided with proper 
clothes and boots to protect them from cold . 


PART 2 
Requirements as to operation . 

1. The doors of the cold storage shall be provided with adequate insulation 
and suitable border gaskets , The design of the door ( ) shall be such that R 
person must be able to open it from inside as well as outside.

2 . Wherever racks are provided , they shall be so constructed that loading 
and unloading operation shall be carried out without endangering the life of 
the employees working in the cold storage. 

3 . The passage between the racks shall not be less than 76 metrcz. 

4 . The diffusers or direct expansion pipes for conveying the refrigerant shall 
be so arranged in the cold storage so as to maintain proper and uniform tem 
perature and humidity in every location of the cold storage where foodstuffs are 
stored. 

5 . Suction lines from diffuser ( s ) or other direct expansion coil ( s ) leading

6 . The Cold Storage shall be provided with thermometers rcading dry bulb

inspection after personal verification of the records. 

2 . Any stoppage of function of the cold storage shall be reported immediately

down shall be recorded in the Log Book and reported to the licensing officer .
