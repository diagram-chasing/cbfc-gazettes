cmployer by way of contribution during any previous year shall be the deductible 
allowance . 

3 . One- Afth of such deductible allowance shall be allowed in the assessment 
year relating to the previous year in which the amount was actually paid and the 
balance of the deductible allowance shall he allowed in equal instalments for each 
the four immediately succeeding assessment years .
