THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF 
INDIA 

New Delhi-110002, the 23rd October 1979 
(Chartered Accountants) 

No. l-CA(116)/79. — -The following draft of certain amend- 
ments to the Chartered Accountants Regulations, 1964. which 
it is proposed to make in exercise of the powers conferred 
by sub-sections (lj and (3) of section 30 of the Chartered 
Accountants Act, 1949, (Act XXXVIII of 1949), is published 
for information of all persons likely to be affected thereby 
and notice is hereby given that the draft will be taken up 
for consideration on or after 30th November, 1979. 

Any objection or suggestion which may be received from 
any person with respect to the said draft before the date 
specified will be considered by the Council of the Institute 
of Chartered Accountants of India, New Delhi. 

In the said Regulations : — 

1. For the existing Regulation 40. substitute the follow- 
ing : — 

“40 Fresh Articles 

(1) Where an articled clerk is not able to complete the 
term of the article by reason of the fact that (a) 
the employer has ceased to practise; or^(b) the name 
of the employer has been removed from the Re- 
gister; or (c) the employer has died; or (d) the 
articles are terminated under sub-regulation (1) of 
regulation 42, he may enter into fresh articles for 
the remainder term of his service, with another 
member entitled to engage and train articled clerks : 

Provided that the Secretary may, in an appropriate 
case covered by category (a), (b) or (c) above, per- 
mit the articled clerk to he trained as an additional 
articled clerk by a member entitled to engage and 
train articled clerk/s notwithstanding any thing con- 
tained in regulation 29. 

(2) Where an articled clerk is not able to complete the 
term of articles for any other valid reason, he mav, 
with the permission of the President, enter into 
fresh articles for the remainder of the term of ser- 
vice with anotlier member entitled to engage and 
train articled clerk/s : 

Provided that the President may, in any appro- 
priate case, permit the articled clerk to be trained 
as an additional articled clerk by a member entitled 
to engage and train articled clerk /s, notwithstand- 
ing anything contained in regulation 29. 

(3) In every case referred to in sub-regulation (1) or sub - 
regulation (2) above, the provisions of regulation 


34B shall apply mutatis mutandis except that no 
fee shall be payable by the articled clerk : 

Provided that in a case covered under category 
(c) of sub-regulation (1), the date of commence- 
ment of training under fresh articles shall be taken 
as the date following the date of the death of the 
employer, if, the [request for permission to ' be 
taken as additional articled clerk under another em- 
ployer is sent so as to reach the Secretary within 
60 days of the death of the employer and the state- 
ment in the appropriate form No. 16A is sent so 
as to reach the Secretary within 30 days from the 
date of the letter of the Secretary granting such per- 
mission.” 

2. In Regulation 178, for the existing clause (16), substi- 
tute the following 

“(16) Registration and noting suspension/cancellation/ 
termination of articles and/or audit service and 
permitting an articled clerk in cases covered by 
categories (a), (b) or (c) of sub-regulation (1) of 
Regulation 40 to be trained as an additional articled 
clerk by a member entitled to engage and train arti- 
cled clerk/s, notwithstanding anything contained in 
regulation 29.” 

P. S. GOPALAKRISHNAN, Secy.
