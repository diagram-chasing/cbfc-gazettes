AWARD 
This is a complaint under Section 33A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947. 

2 . The complainants allege that they were working in the Bhadwa incline in . 
the Serampore Colliery (Giridih ) when they were discharged without the per 
mission of this Tribunal, though Reference No. 6 of 1952 was pending between 
the opposite party and their workmen ; and that though the Government had 
decided to give retrenchment relief to the retrenched workers inclusive of the 
complainants , the opposite party had not paid any amount to them as retrench 
ment relief . It was further alleged that though vacancies occurred in other pits , 
the complainants had not been absorbed . It was therefore prayed that such orders 
as may be deemed fit and proper may be passed in the present case . 

3 . The opposite party denied the allegations made in the application but stated 
that it was willing to pay the retrenchment relief to the complainants at the same 
rates as were granted by this Tribunal in Application No. 61 of 1953. 


4 . It is an admitted fact that the complainants were working in the Bhadwa 
incline in Serampore Colliery which belongs to the State Railways. It appears : 
that the working of this incline was closed , with the result that all workmen 
working there were discharged . The complainants were among the persons who 
were so discharged . Dr. Gupta who appeared on their behalf fairly conceded . 
before me that he could not ask for reinstatement of the complainants , because he 
could not complain about their discharge, as due to the closure of the mine, there 
was no work for them . He, however , urged that they should have been given 
the benefits of gratuity , notice pay , etc , and work should also be given to them 
if new work was started at some other place . 


PART II — SEC. 3 ] 


THE GAZETTE OF INDIA , NOVEMBER 7, 1963 


1877 


5 . A similar application (being Application No. 61 of 1953 ) was made by some 
of the discharged workmen before me. That application was opposed by the 
opposite party on various grounds; but I held that the discharged workmen were 
entitled to notice pay , gratuity etc . and passed an award accordingly on 16th July 
1953. In the present case , the opposite party has filed a written statement 
agreeing to pay notice pay , gratuity etc . to the complainants at the same rates as 
were ordered in the above application . 

6 . Dr. Gupta made a further request that if and when new work was started 
these discharged workmen should be given preference . Mr. Sahgal, who was 
present in court on behalf of the opposite party , stated that the management would . 
have no objection to taking up the complainants in preference to others , provided 
the complainants agreed to accept the new jobs at the new rates as may be given 
to other workmen . Dr. Gupta on behalf of the complainants stated that the 
applicants would agree to take the new jobs at the new rates . 

7 . In the result, I pass the following award : 

The complainants should be paid notice pay , gratuity etc . as mentioned in para , 
3 of the letter from the Government of India , Ministry of Production , No. C . 2 - 14 
(5 ) /52, dated 26th December 1952, addressed to the Coal Commissioner , Calcutta . 
This should be done within one month from the date this award becomes enforce 
able . If and when the opposite party has occasion to engage new men for work 
( either due to retirement or due to opening of new works etc .) , preference should 
be given to the complainants according to their seniority , provided they agree to 
accept the new jobs at the new rates then offered by the management.

AWARD 
This is a complaint under Section 33A of the Industrial Disputes Act. 

2 . The complainants allege that they were working in the Bhadwa incline in 
the Serampore Colliery (Giridih ) till 31st March , 1953, when they were discharged 
without the permission of this Tribunal, though Reference No. 6 of 1952 was 
pending between the opposite party and their workmen ; and that though the 
Government had decided to give retrenchment relief to the retrenched workers 
inclusive of the complainants , the opposite party had not paid any amount to 
them as retrenchment relief . It was further alleged that though vacancies 


1878

[ PART II -- SEC. 3 


occurred in other pits, the complainants had not been absorbed . It was therefore 
prayed that such orders as may be deemed fit and proper may be passed in the 
present case. 

3 . The opposite party denied the allegations made in the application but stated 
that it was willing to pay the retrenchment relief to the complainants at the same 
rates as were granted by this Tribunal in Application No . 61 of 1953. 

4 . It is an admitted fact that the complainants were working in the Bhadwa 
incline in Serampore Colliery which belongs to the State Railways. It appears 
that the working of this incline was closed , with the result that all workmen 
working there were discharged . The complainants were among the persons who 
were so discharged . Dr. Gupta who appeared on their behalf fairly conceded 
before me that he could not ask for reinstatement of the complainants , because 
he could not complain about their discharge, as due to the closure of the mine, 
there was no work for them . He, however , urged that they should have been 
given the benefits of gratuity , notice pay, etc . and work should also be given to 
them if new work was started at some other place , 

5 . A similar application (being Application No. 61 of 1953 ) was made by somo 
of the discharged workmen before me. That application was opposed by the 
opposite party on various grounds ; but I held that the discharged workmen were 
entitled to notice pay , gratuity etc , and passed an award accordingly on 18th July , 
1953. In the present case , the opposite party has Aled a written statement agree 
ing to pay notice pay, gratuity etc . to the complainants at the same rates as were 
- ordered in the above application . 


6 . Dr. Gupta made a further request that if and when new work was started , 
these discharged workmen should be given preference. Mr. Sahgal, who was 
present in court on behalf of the opposite party , stated that the management 
would have no objection to taking up the complainants in preference to others, 
provided the complainants agreed to accept the new jobs at the new rates as may 
be given to other workmen Dr. Gupta on behalf of the complainants stated that 
the applicants would agree to take the new jobs at the new rates . 


7. In the result, I pass the following award : 

The complainants should be paid notice pay , gratuity etc . as mentioned in part 
3 of the letter from the Government of India , Ministry of Production , No. 0 . 2 - 14 
(5 ) / 52, dated 26th December 1952, addressed to the Coal Commissioner, Calcutta . 
This should be done within one month from the date this award becomes enforce 
able . If and when the opposite party has occasion to engage new men for work 
( either due to retirement or due to opening of new works etc . ) , preference should 
be given to the complainants according to their geniority , provided they agree to 
accept the new jobs at the new rates then offered by the management.

AWARD 
This is a complaint under section 33 . A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 . 

2 . This application was made by 60 workmen working in Pit No. 7 of South 
Bulliaree /Kendwadih Colliery belonging to the opposite party , They alleged 
that the opposite party had served notices upon them refusing work , wage etc . to 
them and further that the notices in language , spirit and effect amounted to clear 
contravention of section 33 of the Industrial Disputes Act , 1947 as they acted as 
bbove without obtaining the express permission of this Tribunal in spite of Refer 
ence No. 6 of 1952 ( to which the opposite party and their workmen are parties ) 
being pending before this Tribunal. They prayed that they should be granted 
full wages from the 10th May 1953 till the date of resumption of work . 

3. The opposite party denied that the notice issued by it had in any way altered . 
the conditions of the service of the complainants or that it came within the provi 
sions of section 33 of Industrial Disputes Act. It contended that due to the com 
pletion of de - pillaring operation in their pit No. 7 , the complainants and others . 
became surplus and the opposite party therefore decided to retrench them by 
giving them a week s notice . It therefore urged that the complaint should be 
dismissed . 

4 . In the meanwhile , the management had also filed an application under section 
33 of Industrial Disputes Act ( Application No . 93 of 1953 ) before this Tribunal 
requesting for permission to discharge 52 of their workmen ( all of whom are 
among the present 60 complainants ) . Both the applications were fixed for hearing 
together on the 30th September 1953 . At that time the management gave an 
application stating that the matter was annicably settled between the parties and 
they did not want to proceed with their application . Accordingly application No. 93 
of 1953 was disposed of . So far as the present application is concerned , no one 
appeared on behalf of the complainants on the date of hearing. But a letter was . 
sent by Mr. Kanti Mehta , who was authorised to represent the complainants , stat 
ing that the matter had been amicably settled between the parties and hence the 
complainants did not wish to proceed with the present complaint and wanted to 
withdraw the same. 

5 . In view of this letter , nothing remains to be done regarding the present 
complaint which is dismissed . I pass my award accordingly .
