9 . Under the Sastry Award which admittedly governs the parties it is clearly 
laid down in paragraph 178 sub -paragraph (4 ) (page 134 ) that " no leave cr exten 
sion of leave shall be decmed to have been granted unless an order to that cffect 
is passed and communicated to the employee concerned " . This direction of the 
award has been indisputably contravened . Eyen holding, but not accepting the 
truth of the petitioner s case , that his father had sent leave letter on 2nd March , 
1961 that would not by itself give him the liherty or privilege to absent himself 
beyond the period of leave sanctioned . It is not petitioner s case either that on 
his father s application the leave was sanctioned. While so , his absence without 
leave is a misconduct . The category of misconducts whether gross or minor are 
described in paragraph 521 sub -paragraphs ( lv ) and (vi). On a careful reading 
of suh -paragraph ( iv ) it will be seen that none of the acts mentioned therein could 
be related to the petitioner s conduct. His case will undoubtedly come under sub 
paragraph ( vi ) ( a ) for minor misconduct for absence without leave or overstay of 
the sanctioned leave without sufficient grounds. This is clearly a case of absence 
without leave and in any event overstaying after the sanctioned leave of one day . 
The learned coulisel for the Bank Mr. K . Ş . Naidu strenuously urged that the 
petitioncr s act should be considered to fall under the category of gross misconduct 
in as much as his absence is a gross negligence of duty . He would also state that 
his case also falls under sub - clausc ( e ) to sub - paragraph ( iv ) for wilful insubordi 
nation or disobedience of any lawful and reasonable order of the manageinent or 
of a superior, I am unable to agree with his view . So far as sub - clause ( e ) is 
concerned there is no case of any wilful insubordination or disobedience as 
admittedly he had no notice of the directions made by the Bank to produce the 
medical certificate . All the letters addressed by the Bank have been returned . 
There is no evidence to show that he was posted with knowledge of the Bank s 
directions. Therefore sub - clause ( e ) has no application . I shall try presently to 
find whether sub - clause ( j ) could be invoked . The learned counsel argues that 
there are 4 charges against the petitioner namely , absence without leave , failure 
to produce the medical certificate in spite of directions of the Bank , that even 
after a lapse of 30 days no application was made for extension of Icave, that he 
was guilty of gross -negligence amounting to gross -misconduct. Therefore it is 
urged that it is a misnomer to call his absence as a minor misconduct. He would 
say that it is a case which would certainly fall under the category of gross 
misconduct. No doubt, the workman s conduct is liable to be construed as gross 
negligence , but whether as a consequence of the same he had involved or likely 
to have involved the Bank in serious loss ? Nothing had been shown that as a 
result of wis negligence on his part the Bank was put to serious logs or was likely 
to put to serious loss. When a case of a nature as this , is specifically provided 
for under sub -paragraph (vi) (a ) we cannot traverse beyond it to find out whether 
any other provision could be invoked against the petitioner . The specific provision 
contains a case of absence without leave or overstaying without suficient grounds. 
This is a case which completely falls under the provision of paragraph ( vi) ( a ) . 
Therefore it is only a minor misconduct and not gross misconduct. 

10 . The next question that arises is , whether the punishment of discharge from 
service can be justified . The Sastry Award also lays down the punishment to be 
awarded in cases of an employee found guilty of monor misconduct. Under sub 
paragraph ( vii) there are 3 kinds of punishments which are contemplated for 
minor misconduct : ( a ) He may be warned or censured ; or ( b ) have an adverse 
remark entered against him ; or ( c ) have his increment stopped tor a period not 
longer than 6 months. Therefore the dismissal from service of the petitioner is 
not valid or justified . He can be punished only in any 3 ways mentioned above . 
I think as this is a case of jong absence without leave, his Increment is liable to 
be stopped for a period of 6 months.

therefore he was dismissed. Though the management in Ex . M . 2 ( a ) had com 
municated that if he did not report for duty on or before 10th February 1961 with
