Bombay, the 5th July 1954 

No. 11 — Shri K. S. A. Iyer, officiating Assistant 
Accountant, Reserve Bank of India, Inspection Depart-

effect from 5th June 1954.

Bank of India, Exchange Control Department, Kanpur 
was granted leave from 8th to 18th June 1954.

appointed as Assistant Controller in the same department 
with effect from 15th June 1954 vice Shri R. P. Chatterjee 
proceeded on leave, 

4. On return from leave Shri P. K. Banerjee is reposted 
as Assistant Currency Officer, Reserve Bank of India, 

Issue Department, Kanpur with effect from the close of 
business on 19th June 1954 vice Shri K. S. Jodhka.

as Banking Officer, Reserve Bank of India, Department 
of Banking Operations, Bombay with effect from 21st 
June 1954 vice Shri A. K. Bhuchar reverted as Staff 
Assistant with effect from the close of business on the 
19th June 1954. 

6. On return from leave Shri G. S. Annaswami is re- 
posted as Banking Officer, Reserve Bank of India, Depart- 
ment of Banking Operations, Trivandrum with effect from 
21st June 1954 vice Shri R. P. Iyer reverted as Staff 
Assistant with effect from the close of business on the 
19th June 1954. 

7. Shri M. L. Gogtay, Assistant Chief Officer, Reserve 
Bank of India, Department of Banking Operations. 
Bombay is appointed io officiate as Deputy Chief Officer 
in the same department with effect from 21st June 1954. 

8. Shri K. S. Jodhka is posted as Accountant, Reserve 
Bank of India, Kanpur with effect from the close of 
business on 23rd June 1954 vice Shri K. C. Banerjee re- 
verted as Staff Assistant. 

9. On return from leave Shri K. Venkatarama Ayyar is 

reposted as Banking Officer, Reserve Bank of India. 

Department of Banking Operations, Delhi with effect from 
26th June 1954 vice Shri Harbans Lai reverted as Staff 
Assistant. 

10. On return from leave Shri R. Raghunatha Rao is 

reposted as Banking Officer, Reserve Bank of India, 

Department of Banking Operations, Trivandrum with 
effect from 28th June 1954. 

11. Shri W. T. Korke, Banking Officer, Reserve Bank 
of India, Department of Banking Operations, Bombay is 
appointed as Assistant Chief Officer in the same depart- 
ment with effect from 1st July 1954. 

12. Shri P. S. Sitaram, officiating Deputy Chief Officer, 
Reserve Bank of India, Department of Banking Opera- 
tions, Bombay proceeded on leave with effect from 1st 
July 1954.
