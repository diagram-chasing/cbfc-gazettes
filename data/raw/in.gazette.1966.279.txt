RAILWAY RATES TRIBUNAL 
Before the Railway Rates Tribunal at Madras 

[Public Notice a under Rule 19(3) and (4) of the Rail- 
way Rates Tribunal Rules, 1959.1 

Complaint No. 1 of 1966 

The Hindustan Gum and Chemicals Ltd. — Complainant 

vs. 

The Union of India owning and representing the 
Northern Railway, New Delhi, and others. — Respondents 

Whereas the Complainant above named has filed a 
complaint that they have a factory at Bhiwani on the 
Northern Railway for the production of Guar split and 
Guar meal from the raw material Guar which is trans- 
ported to Bhiwani from various stations on the Northern 
and Western Railways; that the rates charged by the 
respondent railways for the transport of guar from 
the various stations to Bhiwani are unreasonable; that 
guar being a leguminous plant like grams, beans, etc. 
used for human consumption and cattle feed and its 
price being much less than that of gram, charging guar 
a rate which is higher than the rate for gram contra- 
venes Section 28 of the Railways Act; 

That guar meal which is obtained on processing guar 
is a nutritious cattle feed and also used in the manufac- 
ture of mixed fertilisers, and is exported from Bhiwani 
to the various stations on the respondent railways; that 
besides the rate for guar meal being unreasonable per 
sc, charging guar meal a rate which is higher than the 
rate for chuni and oil cakes contravenes Section 28 of 
the Railways Act; that guar meal being a cattle feed and 
raw material for the manufacture of mixed fertilisers, 
the disadvantage of its being charged at a higher rate 
has to be abated in public interests also; 

AND WHEREAS the complainant has prayed for 
(1) declaring the present rates charged by the respon- 
dents for the transport of guar to Bhiwam from Adam- 
pur, Abobar, Bhadra, Barnala, Bhattu, Bhatinda, 
Dabawali, Dudwakhara, Ellenabad, Faridkot, Gogamedi, 
Goniana, Hanumangarh Town, Hadyal, Kalanwali, 
Kotkapura, Mansa, Maur, Muktsar, Nohar, Raman 
Mandi, Rampuraphul, Ratanaarh, Sirsa, Sadulpur, Sri- 
ganganagar, Sangaria, Sardar Shahar, Srikaranpur, 
Taranagar, Alwar, Bhachhau, Budlada, Banner. Chirawa, 
Churu, Ferozepur, Ferozepur Cantt., Giddarbha, Gaga- 
ria, Gadra Road, liindaun City, Jaitu, Jagraon, Jodh- 
pur, Kherli, Khadecn, Lilma, Nokha, Phulera, Raisingh 
Nagar, Ramsar, Sawaimadhopnr, Talwandi, and Tappa
