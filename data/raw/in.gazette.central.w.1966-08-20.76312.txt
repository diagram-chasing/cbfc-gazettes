Order No. 7 ( 10 ) /65 -LRII dated 26th April 1965 , referred this dispute to my learned 
predecessor for adjudication . I had succeeded him on 19th November 1965 as 
Presiding Oficer , Industrial Tribunal, Andhra Pradesh , Hyderabad . By a subse 
quent order No . 7 / 33 /65 -LRII dated 31st January 1966 the Government of India 
in its Ministry of Labour and Employment had , along with similar cases, with 
drawn I. D . No . 28 / 1965 from the file of my lcained predecessor and transferred 
it to me. The issue as per Schedule annexed to the Notification is this : - - 
Whether in view of the actual nature of work peformed by Shri K . Rajesham 

from June 1962, the management of Singareni Collieries Company 
Limited was justified in designating him as Lorry Driver and paying 
him only catcgory VIII wages ? If not, to what relief is he entitled 

and from what date ? 
The statement of claims on behalf of the claimant, Rajesham , was filed by the 
General Secretary, Andhra Pradesh Colliery Mazdoor Sangh . That Sangh is party 
to the reference . Rajesham was appointed as car and lorry driver in the Rama 
gundam Division of the Singareni Collieries Company Limited . His claim is that 
it was in the month of June 1961 that he was so appointed . It is stated that 
later he was working as mechanic in the Automobile Şection but that in the year 
1963 he was sent back to work as driver . His complaint is that instead of desi 
gnating him as in - charge senior mechanic with the appropriate scale of pay, he 
was wrongly designated as driver in Category VIII . He claims that he should be 
designated as in - charge mechanic with appropriate scale of pay . The manuge 
ment s counter is that it was not in June 1961 that Rajesham was appointed as 
driver but that it was on 31st May 1962. It is admitted that Rajesham had been 
working as motor mechanic, but it is explained that it was at his own request. It 
is further stated in the counter that although Rajesham was entitled to only Cate 
gory VII as driver he was given Category VIII because he was working as mecha 
nic . It is added that Rajesham cannot lay claim to a category higher than category
