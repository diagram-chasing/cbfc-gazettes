NATIONAL LIBRARY 

In response to public opinion, the working hours of the 
Reading Rooms and the Lending Section at Belvedere 


have been extended with effect for the 16th August, 1963. 
The revised working hours are specified below: — 

Reading Rooms Weekdays (including Saturdays) 9 A.M. to 8 P.M. 

Sundayp & holidays . . , . 10 A.M. to 6 P.M. 

Lending Section Weekdays (including Saturdays) 10 A.M. to 5 P.M. 

Sundays and holidays .. 10 A.M to 1-30 P.M. 

The Reading Rooms and the Lending Section will, how- 
ever, remain entirely closed on the following days of the 
year : — 

(i) Republic Day, 

(ii) Independence Day, and 

(iii) Mahatma Gandhi's Birth Day, 

Persons above 18 years of age are eligible to make use of 
the Library ; which is a free lending Library open to any 
one residing in any part of the Indian Union. There is no 
subscription to pay but security in cash is essential. 

B. S. KESAVAN 
Librarian
