New Delhi, the 21th November 1963 

No. H2-Pres./63 . — The President is pleased to award the 
President's Police and Fire Services Medal for gallantry to 
the undermentioned officer of the Goa Police : — 

Name of the officer and rank. 

Shri Jiwa Ram Patil, 

Armed Police Constable Driver, 

State Reserve Police Force Group II, 

Panjim, Goa. 

Statement of services for which the decoration has been 
awda-ded. 

During the operations in Goa on the 18th December 1961, 
Shrl Jiwa Ram Patil was employed as a driver of one of the 
Jeeps of a Company of the State Reserve Police Force which 
was to follow the 4th Rajputs and be in support from the 
Rarwar Sector. These forces met with an obstruction at 
the Ardaphonda Bridge which had been blown up by the 
Portuguese who kept our positions under heavy mortar and 
other fire. Tlie diversions along which our forces had to 
advance were heavily mined and in trying to negotiate them 
an Army jeep was blown up. Undeterred by the dangers of 
the situation, Shri Jiwa Ram Patil moved tils jeep forward 
through the mined diversions thus giving a safe lead to opr 
forces. 

Shri Jiwa Ram Patil showed conspicuous courage and great 
devotion to duty in the face of enemy fire. 

2. This award is made for gallantry under rule 4(i) of 
the rules governing the award of the President’s Police and 
Fire Services Medal and consequently carries with it the 
special allowance adrnissible under rule 5, with effect from the 
18th December 1961. 

No, 83-Pr^5./63. — The President is pleased to award the 
Police Medal for gallantry to the undermentioned offlecr of 
the Gujarat Police : — 

Name of the officer and rank. 

Shri Chinubhai Ashabhai Shah, 

Sub-Inspectoi of Police, 

Panchmahals District, Gujarat. 

Statement of sejwices for yvhich the decoration has been 
awdrded. 

Shri Chinubhai Ashabhai Shah was deputed In August 
1962, along with a Section of the State Reserve Police Force 
Group I, Baroda, to apprehend two notorious dacoits Man- 
sing and his brother Savji whose gangs had been terroriring 
the Panchmahals District for several years. 

On the 26th August 1962, Shri Shah set up his camp a 
few miles from Village Sajora, the home of the dacoits; and 
by winning the confidence of the villagers was able to 
'apprehend Mansing on the 9th September 1962. 

On the 3rd October information was received that Savji 
would visit his house that night. After dusk, Shri Shah with 
a member of the State Reserve Police Force and a trusted 
informant, hid in Savji’s house. At about 9.30 p.m., Savji, 
armed with 'a shotgun, came to his house and chatted with 
his brother Masura who was lying on the verandah, Savji 
sent Masnra into the house to bring food, but when the 
cattle tethered in the house made a noise, Savji became sus- 
picious and left the verandah asking Masura whether there 
was anyone inside the house. Realising that Savji might 
leave the house, Shri Shah, despite the risk of being shot, 
rushed out and called on Savji to surrender. Savji fired at 
Shri Shah who promptly returned the fire with his revolver 
wounding the dacoit. Shri Shah then caught hold of him 
and put him under arrest. A DBBL shotgun, with one dis- 
charged and one live cartridge in the breech and 15 rounds of 
ammunition were seized from the dacoit. 

In these operations against two notorious dacoits Shri 
Chinubhai Ashabhai Shah showed commendable zeal, initia- 
tive and courage of a high order. 

2. This award is made for gallantry under rule 4(i) of 
the rules governing the award of the Police Medal and conse- 
quently carries with it the special allowance admissible under 
nile 5, with effect from the 3rd October 1962,
