I Effective date of award — 20th October 1962) 

On 20th October 1962, at dawn, after heavy bombardment, 
Chines© forces attacked the left flank of the position held by 
a Gorkhit Rifles battalion in Tsangdhar area in NEFA, 2/Lt. 
Dogra's platoon was on this flank, guarding a track leading

enemy put in five successive attacks that day, but all of them

the, battalion carried out a successful withdrawal to SENGE 
under orders from the Brigade Headquarters. Lt. Colonel 
Bhatt'acharjea was in charge of the advance guards and 
though already tired, his men marched the whole night, 
covering a distance of 14 miles. When the forward troops 
came under sudden enemy fire from a ridge, Lt. Colonel 
Bhattacharjea led his men gallantly and cleared the ridge 
area, but could not make further progress owing to heavy 
enemy fire and was captured. 

Throughout this period of over three weeks Lt. Colonel 
Bhattach'arjea led his men bravely with great courage and

squalls and very rough seas and since the life boat by which 
they came was no longer serviceable, they were directed to 
stay on board the ship and to be ready for towing operations 
the next morning. But, during the night, the ship wrecked 
by fire, explosions and the rough sea, broke up and sank with 
(he brave volunteers aboard. 

Chief Officer Soares and his companions gave their lives 
in the hazardous task of trying to save a wrecked ship, 
showing great courage in the face of the gravest danger and

plantation line.

desirous of being appointed as a Valuer may apply to the 
Deputy Secretary to the Government of India, Ministry of 
Finance (Department of Revenue), New Delhi, on or before 
the 28th February, 1964. 

3. On receipt of an application, the Central Government 
may require any other information to be furnished or may 
cause such enquiries to be made- as it deems fit. 

4. (1) The appointment of Valuers shall, in the first 
instance, be for a period of three years and every person 
whose name has been included in any list shall have to 
apply again, for the renewal of his appointment for a similar 
period if he satisfies the qualifications prescribed for the time 
being.

any person from the list of Valuers if it finds him guilty of

from Wet Docks and Wharves and Lands and Buildings.

Account and contributions to Reservo Funds and Capital 
Account) during the year under review was Rs. 126.25 lakhs 
as against Rs. "149.62 lakhs in 1961-62. The decrease in 
expenditure during the year under review was mainly due to
