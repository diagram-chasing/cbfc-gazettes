ANNEXURE 

INDIAN PANORAMA, 1985 REGULATIONS 

1. Indian Panorama, 1985 

1.1 The Indian Panorama, 1985 will consist of a 
maximum of 21 feature length films, with English 
sub-titles and a maximum ol 21 short films with 
English commentary or English sub-titles. 

1.2 The exhibition of Indian Panorama films 
will be held along with the 10th International film 
Festival of India to be held in New Delhi from 3rd 
to 17th January, 1985. 

2. Use of Indian Panorama Films 

2.1 One or more print(s) of Indian Panorama 
films will be purchased and sub-titled in English, 
except in the case of short films by the Directorate 
of Film Festivals, National Film Envelopment Cor¬ 
poration (hereinafter referred to as "the DYecto- 
ratc’’) on behalf of the Government of India in the 
Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. After 
their screening at the Indian Panorama Section of 
the 10th International Films Festival of India in 
New Delhi in January, 1985, the prints may be used 
for participation in various International Film 
Festivals abroad, Indian film weeks organised in 
other countries and other non-commercial screen¬ 
ings within the country and abroad. For all such 
screenings, the producer (which term in these Regu¬ 
lations will include the right holder) will not be 
eligible for any payment. He will, however, be kept 
informed of such participation. 

2.2 The producer of a film selected for inclusion 
in the Indian Panorama will issue an authorisation 
to the National Film Archive of India, Pune in Form 
I.P. II to score a positive Print at the cost of the 
Archive for preservation and archival use. 

3. Eligibility 

3.1 All films in 35 mm or in any wider gauge, 
viz. cinema scope, 70 ram. etc. made in any Indian 
language and certified by the Central Board of Film 
Certification between 1st November, 1983 and 30th 
September, 1984 (both days inclusive) are eligible 
for entry in the Indian Panorama. However, filrrts 
entered previously in the Indian Panorama will not

1985. No claims of any kind would lie against the

selected short film due to technical or any other 
reasons. 


4. Procedure for entering films 

4.1 The producer desirous to enter his film for 
the Indian Panorama, 1985 shall apply to the 
Executive Director, Directorate of Film Festivals, 
National Film Development Corporation, 4th floor, 
Lok Nayak Bhavan, Khan Market, New Delhi- 
110003 on the prescribed entry form (Form I.P.I. 
along with forms I.P—II and I.P—III) attached 
with these Regulations. Forms I.P. II and I.P. Ill 
are just applicable to short films. The last date 
for receipt of applications in the Directorate is 10th 
October, 1984. 

4.2 The Directorate shall scrutinise the appli¬ 
cation with a view to determining whether the film 
is eligible for being considered for the Indian Pano¬ 
rama, 1985 and shall send and intimation or ask 
the concerned office of the National Film Develop¬ 
ment Corporation to send an intimation to the pro¬ 
ducer to send one print of the eligible film to one 
of the following officers of the National Film Develo¬ 
pment Corporation, depending upon the language in

ing material :— 

(i) A detailed synopsis of the film in English. 

(ii) A copy of the original script and a copy of 
the English translation of the script (for im¬ 
mediate preparation of spotting sheet for

5. Selection and return of prints 

5.1 The films for inclusion in the Indian Pano¬ 
rama, 1985 shall be selected by an All-Tndia selec¬ 
tion Panel which will be assisted by three Regional 
Panels to be set up in Bombay, Calcutta and 
Madras. The decision of the Selection Panel shall 
be final and no correspondence regarding its deci¬ 
sion shall be entertained. 

5.2 In case the film is selected, the print and the 
scripts shall be retained by the Directorate, except 
in the case of short films for spotting and shall be 
returned only after the spotting is over. In such cases, 
the publicity material shall be retained by the Direc¬ 
torate and the producer will also be required to issue 
an authorisation to the Directorate in Form I.P. III. 
Fie shall, also supply additional publicity material to 
facilitate the participation of the film in International 
Film Festivals, expositions and film weeks abroad and 
within the country, as and when required. 

5.3 In the event of the film not being selected, 
the print and the accompanying material shall be 
returned to the producer within 15 days of the 
final meeting of the Selection Panel. 

5.4 The producer shall bear the freight cost of 
the prints and the publicity material while sending 
and the Directorate shall bear the return costs. In 
view of the inordinate delay often caused while 
sending material by rail, it is preferred that film 
prints "and other materials are sent by air. The 
prints will be returned in the same way as they 
tare sent.
