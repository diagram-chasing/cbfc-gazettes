The following will be inserted as note to the Recruit- 
ment Rules of the Salar Jung Museum. They will apply 
for recruitment to ull posts in the Museum including the 
posts for which recruitment rules have already been 
framed and notified unless specifically provided otherwise 
for any particular post/posts. 

Note : 

1. In respect of posts for which the rules prescribe 
a percentage or whole of the vacancies in those posts to 
be filled by promotion, employees should have put in a 
service (including all kinds of leave during which it is 
certified that they would have continued to officiate in 
the posts but for going on leave) of at least three years 
(continuous or otherwise) in the posts from which such 
promotion is permissible as indicated in column 9 of the 
rules before they can be considered for promotion against 
such reserved vacancies. 

2. At any time when recruitment is made the vacan- 
cies reserved for promotion may be filled by direct re- 
cruitment If it is certified by the Board that there is no 
employee eligible for such promotion or if the eligible 
employees are found unsuitable for promotion. The num- 
ber of such reserved vacancies filled by direct recruit- 
ment in' any year will he carried over for 2 recruitment 


years and if suitable candidates arc not available for pro- 
motion within this period such carried-forward vacancies 
will be treated as having lapsed. 

2. There will be no age limit for promotion against 
the percentage reserved for departmental promotion. 

4, The minimum age limit for recruitment will be 18 
years. This may be relaxed by the appointing authority 
in deserving cases by any period not exceeding one year 
in all. 

The following amendments may be made in the re- 
cruitment rules : — 

1. Head of the column No. 7 of the Recruitment Rules. 
Delete the words “age and" in the column heading, 

2. Recruitment Rules for Guide Lecturers : 

Substitute the words “Graduate Attendants” for the 
word "Attendants" appearing in column 9 in the re- 
cruitment rules for Guide Lecturers.
