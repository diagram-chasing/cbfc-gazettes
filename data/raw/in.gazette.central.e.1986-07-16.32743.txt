INDIAN PANORMA 1987 REGULATIONS 
1 . Indian Panorama, 1987 

1 . 1 The Indian Panorama, 1987 ( hereinafter re 
ferred to as " the Indian Panorama" ) will consist of a 
maximum of 21 full length feature films , with 
English sub -titles and a maximum of 21 short films , 
with English commentary or English sub - titles , 

1.2 The best feature and short films produced in 
India and certified during the period mentioned in 
regulation 2 . 1 distinguished by cinematic , thematic 
and aesthetic excellence , out of the films entered in 
the Indian Panorama, shall be selected for inclusion 
in the Indian Panorama, in accordance with the 
procedure laid down in these Regulations, 

1 . 3 The films selected will be exhibited in the 
Indian Panorama Section of XI International Film 
Festival of India to be held in New Delhi from 10th 
the to 24th January , 1987.

4 . Selection of Indian Panorama films 

4 . 1 The films for inclusion in the Indian Panorama 
shall be selected by an All India Selection Panel 
(hereinafter referred to as “ All India Panel" ) to be 
constituted by the Directorate , with the approval of 
The Government of India . Provided that the film 
which has won the National Award for the Best 
Feature in the 33rd National Film Festival will also 
be included in the Panorama 87 if it had not been 
included in the Panorama 86 . 


4 . 2 The All - India Panel shall be assisted by 
Regional Selection Panels to be set up in Bombay , 
Calcutta and Madras by the Directorate, with the 
approval of the Government of India .
