Technology, Kharagpur, have appointed Shri Amarendra 
Chandrashankar Pandya as Assistant Professor in Farm 
Power & Machinery at the same Institute with effect 
from the forenoon of 26th August 1955 on probation for 
one year.

tute of Technology, Kharagpur, has relinquished the 
charge of his office op the forenoon of 30th August 1955,

special leave term sanctioned under Government of India 
Ministry of Education letter No. F.20-96/54-T.1, dated 15th 
July 1955.

Technology, Kharagpur, have appointed Shri Phanindra
