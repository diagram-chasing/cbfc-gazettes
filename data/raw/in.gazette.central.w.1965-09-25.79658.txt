Companies Amendment Bill 

The Council records its appreciation of the action 
taken hy the Central Government in introducing in Par 
liament, the Companies ( Second Amendment ) Bill , 
1964 contemplating in one of its proposed amendments 
to acquire power to order audit of cost accounts , when 
considered necessary , of companies engaged in certain 
specified activities. The Council has communicated to 
the Central Government its own views so far as cost 
audit is concerned . Besides submitting memoranda to 
the Central Government and the Joint Committee of 
Parliament to which the Bill was referred, oral evidence 
on behalf of the Council was also tendered before the 
Soint Committee . The Joint Committee has already sub 
mitted its report to Parliament incorporating its consider 
ed views. The Bill is now awaiting discussion in Parlia 
ment, and much will depend upon the form in which it 
will be passed . The Council hopes that the passage of 
the Bill through Parliament will open up a new vista to 
the Costing profession in India .
