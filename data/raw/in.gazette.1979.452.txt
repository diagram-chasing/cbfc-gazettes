Kirkee Cantonment, the August 1979 

S.R.O. No. KCB/1.— -Whereas a draft of certain proposals 
to impose a tax on entertainments within the Canton- 
ment of Kirkee was published with Kirkee Cantonment Board’s 
notice No. 7/14/P-138. dated the 30th lune, 1978 as required 
by Section 61 of the Cantonments Act, 1924 (2 of 1924') for 1 
inviting objections and suggestions till the expiry of a period 
of thirty days from the date of publication of the said notice. 

AND WHEREAS the said notice was put on the Cantonment 
Notice Board on the 30th June, 1978. 

AND WHEREAS all the objections received from the public 
on the said draft have been duly considered by the Canton- 
ment Board. 

AND WHEREAS the Central Government have duly ap- 
proved and confirmed the said draft of the tax as required by 
Section 60 of the said Act. 

NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred 
by section 60 of the Cantonments Act 1924 and in superses- 
sion of the notification of the Government of Tndia in the 
Ministry of Defence, No.S.R.O. 263, dated the 12th February, 
1949, the Cantonment Board, Kirkee, with the previous sanc- 
tion of the Central Government, hereby imposes a tax on En- 
tertainments (show tax) within the Cantonment of Kirkee as 
specified in the second column of the Schedule hereto annex- 
ed the rates specified In the third column thereof. 

Provided that, no tax shall be imposed in respect of cinema 
show, dramatic performances or variety entertainment shows 
given in aid of civil or military charitable funds, religious or 
educational funds or relief fund. 

Note : — Exemption in these cases will be granted by the 
Executive Officer. 

The liability to pay the tax shall be on the proprietor and 
payment shall be made in advance before the commencement 
of the show.
