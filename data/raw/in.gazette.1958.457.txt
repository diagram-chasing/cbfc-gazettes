Application for Grading — During the year 1956-57 no 
application for grading was receiyed. 

Grant of Export Certificate — For the period under re- 
view, Certificates for Shipment coal were issued for a 
total quantity of 1,710,100 tons as against 1,556,082 tons 
during the previous year. It may be observed that there 
had been an increase'of 154,018 tons during the year under 
review, mainly owing to coal for Chittagong and Karachi 
exported under the Indian Coal Grading Board. Aden and 
Sudan which used to be previously markets for Indian 
Coal have again taken coal after 1954 and 1951 respective- 
ly. This is an encouraging sign. Increase is also marked to 
the ports of Chittagong, Colombo, Karachi and Rangoon. 
There was no export of coal to Alexandria, Hongkong, 
Mauritius and Vizagapattam during this year. Fall in the 
Export of coal is observed to Bombay, Cochin, Cuddalore, 
Madras, Singapore and Tuticorin.
