THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS 
OF INDIA 

NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi- 1, the 23 id July 1963 

No. 1-04(29,1/63 — ' The following draft of certain 
amendments to the Chartered Accountants Regulations, 
1949, which it is proposed to make in exercise of the 
powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (3) of Sec- 
tion 30 of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 
(XXXV111 of 1949), is published for information of 
all persons likely to be affected thereby and notice is 
hereby given that the draft will be taken up for con- 
sideration on or after the 7th September 1963. 

Any objection or suggestion which may be received 
from any person with respect to the said draft before 
the date specified will be considered by the Council of 
the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, New 
Delhi. 

In the said Regulations : — 

I. In Regulation 19C, add at the end of the first 
paragraph the following : — 

"The candidate who fails in one paper of the Inter- 
mediate Examination but secures a minimum of 60% 
marks in the aggregate in the remaining papers, shall be 
required to appear only in that paper at the next sub- 
sequent examination and on his passing the same he 
will be declared successful in the examination.” 


2. In sub-regulation (3) of Regulation 22, add at 
the end of the first paragraph the following : — 

"The candidate who fails in one paper comprised in 
a Group of papers for the Final Examination, but 
secures a minimum of 60% marks in the aggregate in 
the remaining papers of that Group, shall be required 
to appear only in that paper at the next subsequent 
examination and on his passing the same, he will be 
declared successful in /.hat Group." 

3. In sub-regulation (4) of Regulation 22, add at 
the end of the first paragraph the following : — 

"The candidate who fails in one paper, comprised in 
a Group of pupers for the Final Examination, but 
secures a minimum of 60% marks in the aggregate in 
the remaining papers of that Group, shall be required 
to appear only in that paper a,t the next subsequent 
examination and on his passing the same, he will be 
declared successful in that Group.” 

4. For the existing Regulation 89A, substitute the 

following : — 

“89A. Restriction on fees— No Chaffered Accountant 
in practice shall charge or offer to charge, accept or 
offer to accept, in respect of any professional employ- 
ment, fees which are based on a percentage of profits, 
or which are contingent upon the findings, or results of 
such employment : 

Provided that — 

(a) in the case of a receiver or a liquidator, the 
fees may be based on a percentage of the 
realisation or of the assets: 

(b) in the case of an auditor of a co-operative 
society, the fees may be based on a percen- 
tage of the paid-up capital or the working 
capital or the gross or net income or profits; 
and 

(c) in the case of a valuer for the purposes of 
direct taxes and duties, the fees may be based 
on a percentage of the value of the property.” 

5. For the existing Regulation 107(1), substitute the 
following : — 

“107(1). The Committee shall forward the thesis to 
the referees appointed by it for their advice whether the 
thesis is of a sufficiently high degree of merit as to 
deserve approval or whether it may be modified and 
if so, in what manner or whether it may be rejected.” 

B. R. MALHOTRA
