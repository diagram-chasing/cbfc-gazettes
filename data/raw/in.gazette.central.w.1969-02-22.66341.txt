Accordingly , the offending passages in tho Amirgadh speech fell within s. 123 ( 2 ) 
proviso ( a ) (ii ) . We are satisfied that Shambhu Maharaj spoke at the Amirgadh inoeting 
with the consent of Pupambhai, the election agent of the appellant. Punanbhai was 
present at the Amirga dh meeting. He addressed the meeting beforo Shambhu Mabaraj 
spoke. Shambhu Mabaraj addressed several other clection meetings of the Swatantra 
party . Punambhai issued a pamphlet calling ono of the meetings , P . W . 10 proved that 
he was asked by Punambhai to call Shambhu Maharaj for addressing another meeting 
as the voters were uneducated and had deep belief in religion . Punambhai accompanied 
Shambhu Maharaj from one place to another . On February 8 , 1967 he went with 
Shambhu Maharaj to the meeting at Ikbalgadh and thereafter went to Amirgadh . On 
February 9 , he went with Shambhu Mabaraj to the meeting at Way, The offending 
passages of the speech at the Amirgadh meeting aro integral parts of the dominant theme 
of the gin of cow slaughter . They cannot be regarded as stray words spoken by Shambhu 
Maharaj without Punambhai s consent. Punambhai did not raise any objection to the 
impugned speeches at the meeting . He gave evidenco in Court but did not say that 
he was not a consenting party to the offending passages . Wo hold that the corrupt 
practice under s. 123 ( 2 ) proviso (a ) ( ii ) was committed at the Amirgadh meeting on 
February 8. 1967 with the consent of the election agent of tho appollant. 
In the result , the appeal is dismissed . There will be no order as to costs,

he had not been suspended ."
