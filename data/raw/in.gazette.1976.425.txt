New Delhi, the 29th May 1976 

No. S.R.O,12/26/C/L&C/71, — WHEREAS certain draft of 
the bye-laws regulating the erection and re-erection of build- 
ings in the Cantonment of Babina was published with the 
notice of the Cantonment Board, Babina, No. 186/4074/3088, 
dated the 8th February, 1974, as required by sub-section (1) 
of section 284 of the Cantonments Act, 1924 (2 of 1924) 
(hereinafter referred to as the said Act), for inviting objections 
and suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby 
till the 9th April. 1974. 

AND WHEREAS no objections or suggestions were re- 
ceived from the public on the said draft by the aforesaid 
Cantonment Board before the said date; 

AND WHEREAS it was considered desirable to rc-publish 
the said draft with certain modifications for the information 
of all persons likely to be affected thereby; 

AND WHEREAS the said modified draft of the bye-laws 
was published with the notice of the Cantonment Board, 
Babina, No. 186/4074/2843, dated the 5th February, 1976, 
for inviting objections and suggestions till the 20th March, 
1976; 

AND WHEREAS the said notice was put on the Canton- 
ment Notice Board on the 51hi February, 1976; 

AND WHEREAS no objections or suggestions were re- 
ceived from the public by the Cantonment Board before the 
said date; 

AND WHEREAS the Central Government have duly ap- 
proved and confirmed the said draft of the bye-laws as re- 
quired by sub-section (T) of section 284 of the said Act; 

NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred 
by section 186 of the said Act, the Cantonment Board. Babina, 
hereby makes the following bye-laws, namely : — 

1. Short title and commencement — 

(1) These bye-laws may be called the Babina Canton- 
ment (Regulation of the Erection or Re-erection of 
Buildings) Bye-Laws, 1976. 

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their 
publication in the Official Gazette, 


2. Definitions, — In these bye-laws, unless the context other- 
wise requires,— 

(a) “Act” mean s tire Cantonments Act, 1924 (2 of 
1924); 

(b) ‘‘Board’’ means the Cantonment Board, Babina; 

(c) “Form” means a Form appended to these byc-laws. 

3. Application for erection dr re-erection oj buildings 
Every nerson intending to erect or rc-emct a building in the 
Cantonment of Babina shall apply for sanction to do so by 
giving notice in writing to the Executive Officer, under section 
179 of the Act in Form ‘A’ in triplicate and shall at the same 
time submit duly signed by himself or by his agent, — 

(a) a site plan of the land on which it is intended to 
erect or re-erect the building; 

(b) a plan of the building which it is proposed to erect 
or re-erect together with a plan of the existing 
building, if any, showing platforms, balconies and 
other projections; 

(c) specifications in duplicate, detailed in Form ‘B’ ap- 
pended 10 these bye-laws. 

Note The plans referred lo in clauses. (a) and (b) 
shall be submitted in triplicate with one copy of the plan on 
tracing cloth and the others in Ferro/ Ammonia print. 

4. Requisition in the site plan.—' The site plan shall be 
drawn to a scale not less than ONE Centimetre to ONE 
Metre, The scale used shall be marked on the plan which 
shall clearly show, 

(a) the direction of the North point; 

(b) the boundaries and dimensions on the site; 

(c) the position of the site in relation to adjoining streets, 
drains, houses and vacant lands, with names of the 
owners of such houses and lands; 

(d) the position of the proposed building in relation to 
the boundaries of the site; 

(e) the level of the proposed building in relation to the 
drains and streets in which it abuts and the width 
and name of such streets; 

(f) the position and distance of the nearest waler and 
electric mains. 

5. Scale of the building plan. — The building plan shall be 
drawn to a scale of not less than 1.00 Centimetre to LOO 
Metre and such details as the Board may require shall not 
be less than 2.00 centimetres to 1.00 metre. It shall include 
a front elevation and at least one sectional elevation through 
the main part of the building and shall show, — 

(a) the plan of the ground floor and of every additional 
floor; 

(b) the position and dimension of all projections beyond 
the main walls of the building; 

(c) the position of all proposed and existing drains, pri- 
vies, urinals, cesspools, Are places, gutters, down- 
spouts showing how sullagc will be discharged into 
the nearest public drain; 

fd) the dimension of the main building and of all rooms; 

(e) the size of all doors, windows and ventilators and 
their height from the floor of loom in which they 
are fixed; 

( f) (he width and depth of foundation; 

(g) the material to be used for the building in recog- 
nised abbreviations of which an index shall be 
given on the plan; 

(h) the purpose for which it is intended to use the 
building,

6. Indications on the plans. — All new works shall be indi- 
cated on the plans in a distinctive colour and a key to the 
colours used shall be given thereon as detailed below : — - 

Existing work — Blue. 

Proposed work — Red. 

Demolition work — Green. 

Re-erection work— Yellow. 

7. Building material to he used— (1) No new building 
whether original or entire re-construction shall be creeled 
unless the carcass is built in burnt brick or coursed stone work 
on good and sound foundation as mentioned in sectional 
elevation. 

(2) No second story shall be erected on any existing build- 
ing unless the walls thereof arc properly constructed in burnt 
brick or coursed stone work and the foundation of which 
are sound and capable of taking additional load safelyT 

8. Alignment of buildings. — No building shall be erected 
if it projects beyond tho general alignment fixed for street. 
All buildings abutting on a street or a road shall have ono 
line of frontage. 

9. Minimum area required for latrines and rooms . — -( 1 > 
Latrines.— The surface area of a latrine shall not he less than 

1.00 square metre. 

(2) Rooms. — The minimum floor area of every room in- 
tended for human habitation shall not be less than 8,00 square 
metres and the minimum width of any such room shall not 
be less than 2,5 metres provided that nothing in this bye- 
law shall apply to any kitchen, buthroom, or store room. 

10. Provision for Drains. — ft) There shall be constructed 
house drains through which sullage water is intended to pass 
and they shall be connected with the nearest road side drain 
wherever such u drain exists within 29 metres of the premises 
according to the directions of the Executive Officer. 

(2) In the absence of a road side drain nearby, the house 
drain shall be discharged into a coveted soakage pit which 
shall be constructed and maintained by the house owner as 
per design and specification given in the plain. 

11. Openings to be prdvided in the. rooms. — Every room 
intended for human habitation shall have a minimum opening 
into the external air of an area equal to 10 per cent of 
superficial area of the room. 

12. Provision for staircase. , — Every building of more than 
one floor shall have staircase having a width of not less than 
80 Centimetres to ensure safety in case of fire. 

13. Minimum plinth level of buildings. — The plinth of all 
masonry buildings shall, be at least 30 centimetres above 
the level of tho centre of the nearest street and every wall 
shall have a proof course consisting of bituminous felt, vit- 
reous tiles or cement above the plinth level. 

14. Number of storeys and the height of the building. — (1) 
No building shall have more than three stoiey* and the ag- 
gregate height of a building shall not exceed 14 metres; 

Provided that the Board may for reasons to be recorded 
in writing relax the provision of this clause in any particular 
case or class of cases. 

(2) The minimum height of any floor shall not be less than 

3.00 metres. 

(3) In the case of a single storeyed building, the maximum 
height shall be 6.00 metres or equal to the width of the 
road. 

(4) Tile maximum height of a building abutting on a street 
shall not exceed 4.5 metres : 

Provided that the Board may relax the provision of this 
clause if both the Health Officer and the Executive Officer 
are of the opinion that there is no objection to a second 
storey being erected. 

15. Amendments in the building plan. — The Board may at 
the request of any applicant amend a plan duly submitted 
under tl>ese bye-laws on payment of a sum of Ten Rupees 
in each case, but the Board shall not however be bound to 
make any amendment. 


16. Preparation of building plan. — Every plan shall be 
prepared by a Draughtsman duly approved for this purpose 
by the Board. 

17. Fdrms . — Forms ‘A’ and ‘B’ may be supplied, by the 
Board’s office on payment of twenty five paise per set. there- 
of.
