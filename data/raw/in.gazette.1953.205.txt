S.R.O. 2057. — Whereas having regard to the number of banking companies 
wound up and other circumstances of the case, the Central Government Is of 
opinion that it is not necessary to attach for the time being a court liquidator to 
any High Court other than the High Courts at Calcutta and Bombay and the 
High Court of Travancore -Cochin; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (4) of 
section 38A of the Banking Companies Act, 1949 (X of 1949). the Central Govern- 
ment hereby directs that section 38A of the said Act shall not have effect in 
relation to any High Court except the High Court at Calcutta, the High Court at 
Bombay and the High Court of Travancore-Cochin.
