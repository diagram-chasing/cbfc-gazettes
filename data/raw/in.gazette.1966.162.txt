AGRICULTURAL REFINANCE CORPORATION 

Bombay, the 15 th February 1966 

No. AD M. 27 \H/ . 57 . 1 - 65 / 66 — In pursuance of Regula- 
tion 43 of the Agricultural Refinance Corporation Gene- 
ral Regulations, the Board of Directors of the Corpora- 
tion have delegated the following powers to different offi- 
cials of the Corporation with effect from February 9, 
1966 : — 

Secretary and Chief Accountant 

1. The Secretary and Chief Accountant are authorised 
to exercise, jointly with a Deputy Secretary or an 
Accounts Officer, all or any of the following powers, 
namely : — 

(a) to operate on bank accounts and the S.G.L. 
accounts of the Corporation with the Reserve Bank and 
to draw, accept, endorse and sign cheques; 


(b) to draw, accept, endorse, discount and negotiate 
Bills of Exchange, Drafts, Orders, Cheques, Warrants, 
Hundies, Promissory Notes and other negotiable instru- 
ments and securities whatsoever including securities of 
any Government or local authority or statutory or other 
Corporation; 

(c) to sell, hypothecate, pledge, endorse, negotiate 
and transfer and accept transfer of any stocks, funds or 
securities whatsoever of any Government or -any local 
authority or public body and also any stocks, shares, 
warrants or scrips of or in any joint stock company or 
corporation or otherwise and to receive the interest and 
dividend accrued or to accrue or bonuses or other pay- 
ments of a like nature declared or to be declared in res- 
pect thereof; 

(d) to ask, demand, Bue for, recover and receive and 
give effectual receipts and discharges for moneys or 
other things whatsoever from all and any persons or 
bodies politic or corporation and to settle or compound 
any accounts between the said corporation and any other 
persons or bodies public or corporate. 

2. The Secretary and Chief Accountant are authorised 
to exercise singly all or any of the following powers, 
namely : — 

(a) to make and renew call or short-term deposits for 
periods not exceeding 12 months with any State Co-ope- 
rative Bank or any schedule bank as and when it is con- 
sidered necessary in the interests of the Corporation; 

(b) to incur expenditure in connection with the pay- 
ment of office rent, salaries and allowances to the staff 
of the Corporation and halting and travelling allowances 
in connection with tours undertaken by the staff up to 
the cadre of Deputy Secretary at rates in force from 
time to time. 

3. The Secretary is authorised to exercise singly the 
power to incur other expenditure not exceeding Rs. 1,000 
in each case at any one time, and in cases where such 
expenditure exceeds Rs. 1,000 with the prior approval 
of the Managing Director and in the absence of the 
Managing Director with the prior approval of the Chair- 
man. 

4. The Chief Accountant is authorised to exercise 
singly the following powers, namely ; — 

(a) to incur other expenditure not exceeding Rs. 500 
in each case at any one time and in cases where such 
expenditure exceeds Rs. 500 but not exceeding Rs. 1,000 
with the prior approval of the Secretary and if it exceeds 
Rs. 1,000 with the prior approval of the Managing Direc- 
tor and in the absence of both the Managing Director 
and the Secretary with the prior approval of Chairman. 

(b) to accept on behalf of the Corporation deposits 
of title deeds to be made by the financing banks for 
creating sub-mortgages in favour of the Corporation in 
connection with the refinance given to such financing 
banks and also to entrust on behalf of the Corporation 
the safe custody of such title deeds to the financing 
banks. 

(c) to execute on behalf of the Corporation the agree- 
ments and such other connected papers as may be neces- 
sary in this regard with the financing banks in respect of 
the refinance to be provided by the Corporation by way 
of subscription to special development debentures to be 
floated by co-operative financing institutions or other- 
wise; 

(d) to arrange for the safe custody of documents and 
to do all matters and things incidental or consequential 
thereto. 

Deputy Secretary 

5. The Deputy Secretaries are authorised to exercise 
all or any of the following powers, namely ; — 

(a) jointly with an Accounts Officer to operate on 
bank accounts of the Corporation and to draw, accept, 
endorse and sign cheques; 

(b) singly to incur on behalf of the Corporation ex- 
penditure up to a limit of Rs. 25 in each case at any one 
time and in the absence of the Managing Director, Secre- 
tary and Chief Accountant up to a Emit of Rs. 250.
