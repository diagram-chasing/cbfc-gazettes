tion, working, etc. of the said siding, in or about 1937, 
the complainants’ predecessors in interest by name, The 
Ganga Devi Sugar Mills, Bagaha, entered into an agree- 
ment with the then Oudh and Tirhut Railway, that under 
the said "agreement for the use of the said siding a sum 
of Rs. 922.80 p. per yeaT became payable by them to 
the Oudh and Tirhut Railway in two equal half yearly 
instalments, that they had accordingly paid the said sum 
till above the year 1944 when it was reduced to 
Rs, 780.19 p. per year, that firstly the aforesaid com- 
plainants’ predecessors in interest and later on the com- 
plainants themselves had been paying the said sum till 
July 1958, when the respondent had revised the charges 
in respect of the siding to (a) Rs. 780.19 p, for each 
year towards interest on capital and cost of maintenance 
of the permanent way points and crossings and inter- 
locking connected therewith, plus (b) the amount calcu- 
lated on the basis of Re. 1 per four wheeled wagon haul- 
ed over the siding subject to a minimum of Rs. 10 per 
shunt towards siding charges, that the respondent had 
further revised the said minimum charge from Rs. 10 
to Rs. 7 in September 1958, and asked the complainants 
to pay the revised charges and execute a new agreement 
providing, Inter alia, for payment of the said revised 
charges, that because of the threat of cancellation of 
siding facilities, the complainants had executed such a 
new agreement and also paid the increased charges ' 
under protest, and that the revision by way of increase 
is very abnormal and that the revised charges are un- 
reasonable and have prayed for the fixation of the 
charges on the basis of the agreement of 1937, for giving 
effect to the charges fixed by the Tribunal from the date 
of the complaint, for directions to the respondent to 
refund such amounts as are in excess of the charges fix- 
ed by the Tribunal and to pay costs; 

AND WHEREAS it is thought that there may be per- 
sons who are not on record but have the same interest 
in the proceedings as the complainants or the respondent 
above named;
