officer may require binding themselves for an amount which shall be equal to— 


(a) the value of. the goods, in the case of producer goods; and 


,<b.) -the market price of the goods in :the case of consumer goods. 


4. Imported good# transhipped to be sealed,— <(il>) The proper officer shall, 
before the goods are transhipped, place all small packages containing the goods 
in durable bags and seal the bags with the Customs Department seal in the 
(presence Of an accredited representative of ithe owners iof tne aircraft: 


Provided that the packages containing the goods -which cannot be placed 
n reside a bag may be sealed separately. 


(2) The bags and the materials required for sealing the bags under sub- 
regulation (T) shall -be provided by, and at the cost of the owners of the aircraft. 


b. Term# of the 'bond to 'be executed. — The terms <of the bond shall be that if 
-the person executing the 1 bond produces to the proper officer, within fifteen days 
or within such extended period as such officer may allow, a certificate issued by 
the proper officer at the customs airport of destination that the goods have been 
landed at that airport, the bond shall stand discharged; but otherwise an amount 
•«qual to the value or, as the case may be, the market price of the goods in res- 
pect Of which the said, certificate is not produced shall stand forfeited.
