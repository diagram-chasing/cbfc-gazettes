BYE-LAWS FOB THE CONDUCT OF EXAMINATIONS 

FOB. AND GRANT OF OVERMANS’ CERTIFICATE 

OF COMPETENCY 

1. Application to he supported by documents — No appli- 
cation shall be considered unless it is supported by original 
certificates as to qualification (i.e, Sirdar’s Certificate, valid 
gas-testing certificate, valid First Aid Certificate), literacy, 
sound health and character and is accompanied by evidence 
that the prescribed fee lias been paid. 

2. Holding of Examinations— Examinations shall be held 
at such times and such places as may be fixed by the Board 
and notified to the candidate. The examination foes of a 
candidate who fails to appear for an examination at the 
appointed place and time without sufficient cause to the 
satisfaction of the Chief Inspector shall be forfeited. 

3. (1) Appointment of Examiners — So far as practicable, 
the examiners appointed by the Board for each examination 
shall be two managers holding the First Class Manager’s 
Certificate and an Inspector of Mines who shall also act as 
the Local Secretary for the examination. 

(2) Except in an emergency, no member of the Board shall 
act as an examiner. 

4. Subjects and syllabus for examination— Candidates 
shall be examined in tho following four subjects: — 

(a) General safety and Legislation. 

(b) Methods of working. 

(c) Ventilation and precautions with respect to explo- 
sions, fires and inundations. 

(d) Elements of Mining Machinery. 

Detailed syllabus is laid out in Appendix I, 

5. Type of examination— ( 1) The examination will consist 
Of one written paper (of two hours' duration) and orals test 
by two examiners. 

(2) The written paper shall be meant mainly to test the 
candidate’s ability to write reports, to express himself clear- 
ly in writing and io answer elementary questions relating 
to the various subjects mentioned in byelaw 4. The techni- 
cal and professional knowledge of the candidate shall be 
tested mainly at the orals test. 

(3) The written paper may be answered in English, Hindi 
or the language of the district in which examination is held. 
The orals test also will he conducted in these languages. 

(4) The question paper for the -written examination shall 
be set by the examiner concerned in consultation with the 
Local Secretary. . 

(5) The orals test may be held at a date or dates on or 
after the written examination. The date and place of the 
orals test shall be notified to the candidate bv the Local 
Secretary. 

6. Examination marks — (1) The written paper and the 
ofals test by each of the examiners shall each carry a maxi- 
mum of 50 marks, making a total of 150 marks. 

(2) In order to pass the examination, a candidate must 
obtain: 

(i) not less than 25 m^rks in the written paper; 

(il) not less than 20 marks with each of the two exami- 
ners in the orals test: and not less than 50 marks in 
the orals test as a whole; and 

(iii) not less than 90 marks in the aggregate of written 
paper and orals test. 

7. Re-examination — A candidate who has failed in an 
examination for an Overman’s Certificate shall not be eligi- 
ble for re-examination for a period of six months from the 
date of the examination when he failed, 

8. Conduct of the Examination— Every candidate shall 
conduct himself as required by the Local Secretary. The 
rules laid down in Appendix II shall apply to the conduct 
of the written examination. 

9. Declaration of results— The result of the examination 
shall be notified In writing to the individual candidates 
concerned at a later date.

APPENDIX II 

RULES GOVERNING THE CONDUCT OF WRITTEN 
EXAMINATION 

1. No candidate shall be admitted into the examination 
hall until he has produced an authorisation issued by the 
Secretary. Every candidate should also bring with him his 
Sirdar Certificate for purpose of identification. 

2. No candidate arilving more than 15 minutes late shall 
be permitted to attend the examination; and any such candi- 
date shall not be eligible for any extra compensatory time. 

3. Bo l ore entering the examination hall, every candidate 
shall leave all books, notes and papers etc. outside. 

4. Every candidate shall be required to sign in a register 
in which his roll number would be recorded. 

5. Names shall not be written on the answer book. Every 
candidate shall write his roll number at the 'top of every 
page of the paper on which he writes the answers to the 
questions. 

6. Paper will be supplied, and no paper other than that 
so supplied shall be used. 

7. Ink will be provided. Candidates shall bring their own 
pens and pencils, and drawing instruments etc. 

fi, The calculations and other work by which a result is 
obtained shall be clearly shown on the left-hand side page, 
in immediate connection with the answer to the question 
under reference. 

9. Sketches may be made to illustrate answers. When 
sketches are asked for, it should be understood that free 
hand sketches with dimensions are all that is necessary; and 
candidates need not spend time in making finished draw- 
ings. 

10. Any candidate desiring to consult the examiner or 
requiring any paper or other material should stand in his 
seat to draw attention, but should not shout or call out. 


11. Any candidate speaking to any other candidate, or 
consulting any book or notes, or using paper other than 
that supplied by the Board, or us'ng any other unfair means 
or acting in a manner considering improper by the exami- 
ner, shall be liable to be disqualified. The Board may, in 
case of such candidate, use its discretion as to permitting 
him to sit for any future examination. 

xl. No candidate 'shall leave the, examination hall before 
one hour has passed after the commencement of the exami- 
nation, 

13. When a candidate has completed the answers to the 
questions, or when the time allowed for answering the 
questions has elapsed, or when a candidate has been dis- 
qualified under Rule 11, he must hand over the answer book 
to the Secrotai ,\ of the examination and at once leave the 
examination hall.
