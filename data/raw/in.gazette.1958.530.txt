direct recruitment or promotion as shown in the Annexure to these rules. 

3. Direct Recruitment. — (1) Any person who has completed the age of 18 
years but not 25 years and who possesses the prescribed qualifications shall be- 
eligible for direct recruitment. 

(2) The upper age limit specified in sub-rule, (1) shall he subject to the usual 
relaxations prescribed by the Government of India for persons belonging to the 
Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes, displaced persons and retrenched* 
and demobolized personnel. 

(3) The age limit may in appropriate cases be relaxed by the head of the 
department.

Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes.

peon shall be on probation for a period of six months. The period of probation 
for a peon shall be three months. 

5. Promotions. — Every promotion under these rules shall be made by the 
Departmental Promotion Committee set. up in each Subordinate Office: 

Provided that the authority competent to make, the appointment to a post may 
promote a candidate to that post temporarily for a period not exceeding three 
(months; but such promotions shall not entitle the person so promoted to any 
pi-efe.rential claim to that post.
