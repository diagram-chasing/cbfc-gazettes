MINISTRY OF INFORMATION AND BROADCASTING 
NOTICE 

New Delhi, the 24th December, 1997 

S.0.895 (E).—Whereas after release in various theatres in the country the English Film JUST MARRIED which was 
granted a TIA’ certificate No. 577 dated 29-12-89 by the Mumbai regional office of the Central Board of Film Certification 
(CEFC), the police offici al s at Bade Bazar Police Station in Orissa, Begumpet Police Station in Andhra Pradesh and Brucepet 
Police Station, Bellary in Karnataka, have seized prints of the films for alleged interpolations; and 

Whereas on verification, inteipolations were found in two seized prints and the length of the prints seized at the three 
places was much less than the certified length ; and 

Whereas, after consideration of the representation given by the applicant, the Central Government is of the opinion thal 
the aforesaid film was being exhibited in a form other than the one in which it was certified by the CBFC ; 

The Central Government under sub-section (3) of section 5 E of the Cinematograph Act 1952 (37 of 1952) give a 
notice to all persons interested in the said certificate issued to the aforesaid film to send their suggestions and objections for 
considetation of the Central Government as to why the said certificate should not be revoked. 

Any suggestions and objections as above may be sent to Sh, Raghu Mcnon, Joint Secretary in the Ministry of Informa¬ 
tion and Broadcasting, Shastri Bhavan, New Delhi within 30 days from the date of publication of this notification.
