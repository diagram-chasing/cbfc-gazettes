ESG / 15-16 / film finance scheme/ 2016 


Goa Scheme of FinancialAssistance for 

Films - 2016 


The Government of Goa hereby notifies the 
scheme to provide Financial Assistance for 
production of film originating in Goa , as 
follows: 


(ii) The scheme envisages grant of Financial 
Assistance up to 50 % of the cost of production 
of a film or as per the relevant provisions made 
in Schedule | hereto , whichever is less every 
year. A Feature Film /Non -Feature Film 
Producer /Co-Producer shall be entitled to claim 
assistance under the scheme every year. If the 
Feature Film or Non - Feature Film is categorized 
under " C " Category or below for two 
consecutive years then the Producer/ Co 
-Producer shall not be entitled to avail the 
Scheme benefits for next 2 years . 


A. Title, applicability and duration . — (i) The 
scheme shall be called the Goa Scheme of 
Financial Assistance for Films 2016 (for the 
production of Feature Films in Konkani and 
Marathi and Non - Feature Films including short 
films and Documentaries in Konkani, Marathi, 
English and Hindi). The scheme shall be 
implemented through the Entertainment 
Society of Goa. 


(ii) The scheme shall apply to Feature Films/ 
/Non -Feature Film originating and produced in 
Goa by Film Producers/ Co -Producers who have 
to be registered with the Entertainment 
Society ofGoa on payment of Registration fee 
of Rs. 1000/ -. Film Producers/ Co -Producers 
already registered with Directorate of 
Information and Publicity under the earlier 
scheme are also eligible provided they satisfy 
the conditions laid down in the present 
scheme. 


(iii ) The Entertainment Society of Goa as an 
executing agency shall process all the 
applications received from registered Film 
Producers , Co -Producers willing to avail the 
financial help from the Society for producing 
Feature Films in Konkani or Marathi and Non 
-Feature Films including Short Films & 
Documentaries in Konkani , Marathi, English 
and Hindi in Goa . All necessary Codal 
formalities to be follow ed . Once application is 
processed Entertainment Society of Goa will 
send request to Department of Information and 
Publicity to release sanctioned amount. The 
Department of Information and Publicity shall 
pay ESG 2.00 % of sanctioned amount as 
commission charges towards processing of 
applications. 


( iii ) The scheme will be effective from the 
date of notification . The films released since 
2011which have not received any Government 
support/funding would be made eligible for 
financial support under this scheme. It may be 
review ed or redesigned as the need may be. 
However, the Government of Goa or the 
Entertainment Society of Goa reserves the 
right to change any or all provisions of this 
scheme if found necessary in public interest. 
The assistance under this scheme to be 
announced every year. 


(iv ) The Film Producers / Co -Producers 
making Feature Films or Non -Feature Films in 
more than one of the approved languages as 
above (bilingual) simultaneously shall be able 
to claim Financial Assistance for only one of 
the languages , subject to the condition that 
the film under consideration is original 
production and not a dubbed copy.
