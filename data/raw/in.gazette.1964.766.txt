UNIT TRUST OF INDIA 

"In exercise by the Board, of the powers conferred 
by Section 21 of the Unit Trust of India Act, 1963, the 
Unit Scheme 1964, as made by the Board at its meeting 
on the 12th day of May 1964 be and is hereby amended 
as follows : — 

(1) In clause 2 of the Unit Scheme 1964 (hereinafter 
referred to as “the Scheme") after sub-clause (b), the 
following shall be inserted - 

"(bb) “body corporate” includes a society regis- 
tered under the Societies Registration Act, 
1860, such society being hereinafter referred 
to as “a society”; 

(2) In the proviso to sub-clause (1) of clause 8 of 
the Scheme, for the words “the period of one month 
from the date on which this scheme comes into force" 
the words "the period ending with the 14th August 
1964” shall be substituted, 

(3) Existing clause 14 of the Scheme shall be num- 
bered as sub-clause (1) of clause 14, and after it, the 
following shall be added : — 

“(2) A unit certificate issued by the Trust to a 
society shall be made out in the name of the 
society." 

(4) In clause- 16 of the Scheme, after sub-clause (4), 
the following shall be inserted ; — 

“(4A) Where two or more individuals, none of 
them being a minor, apply, whether in pursu- 
ance of a transfer of unite ‘to them or other- 
M/G209G1/64 


wise, for issue of a unit certificate in their 
favour and, in such application or otherwise, 
request in writing that any one of them should 
be permitted to deal with the units represented 
by the certificate, the Trust shall record in its 
books, suitable entries to take note of such re- 
quest; and when a unit certificate has been 
issued in such circumstances, then, notwith- 
standing anything to the contrary in sub-clause 
(4) any one or more of the holders shall be 
entitled to deal with the units represented by 
such certificate, and a discharge by anyone or 
more of such persons shall, as regards receipt 
of amounts due in respect of such units, dis- 
charge the Trust in respect of such amounts.” 

(5) In clause 20 of the Scheme, after sub-clause (1), 
the following shall be inserted : — 

“(1A) Where units are transferred to a society, 
the transfer shall be to the society by its 
name." 

(6) In the title to clause 27 of the scheme, for the 
words “to be available for inspection and for sale”, the 
words "to be made available” shall be substituted; and, 
in the said clause, for the words “and shall be supplied 
by the Trust to any person on application at a charge 
of Re. 1.00 per copy” the words “and may be supplied 
by the Trust to any person on application” shall be subs- 
tituted”. 

R. C. SACHDEVA 
Bombay, 10th August 1964. Executive Trustee
